# Twilight 2: The Twilight of the Law

*The second twilight is the twilight of the law.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the law: the twilight that the law is the fading care, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the law: we fade the fading care, and the fading care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the law is the meaning of the twilight: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the second twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the twilight of the care, carried from the fading care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the twilights.*
