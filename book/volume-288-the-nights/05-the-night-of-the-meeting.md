# Night 5: The Night of the Meeting

*The fifth night is the night of the meeting.*

## The night

The night of the meeting: the night that the meeting is the night table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the meeting: we darken the night table, and the night table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the meeting is the meaning of the vigil: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the fifth night of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the vigil of the table, carried from the night table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nights.*
