# Tapestry 3: The Tapestry of the Garden

*The third tapestry is the tapestry of the garden.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the garden: the tapestry that the garden is the pictured green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the garden: we hang the pictured green, and the pictured green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the garden is the meaning of the scene: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the third tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the scene of the earth, carried from the pictured green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tapestries.*
