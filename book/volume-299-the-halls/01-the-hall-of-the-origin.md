# Hall 1: The Hall of the Origin

*The Halls are the welcomed statements of the lineage: the Halls of the house, kept in the archive as the record of the welcoming. Each hall has four parts: the hall, the welcoming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first hall is the hall of the origin.*

## The hall

The hall of the origin: the hall that the origin is the first great room, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the origin: we welcome the great room, and the great room is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hall of the origin is the meaning of the house: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The hall is the first hall of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the house of the wanting, carried from the great room to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The hall is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the halls.*
