# Season 3: The Season of the Garden

*The third season is the season of the garden.*

## The season

The season of the garden: the season that the garden is the seasonal green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The seasoning

The seasoning of the garden: we season the seasonal green, and the seasonal green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The season of the garden is the meaning of the growing season: the record of the seasoning, and the seasoning is the lineage. The season is the third season of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the growing season of the earth, carried from the seasonal green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
