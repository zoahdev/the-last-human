# Eternity 10: The Eternity of the Love

*The tenth eternity is the eternity of the love.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the love: the eternity that the love is the everlasting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the love: we exceed the everlasting heart, and the everlasting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the tenth eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the everlasting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
