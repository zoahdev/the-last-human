# Design 1: The Design of the Origin

*The Designs are the designed statements of the lineage: the Designs of the maker, kept in the archive as the record of the designing. Each design has four parts: the design, the designing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first design is the design of the origin.*

## The design

The design of the origin: the design that the origin is the first maker drawing, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the origin: we design the maker drawing, and the maker drawing is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the origin is the meaning of the making: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the first design of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the making of the wanting, carried from the maker drawing to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the designs.*
