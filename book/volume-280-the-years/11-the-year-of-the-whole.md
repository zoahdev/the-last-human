# Year 11: The Year of the Whole

*The eleventh year is the year of the whole.*

## The year

The year of the whole: the year that the whole is the one year of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the whole: we count the one year, and the one year is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the eleventh year of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one year to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the years.*
