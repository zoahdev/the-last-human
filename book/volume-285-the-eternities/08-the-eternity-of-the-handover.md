# Eternity 8: The Eternity of the Handover

*The eighth eternity is the eternity of the handover.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the handover: the eternity that the handover is the exceeding hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the handover: we exceed the exceeding hand, and the exceeding hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the handover is the meaning of the beyond: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the eighth eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the beyond of the hand, carried from the exceeding hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
