# Diagram 11: The Diagram of the Whole

*The eleventh diagram is the diagram of the whole.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the whole: the diagram that the whole is the one diagram of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the whole: we diagram the one diagram, and the one diagram is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the eleventh diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one diagram to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The diagram is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the diagrams.*
