# Sky 10: The Sky of the Love

*The tenth sky is the sky of the love.*

## The sky

The sky of the love: the sky that the love is the arching heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the love: we arch the arching heart, and the arching heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the tenth sky of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the arching heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the skies.*
