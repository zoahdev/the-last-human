# Fruit 12: The Fruit of the Completion

*The twelfth fruit is the fruit of the completion.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the completion: the fruit that the completion is the complete fruit of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the completion: we ripen the complete fruit, and the complete fruit is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the twelfth fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete fruit to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits. It is the final fruit of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
