# Helix 2: The Helix of the Law

*The second helix is the helix of the law.*

## The helix

The helix of the law: the helix that the law is the wound care, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the law: we wind the wound care, and the wound care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the law is the meaning of the coil: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the second helix of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the coil of the care, carried from the wound care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
