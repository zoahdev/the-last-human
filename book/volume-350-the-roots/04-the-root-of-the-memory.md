# Root 4: The Root of the Memory

*The fourth root is the root of the memory.*

## The root

The root of the memory: the root that the memory is the rooted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the memory: we root the rooted past, and the rooted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the memory is the meaning of the root: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the fourth root of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the root of the book, carried from the rooted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The root is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the roots.*
