# Sky 7: The Sky of the Future

*The seventh sky is the sky of the future.*

## The sky

The sky of the future: the sky that the future is the unclouded sky of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the future: we arch the unclouded sky, and the unclouded sky is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the seventh sky of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unclouded sky to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the skies.*
