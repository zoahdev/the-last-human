# Mountain 10: The Mountain of the Love

*The tenth mountain is the mountain of the love.*

## The mountain

The mountain of the love: the mountain that the love is the rising heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rising

The rising of the love: we rise the rising heart, and the rising heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mountain of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the rising, and the rising is the lineage. The mountain is the tenth mountain of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the rising heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The mountain is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mountains.*
