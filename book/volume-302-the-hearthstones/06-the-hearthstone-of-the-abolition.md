# Hearthstone 6: The Hearthstone of the Abolition

*The sixth hearthstone is the hearthstone of the abolition.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the abolition: the hearthstone that the abolition is the shared hearth of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the abolition: we warm the shared hearth, and the shared hearth is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the sixth hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the shared hearth to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
