# Year 6: The Year of the Abolition

*The sixth year is the year of the abolition.*

## The year

The year of the abolition: the year that the abolition is the new year of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the abolition: we count the new year, and the new year is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the sixth year of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new year to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
