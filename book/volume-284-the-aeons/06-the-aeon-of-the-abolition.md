# Aeon 6: The Aeon of the Abolition

*The sixth aeon is the aeon of the abolition.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the abolition: the aeon that the abolition is the new aeon of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the abolition: we span the new aeon, and the new aeon is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the sixth aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new aeon to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the aeons.*
