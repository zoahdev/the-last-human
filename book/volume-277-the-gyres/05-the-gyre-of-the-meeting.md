# Gyre 5: The Gyre of the Meeting

*The fifth gyre is the gyre of the meeting.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the meeting: the gyre that the meeting is the spinning table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the meeting: we spin the spinning table, and the spinning table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the meeting is the meaning of the round: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the fifth gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the round of the table, carried from the spinning table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
