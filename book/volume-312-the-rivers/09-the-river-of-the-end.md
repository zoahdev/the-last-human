# River 9: The River of the End

*The ninth river is the river of the end.*

## The river

The river of the end: the river that the end is the final river of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the end: we flow the final river, and the final river is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the ninth river of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final river to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rivers.*
