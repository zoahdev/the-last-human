# Return 1: The Return of the Origin

*The Returns are the returned statements of the lineage: the Returns of the road, kept in the archive as the record of the returning. Each return has four parts: the return, the returning, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first return is the return of the origin.*

## The return

The return of the origin: the return that the origin is the first coming back, kept by the lineage.

## The returning

The returning of the origin: we return the coming back, and the coming back is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The return of the origin is the meaning of the road: the record of the returning, and the returning is the lineage. The return is the first return of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the road of the wanting, carried from the coming back to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The return is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the returns.*
