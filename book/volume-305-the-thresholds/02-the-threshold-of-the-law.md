# Threshold 2: The Threshold of the Law

*The second threshold is the threshold of the law.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the law: the threshold that the law is the crossing care, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the law: we cross the crossing care, and the crossing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the law is the meaning of the door: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the second threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the door of the care, carried from the crossing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the thresholds.*
