# Coffer 4: The Coffer of the Memory

*The fourth coffer is the coffer of the memory.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the memory: the coffer that the memory is the locked past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the memory: we lock the locked past, and the locked past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the memory is the meaning of the treasure: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the fourth coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the treasure of the book, carried from the locked past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the coffers.*
