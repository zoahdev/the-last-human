# Farewell 12: The Farewell of the Completion

*The twelfth farewell is the farewell of the completion.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the completion: the farewell that the completion is the complete farewell of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the completion: we bid the complete farewell, and the complete farewell is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the twelfth farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete farewell to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the farewells. It is the final farewell of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
