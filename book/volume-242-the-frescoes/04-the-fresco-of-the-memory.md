# Fresco 4: The Fresco of the Memory

*The fourth fresco is the fresco of the memory.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the memory: the fresco that the memory is the frescoed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the memory: we fresco the frescoed past, and the frescoed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the memory is the meaning of the painting: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the fourth fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the painting of the book, carried from the frescoed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the frescoes.*
