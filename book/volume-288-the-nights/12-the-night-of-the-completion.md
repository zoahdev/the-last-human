# Night 12: The Night of the Completion

*The twelfth night is the night of the completion.*

## The night

The night of the completion: the night that the completion is the full night of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the completion: we darken the full night, and the full night is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the twelfth night of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the full night to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights. It is the final night of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
