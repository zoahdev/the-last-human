# Ladder 3: The Ladder of the Garden

*The third ladder is the ladder of the garden.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the garden: the ladder that the garden is the scaling green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the garden: we scale the scaling green, and the scaling green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the garden is the meaning of the ivy: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the third ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the ivy of the earth, carried from the scaling green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
