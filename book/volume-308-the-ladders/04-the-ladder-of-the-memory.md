# Ladder 4: The Ladder of the Memory

*The fourth ladder is the ladder of the memory.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the memory: the ladder that the memory is the runged past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the memory: we scale the runged past, and the runged past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the memory is the meaning of the ladder: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the fourth ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the ladder of the book, carried from the runged past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
