# Oasis 8: The Oasis of the Handover

*The eighth oasis is the oasis of the handover.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the handover: the oasis that the handover is the watering hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the handover: we water the watering hand, and the watering hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the handover is the meaning of the oasis: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the eighth oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the oasis of the hand, carried from the watering hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the oases.*
