# Winter 2: The Winter of the Law

*The second winter is the winter of the law.*

## The winter

The winter of the law: the winter that the law is the frozen care, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the law: we freeze the frozen care, and the frozen care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the law is the meaning of the ice: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the second winter of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the ice of the care, carried from the frozen care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The winter is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the winters.*
