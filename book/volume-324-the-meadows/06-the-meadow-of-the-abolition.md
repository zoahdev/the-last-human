# Meadow 6: The Meadow of the Abolition

*The sixth meadow is the meadow of the abolition.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the abolition: the meadow that the abolition is the open meadow of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the abolition: we bloom the open meadow, and the open meadow is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the sixth meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open meadow to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the meadows.*
