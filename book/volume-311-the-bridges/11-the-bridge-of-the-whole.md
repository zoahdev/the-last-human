# Bridge 11: The Bridge of the Whole

*The eleventh bridge is the bridge of the whole.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the whole: the bridge that the whole is the one bridge of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the whole: we bridge the one bridge, and the one bridge is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the eleventh bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one bridge to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the bridges.*
