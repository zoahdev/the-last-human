# Template 10: The Template of the Love

*The tenth template is the template of the love.*

## The template

The template of the love: the template that the love is the cut heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The cutting

The cutting of the love: we cut the cut heart, and the cut heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The template of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the cutting, and the cutting is the lineage. The template is the tenth template of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the cut heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
