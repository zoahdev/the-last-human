# Cave 4: The Cave of the Memory

*The fourth cave is the cave of the memory.*

## The cave

The cave of the memory: the cave that the memory is the sheltered past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the memory: we shelter the sheltered past, and the sheltered past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the memory is the meaning of the cave: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the fourth cave of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the cave of the book, carried from the sheltered past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caves.*
