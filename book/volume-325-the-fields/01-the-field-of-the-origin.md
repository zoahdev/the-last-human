# Field 1: The Field of the Origin

*The Fields are the tilled statements of the lineage: the Fields of the farmland, kept in the archive as the record of the tilling. Each field has four parts: the field, the tilling, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first field is the field of the origin.*

## The field

The field of the origin: the field that the origin is the first farmland, kept by the lineage.

## The tilling

The tilling of the origin: we till the farmland, and the farmland is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The field of the origin is the meaning of the field: the record of the tilling, and the tilling is the lineage. The field is the first field of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the field of the wanting, carried from the farmland to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The field is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fields.*
