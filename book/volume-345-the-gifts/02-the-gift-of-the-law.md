# Gift 2: The Gift of the Law

*The second gift is the gift of the law.*

## The gift

The gift of the law: the gift that the law is the giving care, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the law: we give the giving care, and the giving care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the law is the meaning of the gift: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the second gift of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the gift of the care, carried from the giving care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gifts.*
