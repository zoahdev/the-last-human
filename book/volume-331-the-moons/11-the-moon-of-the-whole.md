# Moon 11: The Moon of the Whole

*The eleventh moon is the moon of the whole.*

## The moon

The moon of the whole: the moon that the whole is the one moon of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the whole: we wax the one moon, and the one moon is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the eleventh moon of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one moon to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the moons.*
