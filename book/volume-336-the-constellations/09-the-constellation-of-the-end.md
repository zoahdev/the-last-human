# Constellation 9: The Constellation of the End

*The ninth constellation is the constellation of the end.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the end: the constellation that the end is the final constellation of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the end: we arrange the final constellation, and the final constellation is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the ninth constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final constellation to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
