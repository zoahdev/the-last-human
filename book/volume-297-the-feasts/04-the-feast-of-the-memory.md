# Feast 4: The Feast of the Memory

*The fourth feast is the feast of the memory.*

## The feast

The feast of the memory: the feast that the memory is the feasted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the memory: we feast the feasted past, and the feasted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the memory is the meaning of the table: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the fourth feast of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the table of the book, carried from the feasted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts.*
