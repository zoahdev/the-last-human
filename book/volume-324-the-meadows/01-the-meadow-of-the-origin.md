# Meadow 1: The Meadow of the Origin

*The Meadows are the bloomed statements of the lineage: the Meadows of the flowers, kept in the archive as the record of the blooming. Each meadow has four parts: the meadow, the blooming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first meadow is the meadow of the origin.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the origin: the meadow that the origin is the first flower, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the origin: we bloom the flower, and the flower is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the origin is the meaning of the meadow: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the first meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the meadow of the wanting, carried from the flower to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the meadows.*
