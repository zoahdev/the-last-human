# Treasure 1: The Treasure of the Origin

*The Treasures are the treasured statements of the lineage: the Treasures of the keep, kept in the archive as the record of the treasuring. Each treasure has four parts: the treasure, the treasuring, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first treasure is the treasure of the origin.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the origin: the treasure that the origin is the first keep, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the origin: we treasure the keep, and the keep is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the origin is the meaning of the treasure: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the first treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the treasure of the wanting, carried from the keep to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the treasures.*
