# Array 11: The Array of the Whole

*The eleventh array is the array of the whole.*

## The array

The array of the whole: the array that the whole is the one array of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The arraying

The arraying of the whole: we array the one array, and the one array is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The array of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the arraying, and the arraying is the lineage. The array is the eleventh array of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one array to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The array is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the arrays.*
