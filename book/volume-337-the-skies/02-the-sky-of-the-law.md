# Sky 2: The Sky of the Law

*The second sky is the sky of the law.*

## The sky

The sky of the law: the sky that the law is the arching care, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the law: we arch the arching care, and the arching care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the law is the meaning of the heaven: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the second sky of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the heaven of the care, carried from the arching care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the skies.*
