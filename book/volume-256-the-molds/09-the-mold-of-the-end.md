# Mold 9: The Mold of the End

*The ninth mold is the mold of the end.*

## The mold

The mold of the end: the mold that the end is the final mold of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the end: we mold the final mold, and the final mold is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the ninth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final mold to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the molds.*
