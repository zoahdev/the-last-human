# Eternity 1: The Eternity of the Origin

*The Eternities are the exceeded statements of the lineage: the Eternities of the measure, kept in the archive as the record of the exceeding. Each eternity has four parts: the eternity, the exceeding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first eternity is the eternity of the origin.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the origin: the eternity that the origin is the first beyond, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the origin: we exceed the beyond, and the beyond is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the origin is the meaning of the measure: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the first eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the measure of the wanting, carried from the beyond to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
