# Cave 10: The Cave of the Love

*The tenth cave is the cave of the love.*

## The cave

The cave of the love: the cave that the love is the sheltering heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the love: we shelter the sheltering heart, and the sheltering heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the tenth cave of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the sheltering heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caves.*
