# Map 6: The Map of the Abolition

*The sixth map is the map of the abolition.*

## The map

The map of the abolition: the map that the abolition is the unmapped freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the abolition: we map the unmapped freedom, and the unmapped freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the sixth map of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the unmapped freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
