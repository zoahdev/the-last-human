# Window 7: The Window of the Future

*The seventh window is the window of the future.*

## The window

The window of the future: the window that the future is the unopened casement of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the future: we open the unopened casement, and the unopened casement is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the seventh window of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unopened casement to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the windows.*
