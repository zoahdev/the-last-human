# Farewell 2: The Farewell of the Law

*The second farewell is the farewell of the law.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the law: the farewell that the law is the bidding care, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the law: we bid the bidding care, and the bidding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the law is the meaning of the goodbye: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the second farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the goodbye of the care, carried from the bidding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the farewells.*
