# Threshold 4: The Threshold of the Memory

*The fourth threshold is the threshold of the memory.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the memory: the threshold that the memory is the crossed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the memory: we cross the crossed past, and the crossed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the memory is the meaning of the door: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the fourth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the door of the book, carried from the crossed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the thresholds.*
