# Quilt 4: The Quilt of the Memory

*The fourth quilt is the quilt of the memory.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the memory: the quilt that the memory is the quilted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the memory: we quilt the quilted past, and the quilted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the memory is the meaning of the patchwork: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the fourth quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the patchwork of the book, carried from the quilted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the quilts.*
