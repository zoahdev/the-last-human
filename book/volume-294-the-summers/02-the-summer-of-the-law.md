# Summer 2: The Summer of the Law

*The second summer is the summer of the law.*

## The summer

The summer of the law: the summer that the law is the ripened care, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the law: we ripen the ripened care, and the ripened care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the law is the meaning of the sun: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the second summer of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the sun of the care, carried from the ripened care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
