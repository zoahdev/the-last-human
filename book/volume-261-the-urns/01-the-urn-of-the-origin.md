# Urn 1: The Urn of the Origin

*The Urns are the kept statements of the lineage: the Urns of the remains, kept in the archive as the record of the keeping. Each urn has four parts: the urn, the keeping, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first urn is the urn of the origin.*

## The urn

The urn of the origin: the urn that the origin is the first keeper of the ash, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the origin: we keep the ash, and the ash is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the origin is the meaning of the ashes: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the first urn of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the ashes of the wanting, carried from the ash to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the urns.*
