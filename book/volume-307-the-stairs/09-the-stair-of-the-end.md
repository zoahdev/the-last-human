# Stair 9: The Stair of the End

*The ninth stair is the stair of the end.*

## The stair

The stair of the end: the stair that the end is the final stair of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the end: we wind the final stair, and the final stair is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the ninth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final stair to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs.*
