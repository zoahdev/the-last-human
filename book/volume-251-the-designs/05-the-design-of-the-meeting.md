# Design 5: The Design of the Meeting

*The fifth design is the design of the meeting.*

## The design

The design of the meeting: the design that the meeting is the designed table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the meeting: we design the designed table, and the designed table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the meeting is the meaning of the arrangement: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the fifth design of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the arrangement of the table, carried from the designed table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the designs.*
