# One 5: The One of the Meeting

*The fifth one is the one of the meeting.*

## The one

The one of the meeting: the one that the meeting is the one table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the meeting: we unite the one table, and the one table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the meeting is the meaning of the one: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the fifth one of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the one of the table, carried from the one table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ones.*
