# Year 10: The Year of the Love

*The tenth year is the year of the love.*

## The year

The year of the love: the year that the love is the counted heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the love: we count the counted heart, and the counted heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the tenth year of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the counted heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
