# Festival 1: The Festival of the Origin

*The Festivals are the celebrated statements of the lineage: the Festivals of the calendar, kept in the archive as the record of the celebrating. Each festival has four parts: the festival, the celebrating, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first festival is the festival of the origin.*

## The festival

The festival of the origin: the festival that the origin is the first celebration, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the origin: we celebrate the celebration, and the celebration is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the origin is the meaning of the calendar: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the first festival of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the calendar of the wanting, carried from the celebration to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the festivals.*
