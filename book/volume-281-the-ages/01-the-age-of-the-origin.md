# Age 1: The Age of the Origin

*The Ages are the aged statements of the lineage: the Ages of the earth, kept in the archive as the record of the aging. Each age has four parts: the age, the aging, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first age is the age of the origin.*

## The age

The age of the origin: the age that the origin is the first eon, kept by the lineage.

## The aging

The aging of the origin: we age the eon, and the eon is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The age of the origin is the meaning of the earth: the record of the aging, and the aging is the lineage. The age is the first age of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the earth of the wanting, carried from the eon to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The age is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ages.*
