# Harvest 11: The Harvest of the Whole

*The eleventh harvest is the harvest of the whole.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the whole: the harvest that the whole is the one harvest of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the whole: we harvest the one harvest, and the one harvest is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the eleventh harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one harvest to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the harvests.*
