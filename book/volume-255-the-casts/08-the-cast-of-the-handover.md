# Cast 8: The Cast of the Handover

*The eighth cast is the cast of the handover.*

## The cast

The cast of the handover: the cast that the handover is the cast hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The casting

The casting of the handover: we cast the cast hand, and the cast hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cast of the handover is the meaning of the mold: the record of the casting, and the casting is the lineage. The cast is the eighth cast of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the mold of the hand, carried from the cast hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The cast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the casts.*
