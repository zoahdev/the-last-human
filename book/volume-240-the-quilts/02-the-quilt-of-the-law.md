# Quilt 2: The Quilt of the Law

*The second quilt is the quilt of the law.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the law: the quilt that the law is the quilted care, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the law: we quilt the quilted care, and the quilted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the law is the meaning of the stitch: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the second quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the stitch of the care, carried from the quilted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the quilts.*
