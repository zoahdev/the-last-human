# Well 11: The Well of the Whole

*The eleventh well is the well of the whole.*

## The well

The well of the whole: the well that the whole is the one well of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the whole: we draw the one well, and the one well is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the eleventh well of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one well to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
