# Vessel 3: The Vessel of the Garden

*The third vessel is the vessel of the garden.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the garden: the vessel that the garden is the green vessel of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the garden: we hold the green vessel, and the green vessel is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the garden is the meaning of the planter: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the third vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the planter of the earth, carried from the green vessel to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the vessels.*
