# Aeon 9: The Aeon of the End

*The ninth aeon is the aeon of the end.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the end: the aeon that the end is the final aeon of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the end: we span the final aeon, and the final aeon is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the ninth aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final aeon to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the aeons.*
