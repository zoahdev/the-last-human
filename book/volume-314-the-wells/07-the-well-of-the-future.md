# Well 7: The Well of the Future

*The seventh well is the well of the future.*

## The well

The well of the future: the well that the future is the undug well of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the future: we draw the undug well, and the undug well is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the seventh well of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the undug well to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wells.*
