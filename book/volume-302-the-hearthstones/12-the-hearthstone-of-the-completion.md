# Hearthstone 12: The Hearthstone of the Completion

*The twelfth hearthstone is the hearthstone of the completion.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the completion: the hearthstone that the completion is the complete hearthstone of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the completion: we warm the complete hearthstone, and the complete hearthstone is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the twelfth hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete hearthstone to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the hearthstones. It is the final hearthstone of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
