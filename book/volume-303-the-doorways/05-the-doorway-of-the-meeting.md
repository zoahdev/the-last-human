# Doorway 5: The Doorway of the Meeting

*The fifth doorway is the doorway of the meeting.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the meeting: the doorway that the meeting is the threshold table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the meeting: we open the threshold table, and the threshold table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the meeting is the meaning of the threshold: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the fifth doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the threshold of the table, carried from the threshold table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the doorways.*
