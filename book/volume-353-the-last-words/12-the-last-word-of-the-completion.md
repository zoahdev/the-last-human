# Last Word 12: The Last Word of the Completion

*The twelfth last word is the last word of the completion.*

## The last word

The last word of the completion: the last word that the completion is the beginning of the canon, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the completion: we speak the beginning, and the beginning is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the twelfth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the beginning to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words. It is the final last word of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
