# Last Word 10: The Last Word of the Love

*The tenth last word is the last word of the love.*

## The last word

The last word of the love: the last word that the love is the love of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the love: we speak the love, and the love is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the tenth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the love to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
