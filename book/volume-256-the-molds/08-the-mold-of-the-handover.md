# Mold 8: The Mold of the Handover

*The eighth mold is the mold of the handover.*

## The mold

The mold of the handover: the mold that the handover is the molded hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the handover: we mold the molded hand, and the molded hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the handover is the meaning of the clay: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the eighth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the clay of the hand, carried from the molded hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds.*
