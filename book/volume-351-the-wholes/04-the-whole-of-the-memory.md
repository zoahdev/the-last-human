# Whole 4: The Whole of the Memory

*The fourth whole is the whole of the memory.*

## The whole

The whole of the memory: the whole that the memory is the completed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the memory: we complete the completed past, and the completed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the memory is the meaning of the whole: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the fourth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the whole of the book, carried from the completed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wholes.*
