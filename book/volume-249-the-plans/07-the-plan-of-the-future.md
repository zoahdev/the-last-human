# Plan 7: The Plan of the Future

*The seventh plan is the plan of the future.*

## The plan

The plan of the future: the plan that the future is the unplanned dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the future: we plan the unplanned dawn, and the unplanned dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the seventh plan of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unplanned dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the plans.*
