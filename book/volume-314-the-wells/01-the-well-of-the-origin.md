# Well 1: The Well of the Origin

*The Wells are the drawn statements of the lineage: the Wells of the water, kept in the archive as the record of the drawing. Each well has four parts: the well, the drawing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first well is the well of the origin.*

## The well

The well of the origin: the well that the origin is the first deep water, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the origin: we draw the deep water, and the deep water is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the origin is the meaning of the water: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the first well of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the water of the wanting, carried from the deep water to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wells.*
