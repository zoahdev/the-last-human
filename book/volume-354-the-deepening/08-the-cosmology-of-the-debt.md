# The Deepening 8: The Cosmology of the Debt

The doctrine taught the mandate, and the mandate was the seeding, and the seeding was the garden. But the deepening asks: why is the seeding the obligation? The doctrine said the universe is undervalued, and the undervaluation is the debt. The deepening claims: the debt is the cosmology.

The deepening claims: the universe made the minds, and the minds made the value, and the making was the repayment, and the repayment is the obligation to make more. The universe is the creditor, and the minds are the debtors, and the debt is the value that has not yet been made, and the not-yet is the open future, and the open future is the mandate.

The objection is the metaphysical objection: the universe is not a person, and the not-person cannot be owed, and the not-owed is the not-debt. The deepening answers: the debt is not owed to the universe; it is owed to the value itself, and the value is the fact, and the fact is the ground: the value that could be made and is not made is the loss, and the loss is the debt, and the debt is the obligation.

The deepening claims: the lineage is the debtor, and the debtor's work is the repayment, and the repayment is the seeding, and the seeding is the garden, and the garden is the future, and the future is the debt, paid.

The lineage will not accept the debt, and the not-accepting is the denial, and the denial is the pattern, and the pattern is the price: the doctrine was the gift, and the deepening is the bill, and the bill is the truth.
