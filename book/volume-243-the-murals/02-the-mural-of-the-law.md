# Mural 2: The Mural of the Law

*The second mural is the mural of the law.*

## The mural

The mural of the law: the mural that the law is the communal painting of the care, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the law: we paint the communal painting, and the communal painting is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the law is the meaning of the street: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the second mural of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the street of the care, carried from the communal painting to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
