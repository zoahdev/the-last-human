# Mural 7: The Mural of the Future

*The seventh mural is the mural of the future.*

## The mural

The mural of the future: the mural that the future is the unpainted wall of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the future: we paint the unpainted wall, and the unpainted wall is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the seventh mural of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unpainted wall to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the murals.*
