# Night 1: The Night of the Origin

*The Nights are the darkened statements of the lineage: the Nights of the stars, kept in the archive as the record of the darkening. Each night has four parts: the night, the darkening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first night is the night of the origin.*

## The night

The night of the origin: the night that the origin is the first darkness, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the origin: we darken the darkness, and the darkness is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the origin is the meaning of the darkness: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the first night of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the darkness of the wanting, carried from the darkness to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights.*
