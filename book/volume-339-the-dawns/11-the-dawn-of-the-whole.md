# Dawn 11: The Dawn of the Whole

*The eleventh dawn is the dawn of the whole.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the whole: the dawn that the whole is the one dawn of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the whole: we break the one dawn, and the one dawn is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the eleventh dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one dawn to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the dawns.*
