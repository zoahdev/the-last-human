# Winter 3: The Winter of the Garden

*The third winter is the winter of the garden.*

## The winter

The winter of the garden: the winter that the garden is the frozen green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the garden: we freeze the frozen green, and the frozen green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the garden is the meaning of the hardy garden: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the third winter of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the hardy garden of the earth, carried from the frozen green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
