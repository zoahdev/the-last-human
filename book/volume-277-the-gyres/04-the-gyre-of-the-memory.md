# Gyre 4: The Gyre of the Memory

*The fourth gyre is the gyre of the memory.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the memory: the gyre that the memory is the spun past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the memory: we spin the spun past, and the spun past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the memory is the meaning of the gyre: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the fourth gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the gyre of the book, carried from the spun past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
