# Diagram 6: The Diagram of the Abolition

*The sixth diagram is the diagram of the abolition.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the abolition: the diagram that the abolition is the redrawn freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the abolition: we diagram the redrawn freedom, and the redrawn freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the sixth diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the redrawn freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
