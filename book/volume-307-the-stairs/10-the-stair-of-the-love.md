# Stair 10: The Stair of the Love

*The tenth stair is the stair of the love.*

## The stair

The stair of the love: the stair that the love is the winding heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the love: we wind the winding heart, and the winding heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the tenth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the winding heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stairs.*
