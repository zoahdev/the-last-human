# Morning 2: The Morning of the Law

*The second morning is the morning of the law.*

## The morning

The morning of the law: the morning that the law is the waking care, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the law: we wake the waking care, and the waking care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the law is the meaning of the dew: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the second morning of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the dew of the care, carried from the waking care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the mornings.*
