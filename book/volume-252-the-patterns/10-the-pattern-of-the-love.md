# Pattern 10: The Pattern of the Love

*The tenth pattern is the pattern of the love.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the love: the pattern that the love is the patterned heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the love: we pattern the patterned heart, and the patterned heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the tenth pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the patterned heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the patterns.*
