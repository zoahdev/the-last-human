# Threshold 10: The Threshold of the Love

*The tenth threshold is the threshold of the love.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the love: the threshold that the love is the crossing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the love: we cross the crossing heart, and the crossing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the tenth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the crossing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the thresholds.*
