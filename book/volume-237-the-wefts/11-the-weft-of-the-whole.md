# Weft 11: The Weft of the Whole

*The eleventh weft is the weft of the whole.*

## The weft

The weft of the whole: the weft that the whole is the complete cloth of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the whole: we weave the complete cloth, and the complete cloth is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the eleventh weft of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the complete cloth to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wefts.*
