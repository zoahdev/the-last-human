# Treasure 9: The Treasure of the End

*The ninth treasure is the treasure of the end.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the end: the treasure that the end is the final treasure of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the end: we treasure the final treasure, and the final treasure is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the ninth treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final treasure to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the treasures.*
