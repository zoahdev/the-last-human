# Gyre 11: The Gyre of the Whole

*The eleventh gyre is the gyre of the whole.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the whole: the gyre that the whole is the one gyre of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the whole: we spin the one gyre, and the one gyre is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the eleventh gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one gyre to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the gyres.*
