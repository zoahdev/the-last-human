# Moon 2: The Moon of the Law

*The second moon is the moon of the law.*

## The moon

The moon of the law: the moon that the law is the waxing care, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the law: we wax the waxing care, and the waxing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the law is the meaning of the tide: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the second moon of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the tide of the care, carried from the waxing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the moons.*
