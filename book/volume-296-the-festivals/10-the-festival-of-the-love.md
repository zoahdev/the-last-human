# Festival 10: The Festival of the Love

*The tenth festival is the festival of the love.*

## The festival

The festival of the love: the festival that the love is the celebrating heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the love: we celebrate the celebrating heart, and the celebrating heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the tenth festival of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the celebrating heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the festivals.*
