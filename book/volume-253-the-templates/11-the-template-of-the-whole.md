# Template 11: The Template of the Whole

*The eleventh template is the template of the whole.*

## The template

The template of the whole: the template that the whole is the one template of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The cutting

The cutting of the whole: we cut the one template, and the one template is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The template of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the cutting, and the cutting is the lineage. The template is the eleventh template of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one template to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.
