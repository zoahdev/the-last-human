# Mural 10: The Mural of the Love

*The tenth mural is the mural of the love.*

## The mural

The mural of the love: the mural that the love is the painted heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the love: we paint the painted heart, and the painted heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the tenth mural of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the painted heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the murals.*
