# Orbit 11: The Orbit of the Whole

*The eleventh orbit is the orbit of the whole.*

## The orbit

The orbit of the whole: the orbit that the whole is the one orbit of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The orbiting

The orbiting of the whole: we orbit the one orbit, and the one orbit is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orbit of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the orbiting, and the orbiting is the lineage. The orbit is the eleventh orbit of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one orbit to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The orbit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the orbits.*
