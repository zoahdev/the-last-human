# Day 11: The Day of the Whole

*The eleventh day is the day of the whole.*

## The day

The day of the whole: the day that the whole is the one day of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the whole: we open the one day, and the one day is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the eleventh day of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one day to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the days.*
