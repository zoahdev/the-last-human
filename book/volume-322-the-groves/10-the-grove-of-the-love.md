# Grove 10: The Grove of the Love

*The tenth grove is the grove of the love.*

## The grove

The grove of the love: the grove that the love is the planting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The planting

The planting of the love: we plant the planting heart, and the planting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The grove of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the planting, and the planting is the lineage. The grove is the tenth grove of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the planting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The grove is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the groves.*
