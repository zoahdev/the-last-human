# Cup 3: The Cup of the Garden

*The third cup is the cup of the garden.*

## The cup

The cup of the garden: the cup that the garden is the cupped green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the garden: we cup the cupped green, and the cupped green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the garden is the meaning of the bowl: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the third cup of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the bowl of the earth, carried from the cupped green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
