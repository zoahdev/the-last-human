# Star 7: The Star of the Future

*The seventh star is the star of the future.*

## The star

The star of the future: the star that the future is the unlit star of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The shining

The shining of the future: we shine the unlit star, and the unlit star is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The star of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the shining, and the shining is the lineage. The star is the seventh star of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unlit star to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The star is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stars.*
