# Step 7: The Step of the Future

*The seventh step is the step of the future.*

## The step

The step of the future: the step that the future is the unstepped dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the future: we step the unstepped dawn, and the unstepped dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the seventh step of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unstepped dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the steps.*
