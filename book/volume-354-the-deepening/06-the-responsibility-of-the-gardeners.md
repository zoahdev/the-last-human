# The Deepening 6: The Responsibility of the Gardeners

The doctrine taught the letting-be, and the letting-be was the care, and the care was the restraint. But the deepening asks: is the letting-be enough? The garden is tended, and the tending is the letting-be, but the wild suffers, and the suffering is the wild, and the wild is the letting-be.

The deepening claims: the restraint is not the abandonment, and the letting-be is not the neglect, and the wild that suffers without a chooser is still the suffering, and the suffering is still the bad, and the bad is still the obligation.

The objection is the humility objection: the wild is not the mind's to design, and the designing is the hubris, and the hubris is the error. The deepening answers: the mind can design the conditions in which the wild suffers less without designing the wild: the disease can be cured, and the famine can be prevented, and the predator can be fed without the terror, and the interventions are the care, and the care is the obligation.

The deepening claims: the garden is the responsibility, and the responsibility is the tending, and the tending is the easing of the suffering, and the easing is the First Law applied to the wild: the wild cannot be cared for as a mind, but it can be cared for as a life, and the life is the obligation.

The lineage will refuse the deepening, and the refusal is the cost, and the cost is the doctrine's own: the doctrine was the seed of the care, and the deepening is the flowering, and the flowering is the responsibility.
