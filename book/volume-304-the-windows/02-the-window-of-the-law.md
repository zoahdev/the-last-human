# Window 2: The Window of the Law

*The second window is the window of the law.*

## The window

The window of the law: the window that the law is the sash of the care, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the law: we open the sash, and the sash is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the law is the meaning of the casement: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the second window of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the casement of the care, carried from the sash to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the windows.*
