# Chest 2: The Chest of the Law

*The second chest is the chest of the law.*

## The chest

The chest of the law: the chest that the law is the stored care, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the law: we store the stored care, and the stored care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the law is the meaning of the strongbox: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the second chest of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the strongbox of the care, carried from the stored care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the chests.*
