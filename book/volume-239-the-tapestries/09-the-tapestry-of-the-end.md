# Tapestry 9: The Tapestry of the End

*The ninth tapestry is the tapestry of the end.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the end: the tapestry that the end is the final tapestry of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the end: we hang the final tapestry, and the final tapestry is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the ninth tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final tapestry to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tapestries.*
