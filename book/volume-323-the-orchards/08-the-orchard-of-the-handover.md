# Orchard 8: The Orchard of the Handover

*The eighth orchard is the orchard of the handover.*

## The orchard

The orchard of the handover: the orchard that the handover is the fruiting hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The fruiting

The fruiting of the handover: we fruit the fruiting hand, and the fruiting hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orchard of the handover is the meaning of the orchard: the record of the fruiting, and the fruiting is the lineage. The orchard is the eighth orchard of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the orchard of the hand, carried from the fruiting hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The orchard is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the orchards.*
