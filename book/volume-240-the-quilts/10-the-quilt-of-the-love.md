# Quilt 10: The Quilt of the Love

*The tenth quilt is the quilt of the love.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the love: the quilt that the love is the quilted heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the love: we quilt the quilted heart, and the quilted heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the tenth quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the quilted heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the quilts.*
