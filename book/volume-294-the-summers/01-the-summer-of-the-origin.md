# Summer 1: The Summer of the Origin

*The Summers are the ripened statements of the lineage: the Summers of the sun, kept in the archive as the record of the ripening. Each summer has four parts: the summer, the ripening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first summer is the summer of the origin.*

## The summer

The summer of the origin: the summer that the origin is the first long day, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the origin: we ripen the long day, and the long day is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the origin is the meaning of the sun: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the first summer of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the sun of the wanting, carried from the long day to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
