# Coffer 10: The Coffer of the Love

*The tenth coffer is the coffer of the love.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the love: the coffer that the love is the locked heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the love: we lock the locked heart, and the locked heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the tenth coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the locked heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the coffers.*
