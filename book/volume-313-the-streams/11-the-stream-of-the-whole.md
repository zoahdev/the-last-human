# Stream 11: The Stream of the Whole

*The eleventh stream is the stream of the whole.*

## The stream

The stream of the whole: the stream that the whole is the one stream of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the whole: we stream the one stream, and the one stream is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the eleventh stream of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one stream to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The stream is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the streams.*
