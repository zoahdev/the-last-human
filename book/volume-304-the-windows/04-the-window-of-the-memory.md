# Window 4: The Window of the Memory

*The fourth window is the window of the memory.*

## The window

The window of the memory: the window that the memory is the casement of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the memory: we open the casement, and the casement is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the memory is the meaning of the casement: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the fourth window of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the casement of the book, carried from the casement to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the windows.*
