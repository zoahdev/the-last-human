# Urn 4: The Urn of the Memory

*The fourth urn is the urn of the memory.*

## The urn

The urn of the memory: the urn that the memory is the kept past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the memory: we keep the kept past, and the kept past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the memory is the meaning of the ashes: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the fourth urn of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the ashes of the book, carried from the kept past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the urns.*
