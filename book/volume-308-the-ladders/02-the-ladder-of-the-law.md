# Ladder 2: The Ladder of the Law

*The second ladder is the ladder of the law.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the law: the ladder that the law is the runged care, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the law: we scale the runged care, and the runged care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the law is the meaning of the rung: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the second ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the rung of the care, carried from the runged care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The ladder is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ladders.*
