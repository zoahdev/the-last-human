# Last Word 2: The Last Word of the Law

*The second last word is the last word of the law.*

## The last word

The last word of the law: the last word that the law is the final care, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the law: we speak the final care, and the final care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the law is the meaning of the law: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the second last word of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the law of the care, carried from the final care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
