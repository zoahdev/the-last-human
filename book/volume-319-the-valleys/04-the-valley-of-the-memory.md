# Valley 4: The Valley of the Memory

*The fourth valley is the valley of the memory.*

## The valley

The valley of the memory: the valley that the memory is the hollowed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The hollowing

The hollowing of the memory: we hollow the hollowed past, and the hollowed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The valley of the memory is the meaning of the valley: the record of the hollowing, and the hollowing is the lineage. The valley is the fourth valley of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the valley of the book, carried from the hollowed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The valley is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the valleys.*
