# Summer 9: The Summer of the End

*The ninth summer is the summer of the end.*

## The summer

The summer of the end: the summer that the end is the final summer of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the end: we ripen the final summer, and the final summer is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the ninth summer of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final summer to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
