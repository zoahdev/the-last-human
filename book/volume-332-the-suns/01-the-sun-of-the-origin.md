# Sun 1: The Sun of the Origin

*The Suns are the burned statements of the lineage: the Suns of the day, kept in the archive as the record of the burning. Each sun has four parts: the sun, the burning, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first sun is the sun of the origin.*

## The sun

The sun of the origin: the sun that the origin is the first burning light, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the origin: we burn the burning light, and the burning light is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the origin is the meaning of the day: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the first sun of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the day of the wanting, carried from the burning light to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the suns.*
