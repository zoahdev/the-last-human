# Table 10: The Table of the Love

*The tenth table is the table of the love.*

## The table

The table of the love: the table that the love is the shared heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sharing

The sharing of the love: we share the shared heart, and the shared heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The table of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the sharing, and the sharing is the lineage. The table is the tenth table of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the shared heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The table is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tables.*
