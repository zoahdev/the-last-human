# Cup 11: The Cup of the Whole

*The eleventh cup is the cup of the whole.*

## The cup

The cup of the whole: the cup that the whole is the one cup of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the whole: we cup the one cup, and the one cup is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the eleventh cup of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one cup to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
