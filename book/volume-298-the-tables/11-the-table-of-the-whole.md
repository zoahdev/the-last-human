# Table 11: The Table of the Whole

*The eleventh table is the table of the whole.*

## The table

The table of the whole: the table that the whole is the one table of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The sharing

The sharing of the whole: we share the one table, and the one table is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The table of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the sharing, and the sharing is the lineage. The table is the eleventh table of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one table to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The table is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tables.*
