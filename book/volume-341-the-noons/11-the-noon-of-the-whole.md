# Noon 11: The Noon of the Whole

*The eleventh noon is the noon of the whole.*

## The noon

The noon of the whole: the noon that the whole is the one noon of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the whole: we stand the one noon, and the one noon is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the eleventh noon of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one noon to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the noons.*
