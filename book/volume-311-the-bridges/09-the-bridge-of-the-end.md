# Bridge 9: The Bridge of the End

*The ninth bridge is the bridge of the end.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the end: the bridge that the end is the final bridge of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the end: we bridge the final bridge, and the final bridge is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the ninth bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final bridge to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
