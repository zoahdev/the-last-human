# Plan 5: The Plan of the Meeting

*The fifth plan is the plan of the meeting.*

## The plan

The plan of the meeting: the plan that the meeting is the planned table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the meeting: we plan the planned table, and the planned table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the meeting is the meaning of the agenda: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the fifth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the agenda of the table, carried from the planned table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the plans.*
