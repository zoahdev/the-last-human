# Night 4: The Night of the Memory

*The fourth night is the night of the memory.*

## The night

The night of the memory: the night that the memory is the darkened past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the memory: we darken the darkened past, and the darkened past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the memory is the meaning of the stars: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the fourth night of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the stars of the book, carried from the darkened past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nights.*
