# Cabinet 4: The Cabinet of the Memory

*The fourth cabinet is the cabinet of the memory.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the memory: the cabinet that the memory is the arranged past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the memory: we arrange the arranged past, and the arranged past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the memory is the meaning of the curiosities: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the fourth cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the curiosities of the book, carried from the arranged past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The cabinet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the cabinets.*
