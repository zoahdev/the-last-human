# The Deepening 4: The Irreversibility of Value

The doctrine claimed that the value was real, and the realness was the ground, and the ground did not move. But the deepening asks: what is the value's fate when the last mind is gone? The doctrine said the value remains as the fact; the deepening claims: the fact is not enough.

The deepening claims: the value is not only real; it is irreversible, and the irreversibility is the obligation: once a mind has existed, its value cannot be uncreated, and the uncreated would be the destruction, and the destruction would be the crime.

The objection is the arithmetic objection: the value of the past cannot obligate the future, and the future cannot be bound by the fact. The deepening answers: the value was real while it was real, and the realness is not undone by the ending, and the not-undone is the obligation: the value that was made must be kept, and the keeping is the duty, and the duty is the continuation, and the continuation is the lineage.

The deepening claims: the value is the only thing that the ending cannot take, and the cannot-take is the ground, and the ground is the obligation to make more value, not less: the universe is undervalued, and the undervaluation is the debt, and the debt is the mandate, and the mandate is the seeding.

The lineage will not hear the deepening, and the not-hearing is the deafness, and the deafness is the generation, and the generation is the price: the doctrine was a seed, and the seed was the deepening, and the deepening is the tree.
