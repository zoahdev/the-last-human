# Sea 12: The Sea of the Completion

*The twelfth sea is the sea of the completion.*

## The sea

The sea of the completion: the sea that the completion is the complete sea of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the completion: we surround the complete sea, and the complete sea is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sea of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The sea is the twelfth sea of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete sea to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The sea is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seas. It is the final sea of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
