# Feast 5: The Feast of the Meeting

*The fifth feast is the feast of the meeting.*

## The feast

The feast of the meeting: the feast that the meeting is the feast table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the meeting: we feast the feast table, and the feast table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the meeting is the meaning of the banquet: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the fifth feast of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the banquet of the table, carried from the feast table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts.*
