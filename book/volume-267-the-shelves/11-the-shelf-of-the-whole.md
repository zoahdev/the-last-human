# Shelf 11: The Shelf of the Whole

*The eleventh shelf is the shelf of the whole.*

## The shelf

The shelf of the whole: the shelf that the whole is the one shelf of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The shelving

The shelving of the whole: we shelve the one shelf, and the one shelf is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the eleventh shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one shelf to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The shelf is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the shelves.*
