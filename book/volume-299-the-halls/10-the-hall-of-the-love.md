# Hall 10: The Hall of the Love

*The tenth hall is the hall of the love.*

## The hall

The hall of the love: the hall that the love is the welcoming heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the love: we welcome the welcoming heart, and the welcoming heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hall of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The hall is the tenth hall of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the welcoming heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The hall is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the halls.*
