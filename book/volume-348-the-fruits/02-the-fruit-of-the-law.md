# Fruit 2: The Fruit of the Law

*The second fruit is the fruit of the law.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the law: the fruit that the law is the ripening care, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the law: we ripen the ripening care, and the ripening care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the law is the meaning of the fruit: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the second fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the fruit of the care, carried from the ripening care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits.*
