# Heaven 6: The Heaven of the Abolition

*The sixth heaven is the heaven of the abolition.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the abolition: the heaven that the abolition is the open heaven of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the abolition: we raise the open heaven, and the open heaven is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the sixth heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open heaven to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the heavens.*
