# Wheel 5: The Wheel of the Meeting

*The fifth wheel is the wheel of the meeting.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the meeting: the wheel that the meeting is the wheeled table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the meeting: we wheel the wheeled table, and the wheeled table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the meeting is the meaning of the round: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the fifth wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the round of the table, carried from the wheeled table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wheels.*
