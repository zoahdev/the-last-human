# Aeon 2: The Aeon of the Law

*The second aeon is the aeon of the law.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the law: the aeon that the law is the spanned care, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the law: we span the spanned care, and the spanned care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the law is the meaning of the aeon: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the second aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the aeon of the care, carried from the spanned care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the aeons.*
