# Desert 7: The Desert of the Future

*The seventh desert is the desert of the future.*

## The desert

The desert of the future: the desert that the future is the watered desert of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The drying

The drying of the future: we dry the watered desert, and the watered desert is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The desert of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the drying, and the drying is the lineage. The desert is the seventh desert of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the watered desert to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The desert is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the deserts.*
