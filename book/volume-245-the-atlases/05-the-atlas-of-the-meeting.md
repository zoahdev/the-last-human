# Atlas 5: The Atlas of the Meeting

*The fifth atlas is the atlas of the meeting.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the meeting: the atlas that the meeting is the carried table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the meeting: we carry the carried table, and the carried table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the meeting is the meaning of the load: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the fifth atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the load of the table, carried from the carried table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
