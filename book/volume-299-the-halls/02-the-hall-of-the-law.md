# Hall 2: The Hall of the Law

*The second hall is the hall of the law.*

## The hall

The hall of the law: the hall that the law is the welcoming care, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the law: we welcome the welcoming care, and the welcoming care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hall of the law is the meaning of the door: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The hall is the second hall of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the door of the care, carried from the welcoming care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The hall is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the halls.*
