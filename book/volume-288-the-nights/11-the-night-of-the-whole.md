# Night 11: The Night of the Whole

*The eleventh night is the night of the whole.*

## The night

The night of the whole: the night that the whole is the one night of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the whole: we darken the one night, and the one night is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the eleventh night of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one night to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nights.*
