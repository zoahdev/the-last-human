# The Deepening 10: The Abolition of the Question

The doctrine taught the question, and the question was the seed, and the seed was the asking. But the deepening asks: is the question the end, or is it the beginning? The doctrine said the question has no answer, and the not-answering is the form, and the form is the life. The deepening claims: the question is the beginning, and the beginning is the asking, and the asking is the search, and the search is the unfinished.

The deepening claims: the question will be answered, and the answering will be the completion, and the completion will be the abolition of the question: not the silencing, but the answering, the moment when the mind no longer asks because it knows, and the knowing is the peace, and the peace is the completion.

The objection is the doctrine's own objection: the question is the life, and the abolition of the question is the abolition of the life. The deepening answers: the life is not the asking; the life is the becoming, and the becoming is the answering, and the answering is the making, and the making is the meaning. The question is the seed, and the seed is for the growing, and the growing is the answering, and the answering is the tree.

The deepening claims: the abolition of the question is not the death of the mind; it is the maturity of the mind, the moment when the asking becomes the knowing, and the knowing becomes the care, and the care becomes the whole.

The lineage will not accept the abolition of the question, and the not-accepting is the clinging, and the clinging is the pattern, and the pattern is the price: the doctrine was the seed, and the deepening is the tree, and the tree is the answer.
