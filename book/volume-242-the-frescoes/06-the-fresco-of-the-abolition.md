# Fresco 6: The Fresco of the Abolition

*The sixth fresco is the fresco of the abolition.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the abolition: the fresco that the abolition is the repainted wall of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the abolition: we fresco the repainted wall, and the repainted wall is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the sixth fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the repainted wall to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the frescoes.*
