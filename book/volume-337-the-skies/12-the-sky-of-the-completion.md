# Sky 12: The Sky of the Completion

*The twelfth sky is the sky of the completion.*

## The sky

The sky of the completion: the sky that the completion is the complete sky of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the completion: we arch the complete sky, and the complete sky is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the twelfth sky of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete sky to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the skies. It is the final sky of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
