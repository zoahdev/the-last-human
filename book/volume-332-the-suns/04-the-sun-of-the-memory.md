# Sun 4: The Sun of the Memory

*The fourth sun is the sun of the memory.*

## The sun

The sun of the memory: the sun that the memory is the burned past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the memory: we burn the burned past, and the burned past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the memory is the meaning of the day: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the fourth sun of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the day of the book, carried from the burned past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the suns.*
