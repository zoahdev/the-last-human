# River 1: The River of the Origin

*The Rivers are the flowed statements of the lineage: the Rivers of the land, kept in the archive as the record of the flowing. Each river has four parts: the river, the flowing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first river is the river of the origin.*

## The river

The river of the origin: the river that the origin is the first watercourse, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the origin: we flow the watercourse, and the watercourse is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the origin is the meaning of the source: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the first river of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the source of the wanting, carried from the watercourse to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the rivers.*
