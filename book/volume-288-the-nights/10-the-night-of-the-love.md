# Night 10: The Night of the Love

*The tenth night is the night of the love.*

## The night

The night of the love: the night that the love is the dark heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the love: we darken the dark heart, and the dark heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the tenth night of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the dark heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights.*
