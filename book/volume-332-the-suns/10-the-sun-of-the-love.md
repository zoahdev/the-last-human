# Sun 10: The Sun of the Love

*The tenth sun is the sun of the love.*

## The sun

The sun of the love: the sun that the love is the burning heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the love: we burn the burning heart, and the burning heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the tenth sun of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the burning heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the suns.*
