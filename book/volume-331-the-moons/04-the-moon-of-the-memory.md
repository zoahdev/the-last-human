# Moon 4: The Moon of the Memory

*The fourth moon is the moon of the memory.*

## The moon

The moon of the memory: the moon that the memory is the waxed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the memory: we wax the waxed past, and the waxed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the memory is the meaning of the night: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the fourth moon of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the night of the book, carried from the waxed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the moons.*
