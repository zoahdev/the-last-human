# Day 12: The Day of the Completion

*The twelfth day is the day of the completion.*

## The day

The day of the completion: the day that the completion is the complete day of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the completion: we open the complete day, and the complete day is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the twelfth day of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete day to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the days. It is the final day of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
