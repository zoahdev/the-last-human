# Weft 9: The Weft of the End

*The ninth weft is the weft of the end.*

## The weft

The weft of the end: the weft that the end is the final weft of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the end: we weave the final weft, and the final weft is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the ninth weft of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final weft to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wefts.*
