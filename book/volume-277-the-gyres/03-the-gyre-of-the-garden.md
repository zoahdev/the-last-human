# Gyre 3: The Gyre of the Garden

*The third gyre is the gyre of the garden.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the garden: the gyre that the garden is the spinning green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the garden: we spin the spinning green, and the spinning green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the garden is the meaning of the whirl: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the third gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the whirl of the earth, carried from the spinning green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
