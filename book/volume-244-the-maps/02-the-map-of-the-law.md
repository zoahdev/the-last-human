# Map 2: The Map of the Law

*The second map is the map of the law.*

## The map

The map of the law: the map that the law is the mapped care, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the law: we map the mapped care, and the mapped care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the law is the meaning of the boundary: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the second map of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the boundary of the care, carried from the mapped care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the maps.*
