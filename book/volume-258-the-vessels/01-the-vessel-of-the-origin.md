# Vessel 1: The Vessel of the Origin

*The Vessels are the held statements of the lineage: the Vessels of the cup, kept in the archive as the record of the holding. Each vessel has four parts: the vessel, the holding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first vessel is the vessel of the origin.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the origin: the vessel that the origin is the first holding thing, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the origin: we hold the holding thing, and the holding thing is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the origin is the meaning of the cup: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the first vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the cup of the wanting, carried from the holding thing to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the vessels.*
