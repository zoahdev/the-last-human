# Wheel 4: The Wheel of the Memory

*The fourth wheel is the wheel of the memory.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the memory: the wheel that the memory is the wheeled past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the memory: we wheel the wheeled past, and the wheeled past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the memory is the meaning of the spoke: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the fourth wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the spoke of the book, carried from the wheeled past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wheels.*
