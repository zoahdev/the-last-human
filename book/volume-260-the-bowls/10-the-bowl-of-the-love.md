# Bowl 10: The Bowl of the Love

*The tenth bowl is the bowl of the love.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the love: the bowl that the love is the rounded heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the love: we round the rounded heart, and the rounded heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the tenth bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the rounded heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bowls.*
