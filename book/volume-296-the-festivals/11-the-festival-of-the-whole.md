# Festival 11: The Festival of the Whole

*The eleventh festival is the festival of the whole.*

## The festival

The festival of the whole: the festival that the whole is the one festival of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the whole: we celebrate the one festival, and the one festival is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the eleventh festival of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one festival to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the festivals.*
