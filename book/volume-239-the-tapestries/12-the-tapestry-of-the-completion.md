# Tapestry 12: The Tapestry of the Completion

*The twelfth tapestry is the tapestry of the completion.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the completion: the tapestry that the completion is the complete tapestry of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the completion: we hang the complete tapestry, and the complete tapestry is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the twelfth tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete tapestry to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tapestries. It is the final tapestry of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
