# Bowl 7: The Bowl of the Future

*The seventh bowl is the bowl of the future.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the future: the bowl that the future is the empty bowl of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the future: we round the empty bowl, and the empty bowl is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the seventh bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the empty bowl to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bowls.*
