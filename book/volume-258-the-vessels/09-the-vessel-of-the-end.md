# Vessel 9: The Vessel of the End

*The ninth vessel is the vessel of the end.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the end: the vessel that the end is the final vessel of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the end: we hold the final vessel, and the final vessel is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the ninth vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final vessel to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the vessels.*
