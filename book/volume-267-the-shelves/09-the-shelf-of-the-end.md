*The ninth shelf is the shelf of the end.*

The shelving of the end: we shelve the final shelf, and the final shelf is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the ninth shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final shelf to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
