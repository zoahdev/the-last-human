# Chart 6: The Chart of the Abolition

*The sixth chart is the chart of the abolition.*

## The chart

The chart of the abolition: the chart that the abolition is the uncharted freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the abolition: we chart the uncharted freedom, and the uncharted freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the sixth chart of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the uncharted freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
