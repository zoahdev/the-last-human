# Well 9: The Well of the End

*The ninth well is the well of the end.*

## The well

The well of the end: the well that the end is the final well of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the end: we draw the final well, and the final well is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the ninth well of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final well to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
