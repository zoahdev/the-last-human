# Night 8: The Night of the Handover

*The eighth night is the night of the handover.*

## The night

The night of the handover: the night that the handover is the darkened hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the handover: we darken the darkened hand, and the darkened hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the handover is the meaning of the lamp: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the eighth night of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the lamp of the hand, carried from the darkened hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nights.*
