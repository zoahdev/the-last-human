# Lake 7: The Lake of the Future

*The seventh lake is the lake of the future.*

## The lake

The lake of the future: the lake that the future is the unfilled lake of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The mirroring

The mirroring of the future: we mirror the unfilled lake, and the unfilled lake is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The lake of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the mirroring, and the mirroring is the lineage. The lake is the seventh lake of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unfilled lake to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The lake is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the lakes.*
