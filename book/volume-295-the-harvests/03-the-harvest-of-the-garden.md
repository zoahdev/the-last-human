## The meaning

The harvest of the garden is the meaning of the crop: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the third harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the crop of the earth, carried from the harvested green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
