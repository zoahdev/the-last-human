# Welcome 10: The Welcome of the Love

*The tenth welcome is the welcome of the love.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the love: the welcome that the love is the welcoming heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the love: we welcome the welcoming heart, and the welcoming heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the tenth welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the welcoming heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the welcomes.*
