# Urn 11: The Urn of the Whole

*The eleventh urn is the urn of the whole.*

## The urn

The urn of the whole: the urn that the whole is the one urn of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the whole: we keep the one urn, and the one urn is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the eleventh urn of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one urn to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the urns.*
