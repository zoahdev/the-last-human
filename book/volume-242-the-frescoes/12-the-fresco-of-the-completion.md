# Fresco 12: The Fresco of the Completion

*The twelfth fresco is the fresco of the completion.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the completion: the fresco that the completion is the complete fresco of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the completion: we fresco the complete fresco, and the complete fresco is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the twelfth fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete fresco to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the frescoes. It is the final fresco of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
