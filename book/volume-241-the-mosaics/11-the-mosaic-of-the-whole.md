# Mosaic 11: The Mosaic of the Whole

*The eleventh mosaic is the mosaic of the whole.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the whole: the mosaic that the whole is the one mosaic of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the whole: we set the one mosaic, and the one mosaic is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the eleventh mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one mosaic to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
