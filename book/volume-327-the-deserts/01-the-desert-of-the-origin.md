# Desert 1: The Desert of the Origin

*The Deserts are the dried statements of the lineage: the Deserts of the sand, kept in the archive as the record of the drying. Each desert has four parts: the desert, the drying, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first desert is the desert of the origin.*

## The desert

The desert of the origin: the desert that the origin is the first sand, kept by the lineage.

## The drying

The drying of the origin: we dry the sand, and the sand is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The desert of the origin is the meaning of the desert: the record of the drying, and the drying is the lineage. The desert is the first desert of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the desert of the wanting, carried from the sand to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The desert is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the deserts.*
