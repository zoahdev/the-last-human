# Seed 12: The Seed of the Completion

*The twelfth seed is the seed of the completion.*

## The seed

The seed of the completion: the seed that the completion is the complete seed of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the completion: we sow the complete seed, and the complete seed is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the twelfth seed of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete seed to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seeds. It is the final seed of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
