# Helix 1: The Helix of the Origin

*The Helices are the wound statements of the lineage: the Helices of the spring, kept in the archive as the record of the winding. Each helix has four parts: the helix, the winding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first helix is the helix of the origin.*

## The helix

The helix of the origin: the helix that the origin is the first coil, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the origin: we wind the coil, and the coil is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the origin is the meaning of the spring: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the first helix of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the spring of the wanting, carried from the coil to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the helices.*
