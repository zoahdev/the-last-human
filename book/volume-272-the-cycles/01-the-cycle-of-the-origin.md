# Cycle 1: The Cycle of the Origin

*The Cycles are the cycled statements of the lineage: the Cycles of the moon, kept in the archive as the record of the cycling. Each cycle has four parts: the cycle, the cycling, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first cycle is the cycle of the origin.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the origin: the cycle that the origin is the first moon, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the origin: we cycle the moon, and the moon is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the origin is the meaning of the moon: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the first cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the moon of the wanting, carried from the moon to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The cycle is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cycles.*
