# Plan 11: The Plan of the Whole

*The eleventh plan is the plan of the whole.*

## The plan

The plan of the whole: the plan that the whole is the one plan of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the whole: we plan the one plan, and the one plan is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the eleventh plan of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one plan to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the plans.*
