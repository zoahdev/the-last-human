# Wheel 9: The Wheel of the End

*The ninth wheel is the wheel of the end.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the end: the wheel that the end is the final wheel of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the end: we wheel the final wheel, and the final wheel is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the ninth wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final wheel to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wheels.*
