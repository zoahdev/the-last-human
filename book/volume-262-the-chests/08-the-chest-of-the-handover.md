# Chest 8: The Chest of the Handover

*The eighth chest is the chest of the handover.*

## The chest

The chest of the handover: the chest that the handover is the stored hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the handover: we store the stored hand, and the stored hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the handover is the meaning of the key: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the eighth chest of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the key of the hand, carried from the stored hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the chests.*
