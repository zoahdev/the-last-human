# Oasis 4: The Oasis of the Memory

*The fourth oasis is the oasis of the memory.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the memory: the oasis that the memory is the watered past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the memory: we water the watered past, and the watered past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the memory is the meaning of the oasis: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the fourth oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the oasis of the book, carried from the watered past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oases.*
