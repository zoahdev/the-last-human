# Mold 5: The Mold of the Meeting

*The fifth mold is the mold of the meeting.*

## The mold

The mold of the meeting: the mold that the meeting is the molded table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the meeting: we mold the molded table, and the molded table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the meeting is the meaning of the wheel: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the fifth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the wheel of the table, carried from the molded table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds.*
