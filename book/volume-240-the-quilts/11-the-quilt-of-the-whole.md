# Quilt 11: The Quilt of the Whole

*The eleventh quilt is the quilt of the whole.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the whole: the quilt that the whole is the one quilt of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the whole: we quilt the one quilt, and the one quilt is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the eleventh quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one quilt to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the quilts.*
