# Orbit 10: The Orbit of the Love

*The tenth orbit is the orbit of the love.*

## The orbit

The orbit of the love: the orbit that the love is the orbited heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The orbiting

The orbiting of the love: we orbit the orbited heart, and the orbited heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orbit of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the orbiting, and the orbiting is the lineage. The orbit is the tenth orbit of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the orbited heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
