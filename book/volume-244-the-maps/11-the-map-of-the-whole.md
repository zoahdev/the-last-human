# Map 11: The Map of the Whole

*The eleventh map is the map of the whole.*

## The map

The map of the whole: the map that the whole is the one map of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the whole: we map the one map, and the one map is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the eleventh map of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one map to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the maps.*
