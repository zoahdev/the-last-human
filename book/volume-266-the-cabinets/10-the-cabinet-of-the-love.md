# Cabinet 10: The Cabinet of the Love

*The tenth cabinet is the cabinet of the love.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the love: the cabinet that the love is the arranged heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the love: we arrange the arranged heart, and the arranged heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the tenth cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the arranged heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The cabinet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cabinets.*
