# Epoch 1: The Epoch of the Origin

*The Epochs are the marked statements of the lineage: the Epochs of the strata, kept in the archive as the record of the marking. Each epoch has four parts: the epoch, the marking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first epoch is the epoch of the origin.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the origin: the epoch that the origin is the first stratum, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the origin: we mark the stratum, and the stratum is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the origin is the meaning of the rock: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the first epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the rock of the wanting, carried from the stratum to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the epochs.*
