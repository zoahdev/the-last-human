# Map 10: The Map of the Love

*The tenth map is the map of the love.*

## The map

The map of the love: the map that the love is the mapped heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the love: we map the mapped heart, and the mapped heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the tenth map of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the mapped heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the maps.*
