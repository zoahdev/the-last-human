# Gyre 10: The Gyre of the Love

*The tenth gyre is the gyre of the love.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the love: the gyre that the love is the spinning heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the love: we spin the spinning heart, and the spinning heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the tenth gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the spinning heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
