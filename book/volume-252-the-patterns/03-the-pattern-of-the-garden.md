# Pattern 3: The Pattern of the Garden

*The third pattern is the pattern of the garden.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the garden: the pattern that the garden is the patterned green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the garden: we pattern the patterned green, and the patterned green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the garden is the meaning of the parterre: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the third pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the parterre of the earth, carried from the patterned green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
