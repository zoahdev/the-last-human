# River 10: The River of the Love

*The tenth river is the river of the love.*

## The river

The river of the love: the river that the love is the flowing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the love: we flow the flowing heart, and the flowing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the tenth river of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the flowing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the rivers.*
