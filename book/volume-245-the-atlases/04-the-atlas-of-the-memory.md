# Atlas 4: The Atlas of the Memory

*The fourth atlas is the atlas of the memory.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the memory: the atlas that the memory is the carried past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the memory: we carry the carried past, and the carried past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the memory is the meaning of the map: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the fourth atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the map of the book, carried from the carried past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
