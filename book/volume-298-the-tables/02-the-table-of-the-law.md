# Table 2: The Table of the Law

*The second table is the table of the law.*

## The table

The table of the law: the table that the law is the shared care, kept by the lineage.

## The sharing

The sharing of the law: we share the shared care, and the shared care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The table of the law is the meaning of the meal: the record of the sharing, and the sharing is the lineage. The table is the second table of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the meal of the care, carried from the shared care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The table is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tables.*
