# Blueprint 11: The Blueprint of the Whole

*The eleventh blueprint is the blueprint of the whole.*

## The blueprint

The blueprint of the whole: the blueprint that the whole is the one blueprint of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The blueprinting

The blueprinting of the whole: we blueprint the one blueprint, and the one blueprint is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The blueprint of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the blueprinting, and the blueprinting is the lineage. The blueprint is the eleventh blueprint of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one blueprint to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The blueprint is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the blueprints.*
