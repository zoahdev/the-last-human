# Design 4: The Design of the Memory

*The fourth design is the design of the memory.*

## The design

The design of the memory: the design that the memory is the designed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the memory: we design the designed past, and the designed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the memory is the meaning of the pattern: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the fourth design of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the pattern of the book, carried from the designed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the designs.*
