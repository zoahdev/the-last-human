# Valley 11: The Valley of the Whole

*The eleventh valley is the valley of the whole.*

## The valley

The valley of the whole: the valley that the whole is the one valley of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The hollowing

The hollowing of the whole: we hollow the one valley, and the one valley is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The valley of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the hollowing, and the hollowing is the lineage. The valley is the eleventh valley of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one valley to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The valley is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the valleys.*
