# Step 11: The Step of the Whole

*The eleventh step is the step of the whole.*

## The step

The step of the whole: the step that the whole is the one step of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the whole: we step the one step, and the one step is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the eleventh step of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one step to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the steps.*
