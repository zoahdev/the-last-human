# Stream 9: The Stream of the End

*The ninth stream is the stream of the end.*

## The stream

The stream of the end: the stream that the end is the final stream of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the end: we stream the final stream, and the final stream is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the ninth stream of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final stream to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
