# Harvest 4: The Harvest of the Memory

*The fourth harvest is the harvest of the memory.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the memory: the harvest that the memory is the harvested past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the memory: we harvest the harvested past, and the harvested past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the memory is the meaning of the grain: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the fourth harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the grain of the book, carried from the harvested past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the harvests.*
