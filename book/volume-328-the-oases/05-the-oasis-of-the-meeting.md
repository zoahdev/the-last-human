# Oasis 5: The Oasis of the Meeting

*The fifth oasis is the oasis of the meeting.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the meeting: the oasis that the meeting is the oasis table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the meeting: we water the oasis table, and the oasis table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the meeting is the meaning of the oasis: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the fifth oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the oasis of the table, carried from the oasis table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oases.*
