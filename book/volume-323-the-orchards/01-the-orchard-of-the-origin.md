# Orchard 1: The Orchard of the Origin

*The Orchards are the fruited statements of the lineage: the Orchards of the fruit trees, kept in the archive as the record of the fruiting. Each orchard has four parts: the orchard, the fruiting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first orchard is the orchard of the origin.*

## The orchard

The orchard of the origin: the orchard that the origin is the first fruit tree, kept by the lineage.

## The fruiting

The fruiting of the origin: we fruit the fruit tree, and the fruit tree is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orchard of the origin is the meaning of the orchard: the record of the fruiting, and the fruiting is the lineage. The orchard is the first orchard of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the orchard of the wanting, carried from the fruit tree to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The orchard is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the orchards.*
