# Array 10: The Array of the Love

*The tenth array is the array of the love.*

## The array

The array of the love: the array that the love is the arrayed heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arraying

The arraying of the love: we array the arrayed heart, and the arrayed heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The array of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the arraying, and the arraying is the lineage. The array is the tenth array of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the arrayed heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
