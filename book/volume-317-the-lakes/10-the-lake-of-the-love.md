# Lake 10: The Lake of the Love

*The tenth lake is the lake of the love.*

## The lake

The lake of the love: the lake that the love is the mirroring heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The mirroring

The mirroring of the love: we mirror the mirroring heart, and the mirroring heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The lake of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the mirroring, and the mirroring is the lineage. The lake is the tenth lake of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the mirroring heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The lake is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the lakes.*
