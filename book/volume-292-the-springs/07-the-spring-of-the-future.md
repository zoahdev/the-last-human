# Spring 7: The Spring of the Future

*The seventh spring is the spring of the future.*

## The spring

The spring of the future: the spring that the future is the coming spring of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the future: we spring the coming spring, and the coming spring is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the seventh spring of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the coming spring to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the springs.*
