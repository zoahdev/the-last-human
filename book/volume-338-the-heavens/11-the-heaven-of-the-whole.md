# Heaven 11: The Heaven of the Whole

*The eleventh heaven is the heaven of the whole.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the whole: the heaven that the whole is the one heaven of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the whole: we raise the one heaven, and the one heaven is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the eleventh heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one heaven to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the heavens.*
