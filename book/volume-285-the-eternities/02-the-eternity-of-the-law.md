# Eternity 2: The Eternity of the Law

*The second eternity is the eternity of the law.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the law: the eternity that the law is the exceeding care, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the law: we exceed the exceeding care, and the exceeding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the law is the meaning of the forever: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the second eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the forever of the care, carried from the exceeding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
