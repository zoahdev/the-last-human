# Heaven 2: The Heaven of the Law

*The second heaven is the heaven of the law.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the law: the heaven that the law is the raising care, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the law: we raise the raising care, and the raising care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the law is the meaning of the height: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the second heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the height of the care, carried from the raising care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the heavens.*
