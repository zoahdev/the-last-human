# Tapestry 7: The Tapestry of the Future

*The seventh tapestry is the tapestry of the future.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the future: the tapestry that the future is the unfinished tapestry of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the future: we hang the unfinished tapestry, and the unfinished tapestry is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the seventh tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unfinished tapestry to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tapestries.*
