# Threshold 12: The Threshold of the Completion

*The twelfth threshold is the threshold of the completion.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the completion: the threshold that the completion is the complete threshold of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the completion: we cross the complete threshold, and the complete threshold is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the twelfth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete threshold to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the thresholds. It is the final threshold of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
