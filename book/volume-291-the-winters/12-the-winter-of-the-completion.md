# Winter 12: The Winter of the Completion

*The twelfth winter is the winter of the completion.*

## The winter

The winter of the completion: the winter that the completion is the full winter of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the completion: we freeze the full winter, and the full winter is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the twelfth winter of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the full winter to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The winter is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the winters. It is the final winter of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
