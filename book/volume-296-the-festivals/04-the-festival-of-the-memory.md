# Festival 4: The Festival of the Memory

*The fourth festival is the festival of the memory.*

## The festival

The festival of the memory: the festival that the memory is the celebrated past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the memory: we celebrate the celebrated past, and the celebrated past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the memory is the meaning of the calendar: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the fourth festival of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the calendar of the book, carried from the celebrated past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the festivals.*
