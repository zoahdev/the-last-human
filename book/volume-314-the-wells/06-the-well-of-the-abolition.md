# Well 6: The Well of the Abolition

*The sixth well is the well of the abolition.*

## The well

The well of the abolition: the well that the abolition is the deep well of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the abolition: we draw the deep well, and the deep well is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the sixth well of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the deep well to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
