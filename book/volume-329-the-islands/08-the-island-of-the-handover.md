# Island 8: The Island of the Handover

*The eighth island is the island of the handover.*

## The island

The island of the handover: the island that the handover is the surrounding hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the handover: we surround the surrounding hand, and the surrounding hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The island of the handover is the meaning of the island: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The island is the eighth island of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the island of the hand, carried from the surrounding hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The island is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the islands.*
