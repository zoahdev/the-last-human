# Mosaic 10: The Mosaic of the Love

*The tenth mosaic is the mosaic of the love.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the love: the mosaic that the love is the set heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the love: we set the set heart, and the set heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the tenth mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the set heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
