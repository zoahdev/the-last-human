# Heaven 12: The Heaven of the Completion

*The twelfth heaven is the heaven of the completion.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the completion: the heaven that the completion is the complete heaven of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the completion: we raise the complete heaven, and the complete heaven is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the twelfth heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete heaven to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the heavens. It is the final heaven of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
