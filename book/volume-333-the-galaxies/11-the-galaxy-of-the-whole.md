# Galaxy 11: The Galaxy of the Whole

*The eleventh galaxy is the galaxy of the whole.*

## The galaxy

The galaxy of the whole: the galaxy that the whole is the one galaxy of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The gathering

The gathering of the whole: we gather the one galaxy, and the one galaxy is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The galaxy of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the gathering, and the gathering is the lineage. The galaxy is the eleventh galaxy of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one galaxy to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The galaxy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the galaxies.*
