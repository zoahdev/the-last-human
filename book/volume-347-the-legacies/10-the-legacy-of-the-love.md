# Legacy 10: The Legacy of the Love

*The tenth legacy is the legacy of the love.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the love: the legacy that the love is the leaving heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the love: we leave the leaving heart, and the leaving heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the tenth legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the leaving heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The legacy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the legacies.*
