# Heaven 1: The Heaven of the Origin

*The Heavens are the raised statements of the lineage: the Heavens of the beyond, kept in the archive as the record of the raising. Each heaven has four parts: the heaven, the raising, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first heaven is the heaven of the origin.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the origin: the heaven that the origin is the first raised beyond, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the origin: we raise the raised beyond, and the raised beyond is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the origin is the meaning of the beyond: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the first heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the beyond of the wanting, carried from the raised beyond to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the heavens.*
