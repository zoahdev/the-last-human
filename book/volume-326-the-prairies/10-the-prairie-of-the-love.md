# Prairie 10: The Prairie of the Love

*The tenth prairie is the prairie of the love.*

## The prairie

The prairie of the love: the prairie that the love is the stretching heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The stretching

The stretching of the love: we stretch the stretching heart, and the stretching heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The prairie of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the stretching, and the stretching is the lineage. The prairie is the tenth prairie of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the stretching heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The prairie is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the prairies.*
