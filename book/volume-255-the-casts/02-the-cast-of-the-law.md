# Cast 2: The Cast of the Law

*The second cast is the cast of the law.*

## The cast

The cast of the law: the cast that the law is the cast care, kept by the lineage.

## The casting

The casting of the law: we cast the cast care, and the cast care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cast of the law is the meaning of the alloy: the record of the casting, and the casting is the lineage. The cast is the second cast of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the alloy of the care, carried from the cast care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The cast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the casts.*
