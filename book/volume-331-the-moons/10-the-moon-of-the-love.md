# Moon 10: The Moon of the Love

*The tenth moon is the moon of the love.*

## The moon

The moon of the love: the moon that the love is the waxing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the love: we wax the waxing heart, and the waxing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the tenth moon of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the waxing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the moons.*
