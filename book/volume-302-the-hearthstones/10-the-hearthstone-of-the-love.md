# Hearthstone 10: The Hearthstone of the Love

*The tenth hearthstone is the hearthstone of the love.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the love: the hearthstone that the love is the warm heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the love: we warm the warm heart, and the warm heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the tenth hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the warm heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the hearthstones.*
