# Port 1: The Port of the Origin

*The Ports are the arrived statements of the lineage: the Ports of the sea, kept in the archive as the record of the arriving. Each port has four parts: the port, the arriving, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first port is the port of the origin.*

## The port

The port of the origin: the port that the origin is the first anchorage, kept by the lineage.

## The arriving

The arriving of the origin: we arrive the anchorage, and the anchorage is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The port of the origin is the meaning of the harbor: the record of the arriving, and the arriving is the lineage. The port is the first port of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the harbor of the wanting, carried from the anchorage to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The port is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ports.*
