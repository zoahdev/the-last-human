# Mold 1: The Mold of the Origin

*The Molds are the molded statements of the lineage: the Molds of the clay, kept in the archive as the record of the molding. Each mold has four parts: the mold, the molding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first mold is the mold of the origin.*

## The mold

The mold of the origin: the mold that the origin is the first clay, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the origin: we mold the clay, and the clay is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the origin is the meaning of the vessel: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the first mold of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the vessel of the wanting, carried from the clay to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the molds.*
