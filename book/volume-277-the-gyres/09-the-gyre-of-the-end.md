# Gyre 9: The Gyre of the End

*The ninth gyre is the gyre of the end.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the end: the gyre that the end is the final gyre of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the end: we spin the final gyre, and the final gyre is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the ninth gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final gyre to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
