# Dawn 6: The Dawn of the Abolition

*The sixth dawn is the dawn of the abolition.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the abolition: the dawn that the abolition is the full dawn of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the abolition: we break the full dawn, and the full dawn is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the sixth dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the full dawn to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the dawns.*
