# Day 4: The Day of the Memory

*The fourth day is the day of the memory.*

## The day

The day of the memory: the day that the memory is the opened past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the memory: we open the opened past, and the opened past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the memory is the meaning of the diary: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the fourth day of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the diary of the book, carried from the opened past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the days.*
