# Pattern 7: The Pattern of the Future

*The seventh pattern is the pattern of the future.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the future: the pattern that the future is the unpatterned dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the future: we pattern the unpatterned dawn, and the unpatterned dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the seventh pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unpatterned dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
