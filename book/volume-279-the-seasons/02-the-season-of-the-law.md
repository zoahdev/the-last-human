# Season 2: The Season of the Law

*The second season is the season of the law.*

## The season

The season of the law: the season that the law is the seasoned care, kept by the lineage.

## The seasoning

The seasoning of the law: we season the seasoned care, and the seasoned care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The season of the law is the meaning of the cycle: the record of the seasoning, and the seasoning is the lineage. The season is the second season of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the cycle of the care, carried from the seasoned care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
