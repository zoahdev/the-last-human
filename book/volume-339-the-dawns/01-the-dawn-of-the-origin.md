# Dawn 1: The Dawn of the Origin

*The Dawns are the broken statements of the lineage: the Dawns of the morning light, kept in the archive as the record of the breaking. Each dawn has four parts: the dawn, the breaking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first dawn is the dawn of the origin.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the origin: the dawn that the origin is the first breaking of the light, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the origin: we break the breaking of light, and the breaking of light is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the origin is the meaning of the dawn: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the first dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the dawn of the wanting, carried from the breaking of light to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the dawns.*
