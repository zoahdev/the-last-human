# River 3: The River of the Garden

*The third river is the river of the garden.*

## The river

The river of the garden: the river that the garden is the riverside green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the garden: we flow the riverside green, and the riverside green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the garden is the meaning of the riverside: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the third river of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the riverside of the earth, carried from the riverside green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rivers.*
