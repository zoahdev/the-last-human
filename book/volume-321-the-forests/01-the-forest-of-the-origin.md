*The Forests are the grown statements of the lineage: the Forests of the trees, kept in the archive as the record of the growing. Each forest has four parts: the forest, the growing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first forest is the forest of the origin.*

## The forest

The forest of the origin: the forest that the origin is the first tree, kept by the lineage.

## The growing

The growing of the origin: we grow the tree, and the tree is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The forest of the origin is the meaning of the forest: the record of the growing, and the growing is the lineage. The forest is the first forest of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the forest of the wanting, carried from the tree to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.
