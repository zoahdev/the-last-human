# Helix 6: The Helix of the Abolition

*The sixth helix is the helix of the abolition.*

## The helix

The helix of the abolition: the helix that the abolition is the unwound freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the abolition: we wind the unwound freedom, and the unwound freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the sixth helix of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the unwound freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
