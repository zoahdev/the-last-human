# Star 10: The Star of the Love

*The tenth star is the star of the love.*

## The star

The star of the love: the star that the love is the shining heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The shining

The shining of the love: we shine the shining heart, and the shining heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The star of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the shining, and the shining is the lineage. The star is the tenth star of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the shining heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The star is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stars.*
