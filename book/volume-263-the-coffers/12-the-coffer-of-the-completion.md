# Coffer 12: The Coffer of the Completion

*The twelfth coffer is the coffer of the completion.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the completion: the coffer that the completion is the complete coffer of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the completion: we lock the complete coffer, and the complete coffer is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the twelfth coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete coffer to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the coffers. It is the final coffer of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
