# Meadow 10: The Meadow of the Love

*The tenth meadow is the meadow of the love.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the love: the meadow that the love is the blooming heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the love: we bloom the blooming heart, and the blooming heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the tenth meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the blooming heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the meadows.*
