# Box 11: The Box of the Whole

*The eleventh box is the box of the whole.*

## The box

The box of the whole: the box that the whole is the one box of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the whole: we box the one box, and the one box is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the eleventh box of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one box to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the boxes.*
