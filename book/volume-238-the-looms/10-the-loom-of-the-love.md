# Loom 10: The Loom of the Love

*The tenth loom is the loom of the love.*

## The loom

The loom of the love: the loom that the love is the loomed heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the love: we loom the loomed heart, and the loomed heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the tenth loom of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the loomed heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the looms.*
