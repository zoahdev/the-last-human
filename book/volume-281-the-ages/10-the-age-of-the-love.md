# Age 10: The Age of the Love

*The tenth age is the age of the love.*

## The age

The age of the love: the age that the love is the aged heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The aging

The aging of the love: we age the aged heart, and the aged heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The age of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the aging, and the aging is the lineage. The age is the tenth age of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the aged heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The age is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ages.*
