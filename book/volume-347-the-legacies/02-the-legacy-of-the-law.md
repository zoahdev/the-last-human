# Legacy 2: The Legacy of the Law

*The second legacy is the legacy of the law.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the law: the legacy that the law is the leaving care, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the law: we leave the leaving care, and the leaving care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the law is the meaning of the legacy: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the second legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the legacy of the care, carried from the leaving care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The legacy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the legacies.*
