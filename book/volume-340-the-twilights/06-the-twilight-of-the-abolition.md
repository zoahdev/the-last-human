# Twilight 6: The Twilight of the Abolition

*The sixth twilight is the twilight of the abolition.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the abolition: the twilight that the abolition is the lasting light of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the abolition: we fade the lasting light, and the lasting light is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the sixth twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the lasting light to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the twilights.*
