# Atlas 2: The Atlas of the Law

*The second atlas is the atlas of the law.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the law: the atlas that the law is the carried care, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the law: we carry the carried care, and the carried care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the law is the meaning of the weight: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the second atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the weight of the care, carried from the carried care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
