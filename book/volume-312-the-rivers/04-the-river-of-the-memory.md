# River 4: The River of the Memory

*The fourth river is the river of the memory.*

## The river

The river of the memory: the river that the memory is the flowed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the memory: we flow the flowed past, and the flowed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the memory is the meaning of the source: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the fourth river of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the source of the book, carried from the flowed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the rivers.*
