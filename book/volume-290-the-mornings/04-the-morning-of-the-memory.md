# Morning 4: The Morning of the Memory

*The fourth morning is the morning of the memory.*

## The morning

The morning of the memory: the morning that the memory is the woken past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the memory: we wake the woken past, and the woken past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the memory is the meaning of the birdsong: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the fourth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the birdsong of the book, carried from the woken past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the mornings.*
