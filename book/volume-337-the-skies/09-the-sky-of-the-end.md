# Sky 9: The Sky of the End

*The ninth sky is the sky of the end.*

## The sky

The sky of the end: the sky that the end is the final sky of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the end: we arch the final sky, and the final sky is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the ninth sky of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final sky to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the skies.*
