# Chest 10: The Chest of the Love

*The tenth chest is the chest of the love.*

## The chest

The chest of the love: the chest that the love is the stored heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the love: we store the stored heart, and the stored heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the tenth chest of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the stored heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the chests.*
