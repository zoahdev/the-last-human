# Sea 11: The Sea of the Whole

*The eleventh sea is the sea of the whole.*

## The sea

The sea of the whole: the sea that the whole is the one sea of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the whole: we surround the one sea, and the one sea is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sea of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The sea is the eleventh sea of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one sea to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The sea is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seas.*
