# Row 1: The Row of the Origin

*The Rows are the rowed statements of the lineage: the Rows of the field, kept in the archive as the record of the rowing. Each row has four parts: the row, the rowing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first row is the row of the origin.*

## The row

The row of the origin: the row that the origin is the first furrow, kept by the lineage.

## The rowing

The rowing of the origin: we row the furrow, and the furrow is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The row of the origin is the meaning of the field: the record of the rowing, and the rowing is the lineage. The row is the first row of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the field of the wanting, carried from the furrow to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The row is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rows.*
