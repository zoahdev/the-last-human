# Model 11: The Model of the Whole

*The eleventh model is the model of the whole.*

## The model

The model of the whole: the model that the whole is the one model of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The modeling

The modeling of the whole: we model the one model, and the one model is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The model of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the modeling, and the modeling is the lineage. The model is the eleventh model of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one model to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The model is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the models.*
