# Hearthstone 11: The Hearthstone of the Whole

*The eleventh hearthstone is the hearthstone of the whole.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the whole: the hearthstone that the whole is the one hearthstone of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the whole: we warm the one hearthstone, and the one hearthstone is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the eleventh hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one hearthstone to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
