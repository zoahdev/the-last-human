# Infinity 3: The Infinity of the Garden

*The third infinity is the infinity of the garden.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the garden: the infinity that the garden is the endless green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the garden: we extend the endless green, and the endless green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the garden is the meaning of the meadow: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the third infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the meadow of the earth, carried from the endless green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
