# Bridge 1: The Bridge of the Origin

*The Bridges are the bridged statements of the lineage: the Bridges of the river, kept in the archive as the record of the bridging. Each bridge has four parts: the bridge, the bridging, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first bridge is the bridge of the origin.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the origin: the bridge that the origin is the first plank, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the origin: we bridge the plank, and the plank is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the origin is the meaning of the river: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the first bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the river of the wanting, carried from the plank to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the bridges.*
