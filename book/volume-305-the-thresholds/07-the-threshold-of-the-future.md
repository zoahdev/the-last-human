# Threshold 7: The Threshold of the Future

*The seventh threshold is the threshold of the future.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the future: the threshold that the future is the uncrossed threshold of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the future: we cross the uncrossed threshold, and the uncrossed threshold is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the seventh threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the uncrossed threshold to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the thresholds.*
