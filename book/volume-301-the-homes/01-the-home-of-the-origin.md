# Home 1: The Home of the Origin

*The Homes are the homed statements of the lineage: the Homes of the heart, kept in the archive as the record of the homing. Each home has four parts: the home, the homing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first home is the home of the origin.*

## The home

The home of the origin: the home that the origin is the first heart place, kept by the lineage.

## The homing

The homing of the origin: we home the heart place, and the heart place is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The home of the origin is the meaning of the heart: the record of the homing, and the homing is the lineage. The home is the first home of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the heart of the wanting, carried from the heart place to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The home is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the homes.*
