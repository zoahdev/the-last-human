# Tapestry 11: The Tapestry of the Whole

*The eleventh tapestry is the tapestry of the whole.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the whole: the tapestry that the whole is the one tapestry of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the whole: we hang the one tapestry, and the one tapestry is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the eleventh tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one tapestry to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tapestries.*
