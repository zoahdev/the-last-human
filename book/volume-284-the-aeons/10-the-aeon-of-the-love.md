# Aeon 10: The Aeon of the Love

*The tenth aeon is the aeon of the love.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the love: the aeon that the love is the spanned heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the love: we span the spanned heart, and the spanned heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the tenth aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the spanned heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the aeons.*
