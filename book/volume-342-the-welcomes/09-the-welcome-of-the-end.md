# Welcome 9: The Welcome of the End

*The ninth welcome is the welcome of the end.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the end: the welcome that the end is the final welcome of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the end: we welcome the final welcome, and the final welcome is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the ninth welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final welcome to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the welcomes.*
