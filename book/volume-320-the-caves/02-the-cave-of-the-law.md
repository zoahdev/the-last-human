# Cave 2: The Cave of the Law

*The second cave is the cave of the law.*

## The cave

The cave of the law: the cave that the law is the sheltering care, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the law: we shelter the sheltering care, and the sheltering care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the law is the meaning of the cave: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the second cave of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the cave of the care, carried from the sheltering care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caves.*
