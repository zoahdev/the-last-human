# Day 3: The Day of the Garden

*The third day is the day of the garden.*

## The day

The day of the garden: the day that the garden is the daylight green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the garden: we open the daylight green, and the daylight green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the garden is the meaning of the sunlight: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the third day of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the sunlight of the earth, carried from the daylight green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the days.*
