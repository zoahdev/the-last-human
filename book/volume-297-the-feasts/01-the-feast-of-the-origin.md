# Feast 1: The Feast of the Origin

*The Feasts are the feasted statements of the lineage: the Feasts of the table, kept in the archive as the record of the feasting. Each feast has four parts: the feast, the feasting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first feast is the feast of the origin.*

## The feast

The feast of the origin: the feast that the origin is the first shared meal, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the origin: we feast the shared meal, and the shared meal is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the origin is the meaning of the table: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the first feast of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the table of the wanting, carried from the shared meal to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the feasts.*
