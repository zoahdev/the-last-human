# House 11: The House of the Whole

*The eleventh house is the house of the whole.*

## The house

The house of the whole: the house that the whole is the one house of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The housing

The housing of the whole: we house the one house, and the one house is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The house of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the housing, and the housing is the lineage. The house is the eleventh house of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one house to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The house is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the houses.*
