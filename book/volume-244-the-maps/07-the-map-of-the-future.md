# Map 7: The Map of the Future

*The seventh map is the map of the future.*

## The map

The map of the future: the map that the future is the unmapped land of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the future: we map the unmapped land, and the unmapped land is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the seventh map of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unmapped land to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the maps.*
