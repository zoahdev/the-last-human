# Aeon 3: The Aeon of the Garden

*The third aeon is the aeon of the garden.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the garden: the aeon that the garden is the spanned green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the garden: we span the spanned green, and the spanned green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the garden is the meaning of the cosmic garden: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the third aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the cosmic garden of the earth, carried from the spanned green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the aeons.*
