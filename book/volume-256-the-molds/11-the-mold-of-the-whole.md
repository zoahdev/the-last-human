# Mold 11: The Mold of the Whole

*The eleventh mold is the mold of the whole.*

## The mold

The mold of the whole: the mold that the whole is the one mold of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the whole: we mold the one mold, and the one mold is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the eleventh mold of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one mold to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds.*
