# Moon 9: The Moon of the End

*The ninth moon is the moon of the end.*

## The moon

The moon of the end: the moon that the end is the final moon of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the end: we wax the final moon, and the final moon is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the ninth moon of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final moon to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the moons.*
