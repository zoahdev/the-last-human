# Fruit 9: The Fruit of the End

*The ninth fruit is the fruit of the end.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the end: the fruit that the end is the final fruit of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the end: we ripen the final fruit, and the final fruit is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the ninth fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final fruit to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the fruits.*
