# Night 7: The Night of the Future

*The seventh night is the night of the future.*

## The night

The night of the future: the night that the future is the night before the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the future: we darken the night before, and the night before is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the seventh night of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the night before to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights.*
