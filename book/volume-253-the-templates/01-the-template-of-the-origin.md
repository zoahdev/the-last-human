# Template 1: The Template of the Origin

*The Templates are the cut statements of the lineage: the Templates of the mold, kept in the archive as the record of the cutting. Each template has four parts: the template, the cutting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first template is the template of the origin.*

## The template

The template of the origin: the template that the origin is the first master form, kept by the lineage.

## The cutting

The cutting of the origin: we cut the master form, and the master form is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The template of the origin is the meaning of the mold: the record of the cutting, and the cutting is the lineage. The template is the first template of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the mold of the wanting, carried from the master form to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The template is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the templates.*
