# Treasure 6: The Treasure of the Abolition

*The sixth treasure is the treasure of the abolition.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the abolition: the treasure that the abolition is the common treasure of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the abolition: we treasure the common treasure, and the common treasure is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the sixth treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the common treasure to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the treasures.*
