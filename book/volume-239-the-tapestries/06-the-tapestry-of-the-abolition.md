# Tapestry 6: The Tapestry of the Abolition

*The sixth tapestry is the tapestry of the abolition.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the abolition: the tapestry that the abolition is the new picture of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the abolition: we hang the new picture, and the new picture is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the sixth tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new picture to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tapestries.*
