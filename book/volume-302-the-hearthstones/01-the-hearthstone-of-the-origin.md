# Hearthstone 1: The Hearthstone of the Origin

*The Hearthstones are the warmed statements of the lineage: the Hearthstones of the fire, kept in the archive as the record of the warming. Each hearthstone has four parts: the hearthstone, the warming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first hearthstone is the hearthstone of the origin.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the origin: the hearthstone that the origin is the first warm stone, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the origin: we warm the warm stone, and the warm stone is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the origin is the meaning of the fire: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the first hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the fire of the wanting, carried from the warm stone to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
