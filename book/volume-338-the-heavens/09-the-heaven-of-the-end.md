# Heaven 9: The Heaven of the End

*The ninth heaven is the heaven of the end.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the end: the heaven that the end is the final heaven of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the end: we raise the final heaven, and the final heaven is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the ninth heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final heaven to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the heavens.*
