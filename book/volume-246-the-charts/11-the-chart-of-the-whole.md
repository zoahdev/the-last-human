# Chart 11: The Chart of the Whole

*The eleventh chart is the chart of the whole.*

## The chart

The chart of the whole: the chart that the whole is the one chart of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the whole: we chart the one chart, and the one chart is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the eleventh chart of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one chart to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
