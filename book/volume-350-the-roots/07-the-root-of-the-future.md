# Root 7: The Root of the Future

*The seventh root is the root of the future.*

## The root

The root of the future: the root that the future is the unrooted root of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the future: we root the unrooted root, and the unrooted root is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the seventh root of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unrooted root to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
