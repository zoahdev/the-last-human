# Stair 7: The Stair of the Future

*The seventh stair is the stair of the future.*

## The stair

The stair of the future: the stair that the future is the unwound stair of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the future: we wind the unwound stair, and the unwound stair is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the seventh stair of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unwound stair to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs.*
