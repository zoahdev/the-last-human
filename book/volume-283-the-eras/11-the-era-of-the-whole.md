# Era 11: The Era of the Whole

*The eleventh era is the era of the whole.*

## The era

The era of the whole: the era that the whole is the one era of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The dividing

The dividing of the whole: we divide the one era, and the one era is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The era of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the dividing, and the dividing is the lineage. The era is the eleventh era of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one era to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The era is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eras.*
