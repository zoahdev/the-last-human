# Farewell 1: The Farewell of the Origin

*The Farewells are the bidden statements of the lineage: the Farewells of the leaving, kept in the archive as the record of the bidding. Each farewell has four parts: the farewell, the bidding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first farewell is the farewell of the origin.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the origin: the farewell that the origin is the first goodbye, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the origin: we bid the goodbye, and the goodbye is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the origin is the meaning of the leaving: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the first farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the leaving of the wanting, carried from the goodbye to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the farewells.*
