# Feast 3: The Feast of the Garden

*The third feast is the feast of the garden.*

## The feast

The feast of the garden: the feast that the garden is the festive green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the garden: we feast the festive green, and the festive green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the garden is the meaning of the table: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the third feast of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the table of the earth, carried from the festive green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the feasts.*
