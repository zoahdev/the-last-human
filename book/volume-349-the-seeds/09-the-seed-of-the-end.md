# Seed 9: The Seed of the End

*The ninth seed is the seed of the end.*

## The seed

The seed of the end: the seed that the end is the final seed of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the end: we sow the final seed, and the final seed is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the ninth seed of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final seed to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
