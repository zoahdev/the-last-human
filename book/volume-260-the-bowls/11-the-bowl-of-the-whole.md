# Bowl 11: The Bowl of the Whole

*The eleventh bowl is the bowl of the whole.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the whole: the bowl that the whole is the one bowl of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the whole: we round the one bowl, and the one bowl is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the eleventh bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one bowl to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bowls.*
