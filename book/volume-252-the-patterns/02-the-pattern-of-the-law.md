# Pattern 2: The Pattern of the Law

*The second pattern is the pattern of the law.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the law: the pattern that the law is the patterned care, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the law: we pattern the patterned care, and the patterned care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the law is the meaning of the regularity: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the second pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the regularity of the care, carried from the patterned care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the patterns.*
