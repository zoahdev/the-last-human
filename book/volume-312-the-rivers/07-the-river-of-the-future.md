# River 7: The River of the Future

*The seventh river is the river of the future.*

## The river

The river of the future: the river that the future is the unflowed river of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the future: we flow the unflowed river, and the unflowed river is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the seventh river of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unflowed river to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rivers.*
