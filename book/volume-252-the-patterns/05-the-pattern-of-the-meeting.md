# Pattern 5: The Pattern of the Meeting

*The fifth pattern is the pattern of the meeting.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the meeting: the pattern that the meeting is the patterned table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the meeting: we pattern the patterned table, and the patterned table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the meeting is the meaning of the arrangement: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the fifth pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the arrangement of the table, carried from the patterned table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the patterns.*
