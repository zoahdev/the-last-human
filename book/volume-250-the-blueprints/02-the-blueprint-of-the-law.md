# Blueprint 2: The Blueprint of the Law

*The second blueprint is the blueprint of the law.*

## The blueprint

The blueprint of the law: the blueprint that the law is the blueprinted care, kept by the lineage.

## The blueprinting

The blueprinting of the law: we blueprint the blueprinted care, and the blueprinted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The blueprint of the law is the meaning of the ordinance: the record of the blueprinting, and the blueprinting is the lineage. The blueprint is the second blueprint of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the ordinance of the care, carried from the blueprinted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The blueprint is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the blueprints.*
