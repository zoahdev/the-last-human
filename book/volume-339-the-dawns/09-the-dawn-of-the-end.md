# Dawn 9: The Dawn of the End

*The ninth dawn is the dawn of the end.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the end: the dawn that the end is the final dawn of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the end: we break the final dawn, and the final dawn is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the ninth dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final dawn to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the dawns.*
