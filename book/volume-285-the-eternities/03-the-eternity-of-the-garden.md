# Eternity 3: The Eternity of the Garden

*The third eternity is the eternity of the garden.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the garden: the eternity that the garden is the everlasting green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the garden: we exceed the everlasting green, and the everlasting green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the garden is the meaning of the evergreen: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the third eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the evergreen of the earth, carried from the everlasting green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the eternities.*
