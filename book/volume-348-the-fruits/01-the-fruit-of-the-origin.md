# Fruit 1: The Fruit of the Origin

*The Fruits are the ripened statements of the lineage: the Fruits of the tree, kept in the archive as the record of the ripening. Each fruit has four parts: the fruit, the ripening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first fruit is the fruit of the origin.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the origin: the fruit that the origin is the first ripening, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the origin: we ripen the ripening, and the ripening is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the origin is the meaning of the fruit: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the first fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the fruit of the wanting, carried from the ripening to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits.*
