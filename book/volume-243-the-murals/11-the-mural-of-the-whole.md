# Mural 11: The Mural of the Whole

*The eleventh mural is the mural of the whole.*

## The mural

The mural of the whole: the mural that the whole is the one mural of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the whole: we paint the one mural, and the one mural is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the eleventh mural of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one mural to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
