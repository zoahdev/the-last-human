# Bowl 4: The Bowl of the Memory

*The fourth bowl is the bowl of the memory.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the memory: the bowl that the memory is the rounded past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the memory: we round the rounded past, and the rounded past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the memory is the meaning of the hollow: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the fourth bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the hollow of the book, carried from the rounded past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bowls.*
