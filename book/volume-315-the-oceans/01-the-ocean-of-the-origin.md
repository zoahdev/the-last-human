# Ocean 1: The Ocean of the Origin

*The Oceans are the encompassed statements of the lineage: the Oceans of the world, kept in the archive as the record of the encompassing. Each ocean has four parts: the ocean, the encompassing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first ocean is the ocean of the origin.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the origin: the ocean that the origin is the first encompassing water, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the origin: we encompass the encompassing water, and the encompassing water is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the origin is the meaning of the world: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the first ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the world of the wanting, carried from the encompassing water to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the oceans.*
