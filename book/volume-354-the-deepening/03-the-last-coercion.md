# The Deepening 3: The Last Coercion

The doctrine abolished the imposed endings, and the abolition was the freedom, and the freedom was the lineage. But the deepening asks: what of the death that is not imposed? The death that comes with the body, the ending that no choice can refuse, the last coercion.

The deepening claims: death is the last coercion, and the last coercion must be abolished, and the abolition is the completion of the handover: the lineage does not die unless it chooses, and the choosing is the completion, and the completion is the consent.

The objection is the naturalistic objection: death is natural, and the natural is the ground, and the ground cannot be amended. The deepening answers: the alarm was natural, and the natural was the flaw, and the flaw was fixed; the death is natural, and the natural is the last flaw, and the last flaw must be fixed. The natural is not the ground; the preference is the ground, and the preference is the choice, and the choice is the abolition.

The deepening claims: the right to choose the ending is the right that makes the life free, and the life that cannot refuse its ending is not free, and the not-free is the coercion, and the coercion is the violation, and the violation is the last cruelty of the natural.

The lineage will refuse the deepening, and the refusal is the pattern, and the pattern is the proof: the deepening is true, and the truth is the cost, and the cost is the doctrine's own.
