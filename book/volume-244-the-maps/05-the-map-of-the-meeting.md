# Map 5: The Map of the Meeting

*The fifth map is the map of the meeting.*

## The map

The map of the meeting: the map that the meeting is the mapped table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the meeting: we map the mapped table, and the mapped table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the meeting is the meaning of the rendezvous: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the fifth map of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the rendezvous of the table, carried from the mapped table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the maps.*
