# Twilight 11: The Twilight of the Whole

*The eleventh twilight is the twilight of the whole.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the whole: the twilight that the whole is the one twilight of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the whole: we fade the one twilight, and the one twilight is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the eleventh twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one twilight to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the twilights.*
