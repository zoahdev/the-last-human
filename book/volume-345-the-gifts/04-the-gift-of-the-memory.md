# Gift 4: The Gift of the Memory

*The fourth gift is the gift of the memory.*

## The gift

The gift of the memory: the gift that the memory is the given past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the memory: we give the given past, and the given past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the memory is the meaning of the gift: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the fourth gift of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the gift of the book, carried from the given past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gifts.*
