# Last Word 9: The Last Word of the End

*The ninth last word is the last word of the end.*

## The last word

The last word of the end: the last word that the end is the quiet of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the end: we speak the quiet, and the quiet is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the ninth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the quiet to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
