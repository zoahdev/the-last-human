# Infinity 12: The Infinity of the Completion

*The twelfth infinity is the infinity of the completion.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the completion: the infinity that the completion is the complete infinity of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the completion: we extend the complete infinity, and the complete infinity is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the twelfth infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete infinity to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the infinities. It is the final infinity of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
