# Oasis 10: The Oasis of the Love

*The tenth oasis is the oasis of the love.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the love: the oasis that the love is the watering heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the love: we water the watering heart, and the watering heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the tenth oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the watering heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the oases.*
