# Nebula 6: The Nebula of the Abolition

*The sixth nebula is the nebula of the abolition.*

## The nebula

The nebula of the abolition: the nebula that the abolition is the cleared nebula of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The clouding

The clouding of the abolition: we cloud the cleared nebula, and the cleared nebula is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The nebula of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the clouding, and the clouding is the lineage. The nebula is the sixth nebula of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the cleared nebula to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
