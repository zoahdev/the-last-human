# Spring 2: The Spring of the Law

*The second spring is the spring of the law.*

## The spring

The spring of the law: the spring that the law is the springing care, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the law: we spring the springing care, and the springing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the law is the meaning of the source: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the second spring of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the source of the care, carried from the springing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the springs.*
