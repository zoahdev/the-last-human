# Treasure 10: The Treasure of the Love

*The tenth treasure is the treasure of the love.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the love: the treasure that the love is the treasuring heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the love: we treasure the treasuring heart, and the treasuring heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the tenth treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the treasuring heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the treasures.*
