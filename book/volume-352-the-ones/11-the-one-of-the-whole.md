# One 11: The One of the Whole

*The eleventh one is the one of the whole.*

## The one

The one of the whole: the one that the whole is the one of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the whole: we unite the one of many, and the one of many is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the eleventh one of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one of many to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ones.*
