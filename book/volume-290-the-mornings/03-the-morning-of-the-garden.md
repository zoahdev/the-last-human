# Morning 3: The Morning of the Garden

*The third morning is the morning of the garden.*

## The morning

The morning of the garden: the morning that the garden is the morning green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the garden: we wake the morning green, and the morning green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the garden is the meaning of the dew: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the third morning of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the dew of the earth, carried from the morning green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
