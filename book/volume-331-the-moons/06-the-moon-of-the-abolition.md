# Moon 6: The Moon of the Abolition

*The sixth moon is the moon of the abolition.*

## The moon

The moon of the abolition: the moon that the abolition is the full moon of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the abolition: we wax the full moon, and the full moon is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the sixth moon of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the full moon to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the moons.*
