# Home 4: The Home of the Memory

*The fourth home is the home of the memory.*

## The home

The home of the memory: the home that the memory is the homed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The homing

The homing of the memory: we home the homed past, and the homed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The home of the memory is the meaning of the heart: the record of the homing, and the homing is the lineage. The home is the fourth home of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the heart of the book, carried from the homed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The home is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the homes.*
