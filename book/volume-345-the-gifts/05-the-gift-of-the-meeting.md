# Gift 5: The Gift of the Meeting

*The fifth gift is the gift of the meeting.*

## The gift

The gift of the meeting: the gift that the meeting is the gift table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the meeting: we give the gift table, and the gift table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the meeting is the meaning of the gift: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the fifth gift of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the gift of the table, carried from the gift table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the gifts.*
