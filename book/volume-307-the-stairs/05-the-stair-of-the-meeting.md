# Stair 5: The Stair of the Meeting

*The fifth stair is the stair of the meeting.*

## The stair

The stair of the meeting: the stair that the meeting is the winding table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the meeting: we wind the winding table, and the winding table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the meeting is the meaning of the landing: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the fifth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the landing of the table, carried from the winding table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs.*
