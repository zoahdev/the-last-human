# Mold 3: The Mold of the Garden

*The third mold is the mold of the garden.*

## The mold

The mold of the garden: the mold that the garden is the molded green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the garden: we mold the molded green, and the molded green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the garden is the meaning of the terracotta: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the third mold of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the terracotta of the earth, carried from the molded green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds.*
