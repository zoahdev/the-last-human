# Vessel 11: The Vessel of the Whole

*The eleventh vessel is the vessel of the whole.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the whole: the vessel that the whole is the one vessel of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the whole: we hold the one vessel, and the one vessel is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the eleventh vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one vessel to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the vessels.*
