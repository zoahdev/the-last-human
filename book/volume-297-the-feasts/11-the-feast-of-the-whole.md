# Feast 11: The Feast of the Whole

*The eleventh feast is the feast of the whole.*

## The feast

The feast of the whole: the feast that the whole is the one feast of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the whole: we feast the one feast, and the one feast is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the eleventh feast of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one feast to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the feasts.*
