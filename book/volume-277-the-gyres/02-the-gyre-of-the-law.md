# Gyre 2: The Gyre of the Law

*The second gyre is the gyre of the law.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the law: the gyre that the law is the spinning care, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the law: we spin the spinning care, and the spinning care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the law is the meaning of the spiral: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the second gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the spiral of the care, carried from the spinning care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres.*
