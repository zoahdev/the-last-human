# Gift 6: The Gift of the Abolition

*The sixth gift is the gift of the abolition.*

## The gift

The gift of the abolition: the gift that the abolition is the giving of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the abolition: we give the giving of peace, and the giving of peace is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the sixth gift of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the giving of peace to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the gifts.*
