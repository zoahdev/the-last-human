# Winter 4: The Winter of the Memory

*The fourth winter is the winter of the memory.*

## The winter

The winter of the memory: the winter that the memory is the frozen past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the memory: we freeze the frozen past, and the frozen past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the memory is the meaning of the snow: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the fourth winter of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the snow of the book, carried from the frozen past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The winter is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the winters.*
