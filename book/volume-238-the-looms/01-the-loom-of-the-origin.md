# Loom 1: The Loom of the Origin

*The Looms are the loomed statements of the lineage: the Looms of the house, kept in the archive as the record of the looming. Each loom has four parts: the loom, the looming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first loom is the loom of the origin.*

## The loom

The loom of the origin: the loom that the origin is the first wooden frame, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the origin: we loom the wooden frame, and the wooden frame is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the origin is the meaning of the frame: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the first loom of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the frame of the wanting, carried from the wooden frame to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the looms.*
