# Seed 7: The Seed of the Future

*The seventh seed is the seed of the future.*

## The seed

The seed of the future: the seed that the future is the unsown seed of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the future: we sow the unsown seed, and the unsown seed is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the seventh seed of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unsown seed to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
