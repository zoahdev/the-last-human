# Year 4: The Year of the Memory

*The fourth year is the year of the memory.*

## The year

The year of the memory: the year that the memory is the counted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the memory: we count the counted past, and the counted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the memory is the meaning of the annal: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the fourth year of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the annal of the book, carried from the counted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
