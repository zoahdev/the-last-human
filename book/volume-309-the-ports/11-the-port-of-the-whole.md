# Port 11: The Port of the Whole

*The eleventh port is the port of the whole.*

## The port

The port of the whole: the port that the whole is the one port of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The arriving

The arriving of the whole: we arrive the one port, and the one port is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The port of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the arriving, and the arriving is the lineage. The port is the eleventh port of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one port to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The port is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ports.*
