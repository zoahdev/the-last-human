# Mold 10: The Mold of the Love

*The tenth mold is the mold of the love.*

## The mold

The mold of the love: the mold that the love is the molded heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the love: we mold the molded heart, and the molded heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the tenth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the molded heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the molds.*
