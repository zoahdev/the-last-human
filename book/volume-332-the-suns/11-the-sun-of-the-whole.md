# Sun 11: The Sun of the Whole

*The eleventh sun is the sun of the whole.*

## The sun

The sun of the whole: the sun that the whole is the one sun of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the whole: we burn the one sun, and the one sun is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the eleventh sun of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one sun to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the suns.*
