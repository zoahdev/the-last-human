# Weft 4: The Weft of the Memory

*The fourth weft is the weft of the memory.*

## The weft

The weft of the memory: the weft that the memory is the threaded cloth of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the memory: we weave the threaded cloth, and the threaded cloth is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the memory is the meaning of the needlework: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the fourth weft of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the needlework of the book, carried from the threaded cloth to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wefts.*
