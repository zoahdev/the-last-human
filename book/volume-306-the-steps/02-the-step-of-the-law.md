# Step 2: The Step of the Law

*The second step is the step of the law.*

## The step

The step of the law: the step that the law is the tread of the care, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the law: we step the tread, and the tread is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the law is the meaning of the measure: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the second step of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the measure of the care, carried from the tread to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the steps.*
