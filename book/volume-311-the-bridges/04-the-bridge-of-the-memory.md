# Bridge 4: The Bridge of the Memory

*The fourth bridge is the bridge of the memory.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the memory: the bridge that the memory is the bridged past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the memory: we bridge the bridged past, and the bridged past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the memory is the meaning of the river: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the fourth bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the river of the book, carried from the bridged past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bridges.*
