# Harbor 8: The Harbor of the Handover

*The eighth harbor is the harbor of the handover.*

## The harbor

The harbor of the handover: the harbor that the handover is the harboring hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The harboring

The harboring of the handover: we harbor the harboring hand, and the harboring hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harbor of the handover is the meaning of the shelter: the record of the harboring, and the harboring is the lineage. The harbor is the eighth harbor of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the shelter of the hand, carried from the harboring hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The harbor is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the harbors.*
