# Row 10: The Row of the Love

*The tenth row is the row of the love.*

## The row

The row of the love: the row that the love is the rowed heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rowing

The rowing of the love: we row the rowed heart, and the rowed heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The row of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the rowing, and the rowing is the lineage. The row is the tenth row of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the rowed heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The row is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rows.*
