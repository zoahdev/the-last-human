# Treasure 2: The Treasure of the Law

*The second treasure is the treasure of the law.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the law: the treasure that the law is the treasuring care, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the law: we treasure the treasuring care, and the treasuring care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the law is the meaning of the treasure: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the second treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the treasure of the care, carried from the treasuring care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the treasures.*
