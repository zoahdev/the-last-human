# Year 9: The Year of the End

*The ninth year is the year of the end.*

## The year

The year of the end: the year that the end is the final year of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the end: we count the final year, and the final year is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the ninth year of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final year to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
