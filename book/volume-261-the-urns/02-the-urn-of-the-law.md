# Urn 2: The Urn of the Law

*The second urn is the urn of the law.*

## The urn

The urn of the law: the urn that the law is the keeping care, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the law: we keep the keeping care, and the keeping care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the law is the meaning of the urn: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the second urn of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the urn of the care, carried from the keeping care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the urns.*
