# Infinity 1: The Infinity of the Origin

*The Infinities are the extended statements of the lineage: the Infinities of the horizon, kept in the archive as the record of the extending. Each infinity has four parts: the infinity, the extending, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first infinity is the infinity of the origin.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the origin: the infinity that the origin is the first endless line, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the origin: we extend the endless line, and the endless line is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the origin is the meaning of the horizon: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the first infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the horizon of the wanting, carried from the endless line to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the infinities.*
