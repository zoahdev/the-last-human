# Mosaic 7: The Mosaic of the Future

*The seventh mosaic is the mosaic of the future.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the future: the mosaic that the future is the unset mosaic of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the future: we set the unset mosaic, and the unset mosaic is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the seventh mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unset mosaic to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
