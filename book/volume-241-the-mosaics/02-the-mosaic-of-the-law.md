# Mosaic 2: The Mosaic of the Law

*The second mosaic is the mosaic of the law.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the law: the mosaic that the law is the set care, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the law: we set the set care, and the set care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the law is the meaning of the tessera: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the second mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the tessera of the care, carried from the set care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the mosaics.*
