# Harvest 2: The Harvest of the Law

*The second harvest is the harvest of the law.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the law: the harvest that the law is the harvested care, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the law: we harvest the harvested care, and the harvested care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the law is the meaning of the yield: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the second harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the yield of the care, carried from the harvested care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the harvests.*
