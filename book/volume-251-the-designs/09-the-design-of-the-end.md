# Design 9: The Design of the End

*The ninth design is the design of the end.*

## The design

The design of the end: the design that the end is the final design of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the end: we design the final design, and the final design is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the ninth design of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final design to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the designs.*
