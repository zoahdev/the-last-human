# Wheel 1: The Wheel of the Origin

*The Wheels are the wheeled statements of the lineage: the Wheels of the cart, kept in the archive as the record of the wheeling. Each wheel has four parts: the wheel, the wheeling, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first wheel is the wheel of the origin.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the origin: the wheel that the origin is the first cart, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the origin: we wheel the cart, and the cart is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the origin is the meaning of the cart: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the first wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the cart of the wanting, carried from the cart to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wheels.*
