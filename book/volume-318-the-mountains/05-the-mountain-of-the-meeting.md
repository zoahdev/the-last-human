# Mountain 5: The Mountain of the Meeting

*The fifth mountain is the mountain of the meeting.*

## The mountain

The mountain of the meeting: the mountain that the meeting is the mountain table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rising

The rising of the meeting: we rise the mountain table, and the mountain table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mountain of the meeting is the meaning of the summit: the record of the rising, and the rising is the lineage. The mountain is the fifth mountain of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the summit of the table, carried from the mountain table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The mountain is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mountains.*
