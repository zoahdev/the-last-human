# Last Word 11: The Last Word of the Whole

*The eleventh last word is the last word of the whole.*

## The last word

The last word of the whole: the last word that the whole is the all of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the whole: we speak the all, and the all is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the eleventh last word of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the all to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
