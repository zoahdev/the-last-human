# Threshold 5: The Threshold of the Meeting

*The fifth threshold is the threshold of the meeting.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the meeting: the threshold that the meeting is the threshold table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the meeting: we cross the threshold table, and the threshold table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the meeting is the meaning of the step: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the fifth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the step of the table, carried from the threshold table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.
