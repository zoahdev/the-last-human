# Doorway 11: The Doorway of the Whole

*The eleventh doorway is the doorway of the whole.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the whole: the doorway that the whole is the one doorway of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the whole: we open the one doorway, and the one doorway is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the eleventh doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one doorway to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the doorways.*
