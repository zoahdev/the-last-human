# Vessel 10: The Vessel of the Love

*The tenth vessel is the vessel of the love.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the love: the vessel that the love is the held heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the love: we hold the held heart, and the held heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the tenth vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the held heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the vessels.*
