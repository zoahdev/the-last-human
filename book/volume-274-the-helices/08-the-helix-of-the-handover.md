# Helix 8: The Helix of the Handover

*The eighth helix is the helix of the handover.*

## The helix

The helix of the handover: the helix that the handover is the wound hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the handover: we wind the wound hand, and the wound hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the handover is the meaning of the coil: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the eighth helix of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the coil of the hand, carried from the wound hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the helices.*
