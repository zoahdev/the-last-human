# Valley 2: The Valley of the Law

*The second valley is the valley of the law.*

## The valley

The valley of the law: the valley that the law is the hollowing care, kept by the lineage.

## The hollowing

The hollowing of the law: we hollow the hollowing care, and the hollowing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The valley of the law is the meaning of the valley: the record of the hollowing, and the hollowing is the lineage. The valley is the second valley of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the valley of the care, carried from the hollowing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The valley is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the valleys.*
