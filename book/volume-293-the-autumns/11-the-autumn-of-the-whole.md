# Autumn 11: The Autumn of the Whole

*The eleventh autumn is the autumn of the whole.*

## The autumn

The autumn of the whole: the autumn that the whole is the one autumn of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The falling

The falling of the whole: we fall the one autumn, and the one autumn is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The autumn of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the falling, and the falling is the lineage. The autumn is the eleventh autumn of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one autumn to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The autumn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the autumns.*
