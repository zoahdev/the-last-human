# Aeon 7: The Aeon of the Future

*The seventh aeon is the aeon of the future.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the future: the aeon that the future is the unspanned aeon of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the future: we span the unspanned aeon, and the unspanned aeon is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the seventh aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unspanned aeon to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the aeons.*
