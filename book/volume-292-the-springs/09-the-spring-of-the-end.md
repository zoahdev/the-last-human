# Spring 9: The Spring of the End

*The ninth spring is the spring of the end.*

## The spring

The spring of the end: the spring that the end is the final spring of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the end: we spring the final spring, and the final spring is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the ninth spring of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final spring to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the springs.*
