# Helix 3: The Helix of the Garden

*The third helix is the helix of the garden.*

## The helix

The helix of the garden: the helix that the garden is the wound green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the garden: we wind the wound green, and the wound green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the garden is the meaning of the twining: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the third helix of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the twining of the earth, carried from the wound green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
