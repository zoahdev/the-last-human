# Chart 4: The Chart of the Memory

*The fourth chart is the chart of the memory.*

## The chart

The chart of the memory: the chart that the memory is the charted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the memory: we chart the charted past, and the charted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the memory is the meaning of the log: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the fourth chart of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the log of the book, carried from the charted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the charts.*
