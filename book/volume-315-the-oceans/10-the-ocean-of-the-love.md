# Ocean 10: The Ocean of the Love

*The tenth ocean is the ocean of the love.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the love: the ocean that the love is the encompassing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the love: we encompass the encompassing heart, and the encompassing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the tenth ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the encompassing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oceans.*
