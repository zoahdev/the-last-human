# Fruit 11: The Fruit of the Whole

*The eleventh fruit is the fruit of the whole.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the whole: the fruit that the whole is the one fruit of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the whole: we ripen the one fruit, and the one fruit is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the eleventh fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one fruit to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the fruits.*
