# Cabinet 3: The Cabinet of the Garden

*The third cabinet is the cabinet of the garden.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the garden: the cabinet that the garden is the arranged green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the garden: we arrange the arranged green, and the arranged green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the garden is the meaning of the herbarium: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the third cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the herbarium of the earth, carried from the arranged green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The cabinet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cabinets.*
