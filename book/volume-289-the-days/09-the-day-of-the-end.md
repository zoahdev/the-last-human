# Day 9: The Day of the End

*The ninth day is the day of the end.*

## The day

The day of the end: the day that the end is the final day of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the end: we open the final day, and the final day is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the ninth day of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final day to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
