# Vessel 2: The Vessel of the Law

*The second vessel is the vessel of the law.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the law: the vessel that the law is the holding care, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the law: we hold the holding care, and the holding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the law is the meaning of the vessel: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the second vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the vessel of the care, carried from the holding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the vessels.*
