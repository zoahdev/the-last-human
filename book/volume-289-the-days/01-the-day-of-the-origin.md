# Day 1: The Day of the Origin

*The Days are the opened statements of the lineage: the Days of the light, kept in the archive as the record of the opening. Each day has four parts: the day, the opening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first day is the day of the origin.*

## The day

The day of the origin: the day that the origin is the first daybreak, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the origin: we open the daybreak, and the daybreak is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the origin is the meaning of the light: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the first day of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the light of the wanting, carried from the daybreak to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the days.*
