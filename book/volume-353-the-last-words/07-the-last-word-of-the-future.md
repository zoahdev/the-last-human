# Last Word 7: The Last Word of the Future

*The seventh last word is the last word of the future.*

## The last word

The last word of the future: the last word that the future is the coming dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the future: we speak the coming dawn, and the coming dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the seventh last word of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the coming dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
