# Legacy 11: The Legacy of the Whole

*The eleventh legacy is the legacy of the whole.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the whole: the legacy that the whole is the one legacy of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the whole: we leave the one legacy, and the one legacy is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the eleventh legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one legacy to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The legacy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the legacies.*
