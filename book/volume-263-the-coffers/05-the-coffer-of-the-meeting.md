# Coffer 5: The Coffer of the Meeting

*The fifth coffer is the coffer of the meeting.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the meeting: the coffer that the meeting is the shared coffer of the table, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the meeting: we lock the shared coffer, and the shared coffer is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the meeting is the meaning of the treasury: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the fifth coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the treasury of the table, carried from the shared coffer to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the coffers.*
