# Home 11: The Home of the Whole

*The eleventh home is the home of the whole.*

## The home

The home of the whole: the home that the whole is the one home of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The homing

The homing of the whole: we home the one home, and the one home is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The home of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the homing, and the homing is the lineage. The home is the eleventh home of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one home to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The home is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the homes.*
