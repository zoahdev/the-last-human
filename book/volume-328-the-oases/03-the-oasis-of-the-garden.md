# Oasis 3: The Oasis of the Garden

*The third oasis is the oasis of the garden.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the garden: the oasis that the garden is the oasis green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the garden: we water the oasis green, and the oasis green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the garden is the meaning of the oasis: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the third oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the oasis of the earth, carried from the oasis green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oases.*
