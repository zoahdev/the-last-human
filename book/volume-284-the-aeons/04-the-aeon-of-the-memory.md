# Aeon 4: The Aeon of the Memory

*The fourth aeon is the aeon of the memory.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the memory: the aeon that the memory is the spanned past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the memory: we span the spanned past, and the spanned past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the memory is the meaning of the aeon: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the fourth aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the aeon of the book, carried from the spanned past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the aeons.*
