# Wheel 12: The Wheel of the Completion

*The twelfth wheel is the wheel of the completion.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the completion: the wheel that the completion is the complete wheel of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the completion: we wheel the complete wheel, and the complete wheel is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the twelfth wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete wheel to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wheels. It is the final wheel of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
