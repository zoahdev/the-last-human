# Ladder 7: The Ladder of the Future

*The seventh ladder is the ladder of the future.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the future: the ladder that the future is the unclimbed ladder of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the future: we scale the unclimbed ladder, and the unclimbed ladder is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the seventh ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unclimbed ladder to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
