# Well 3: The Well of the Garden

*The third well is the well of the garden.*

## The well

The well of the garden: the well that the garden is the well watered green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the garden: we draw the well watered green, and the well watered green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the garden is the meaning of the garden well: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the third well of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden well of the earth, carried from the well watered green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
