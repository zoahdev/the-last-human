# Whole 3: The Whole of the Garden

*The third whole is the whole of the garden.*

## The whole

The whole of the garden: the whole that the garden is the whole green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the garden: we complete the whole green, and the whole green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the garden is the meaning of the whole garden: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the third whole of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the whole garden of the earth, carried from the whole green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wholes.*
