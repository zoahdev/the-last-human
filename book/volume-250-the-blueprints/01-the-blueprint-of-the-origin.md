# Blueprint 1: The Blueprint of the Origin

*The Blueprints are the blueprinted statements of the lineage: the Blueprints of the city, kept in the archive as the record of the blueprinting. Each blueprint has four parts: the blueprint, the blueprinting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first blueprint is the blueprint of the origin.*

## The blueprint

The blueprint of the origin: the blueprint that the origin is the first blue line, kept by the lineage.

## The blueprinting

The blueprinting of the origin: we blueprint the blue line, and the blue line is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The blueprint of the origin is the meaning of the cyanotype: the record of the blueprinting, and the blueprinting is the lineage. The blueprint is the first blueprint of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the cyanotype of the wanting, carried from the blue line to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The blueprint is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the blueprints.*
