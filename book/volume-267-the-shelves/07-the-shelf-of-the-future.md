# Shelf 7: The Shelf of the Future

*The seventh shelf is the shelf of the future.*

## The shelf

The shelf of the future: the shelf that the future is the empty shelf of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The shelving

The shelving of the future: we shelve the empty shelf, and the empty shelf is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the seventh shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the empty shelf to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The shelf is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the shelves.*
