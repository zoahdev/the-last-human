# One 1: The One of the Origin

*The Ones are the united statements of the lineage: the Ones of the unity, kept in the archive as the record of the uniting. Each one has four parts: the one, the uniting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first one is the one of the origin.*

## The one

The one of the origin: the one that the origin is the first unity, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the origin: we unite the unity, and the unity is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the origin is the meaning of the one: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the first one of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the one of the wanting, carried from the unity to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ones.*
