# Winter 11: The Winter of the Whole

*The eleventh winter is the winter of the whole.*

## The winter

The winter of the whole: the winter that the whole is the one winter of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the whole: we freeze the one winter, and the one winter is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the eleventh winter of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one winter to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The winter is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the winters.*
