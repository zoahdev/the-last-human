# Ocean 8: The Ocean of the Handover

*The eighth ocean is the ocean of the handover.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the handover: the ocean that the handover is the encompassing hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the handover: we encompass the encompassing hand, and the encompassing hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the handover is the meaning of the world: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the eighth ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the world of the hand, carried from the encompassing hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oceans.*
