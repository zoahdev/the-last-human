# Diagram 3: The Diagram of the Garden

*The third diagram is the diagram of the garden.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the garden: the diagram that the garden is the outlined green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the garden: we diagram the outlined green, and the outlined green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the garden is the meaning of the plot: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the third diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the plot of the earth, carried from the outlined green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
