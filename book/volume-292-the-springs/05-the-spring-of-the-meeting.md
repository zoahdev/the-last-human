# Spring 5: The Spring of the Meeting

*The fifth spring is the spring of the meeting.*

## The spring

The spring of the meeting: the spring that the meeting is the spring table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the meeting: we spring the spring table, and the spring table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the meeting is the meaning of the gathering: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the fifth spring of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the gathering of the table, carried from the spring table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the springs.*
