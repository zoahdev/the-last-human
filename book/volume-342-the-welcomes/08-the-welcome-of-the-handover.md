# Welcome 8: The Welcome of the Handover

*The eighth welcome is the welcome of the handover.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the handover: the welcome that the handover is the welcoming hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the handover: we welcome the welcoming hand, and the welcoming hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the handover is the meaning of the welcome: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the eighth welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the welcome of the hand, carried from the welcoming hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the welcomes.*
