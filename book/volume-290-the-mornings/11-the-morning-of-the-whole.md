# Morning 11: The Morning of the Whole

*The eleventh morning is the morning of the whole.*

## The morning

The morning of the whole: the morning that the whole is the one morning of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the whole: we wake the one morning, and the one morning is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the eleventh morning of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one morning to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
