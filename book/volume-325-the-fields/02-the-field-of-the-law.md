# Field 2: The Field of the Law

*The second field is the field of the law.*

## The field

The field of the law: the field that the law is the tilled care, kept by the lineage.

## The tilling

The tilling of the law: we till the tilled care, and the tilled care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The field of the law is the meaning of the field: the record of the tilling, and the tilling is the lineage. The field is the second field of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the field of the care, carried from the tilled care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The field is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fields.*
