# Nebula 10: The Nebula of the Love

*The tenth nebula is the nebula of the love.*

## The nebula

The nebula of the love: the nebula that the love is the clouding heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The clouding

The clouding of the love: we cloud the clouding heart, and the clouding heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The nebula of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the clouding, and the clouding is the lineage. The nebula is the tenth nebula of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the clouding heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The nebula is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nebulae.*
