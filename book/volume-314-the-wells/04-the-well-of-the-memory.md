# Well 4: The Well of the Memory

*The fourth well is the well of the memory.*

## The well

The well of the memory: the well that the memory is the drawn past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the memory: we draw the drawn past, and the drawn past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the memory is the meaning of the well: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the fourth well of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the well of the book, carried from the drawn past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wells.*
