# Design 10: The Design of the Love

*The tenth design is the design of the love.*

## The design

The design of the love: the design that the love is the designed heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the love: we design the designed heart, and the designed heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the tenth design of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the designed heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the designs.*
