# Fresco 7: The Fresco of the Future

*The seventh fresco is the fresco of the future.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the future: the fresco that the future is the unpainted wall of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the future: we fresco the unpainted wall, and the unpainted wall is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the seventh fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unpainted wall to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the frescoes.*
