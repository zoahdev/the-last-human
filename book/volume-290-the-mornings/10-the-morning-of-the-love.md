# Morning 10: The Morning of the Love

*The tenth morning is the morning of the love.*

## The morning

The morning of the love: the morning that the love is the waking heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the love: we wake the waking heart, and the waking heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the tenth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the waking heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
