# Sky 4: The Sky of the Memory

*The fourth sky is the sky of the memory.*

## The sky

The sky of the memory: the sky that the memory is the arched past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the memory: we arch the arched past, and the arched past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the memory is the meaning of the blue: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the fourth sky of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the blue of the book, carried from the arched past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the skies.*
