# Desert 10: The Desert of the Love

*The tenth desert is the desert of the love.*

## The desert

The desert of the love: the desert that the love is the watering heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drying

The drying of the love: we dry the watering heart, and the watering heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The desert of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the drying, and the drying is the lineage. The desert is the tenth desert of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the watering heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The desert is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the deserts.*
