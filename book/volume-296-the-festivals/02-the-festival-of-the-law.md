# Festival 2: The Festival of the Law

*The second festival is the festival of the law.*

## The festival

The festival of the law: the festival that the law is the celebrated care, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the law: we celebrate the celebrated care, and the celebrated care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the law is the meaning of the holiday: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the second festival of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the holiday of the care, carried from the celebrated care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the festivals.*
