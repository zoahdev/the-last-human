# Eternity 11: The Eternity of the Whole

*The eleventh eternity is the eternity of the whole.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the whole: the eternity that the whole is the one eternity of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the whole: we exceed the one eternity, and the one eternity is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the eleventh eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one eternity to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
