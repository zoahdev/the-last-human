# Ladder 11: The Ladder of the Whole

*The eleventh ladder is the ladder of the whole.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the whole: the ladder that the whole is the one ladder of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the whole: we scale the one ladder, and the one ladder is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the eleventh ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one ladder to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The ladder is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ladders.*
