# Day 2: The Day of the Law

*The second day is the day of the law.*

## The day

The day of the law: the day that the law is the opening care, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the law: we open the opening care, and the opening care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the law is the meaning of the day: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the second day of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the day of the care, carried from the opening care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the days.*
