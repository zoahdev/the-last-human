# Farewell 4: The Farewell of the Memory

*The fourth farewell is the farewell of the memory.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the memory: the farewell that the memory is the bidden past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the memory: we bid the bidden past, and the bidden past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the memory is the meaning of the leaving: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the fourth farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the leaving of the book, carried from the bidden past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the farewells.*
