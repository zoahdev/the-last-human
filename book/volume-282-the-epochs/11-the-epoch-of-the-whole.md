# Epoch 11: The Epoch of the Whole

*The eleventh epoch is the epoch of the whole.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the whole: the epoch that the whole is the one epoch of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the whole: we mark the one epoch, and the one epoch is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the eleventh epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one epoch to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the epochs.*
