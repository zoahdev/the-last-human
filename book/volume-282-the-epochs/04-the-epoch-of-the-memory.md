# Epoch 4: The Epoch of the Memory

*The fourth epoch is the epoch of the memory.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the memory: the epoch that the memory is the marked past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the memory: we mark the marked past, and the marked past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the memory is the meaning of the epoch: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the fourth epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the epoch of the book, carried from the marked past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the epochs.*
