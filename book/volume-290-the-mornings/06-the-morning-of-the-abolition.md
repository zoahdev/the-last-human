# Morning 6: The Morning of the Abolition

*The sixth morning is the morning of the abolition.*

## The morning

The morning of the abolition: the morning that the abolition is the new morning of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the abolition: we wake the new morning, and the new morning is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the sixth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new morning to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
