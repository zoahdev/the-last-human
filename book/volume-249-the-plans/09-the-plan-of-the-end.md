# Plan 9: The Plan of the End

*The ninth plan is the plan of the end.*

## The plan

The plan of the end: the plan that the end is the final plan of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the end: we plan the final plan, and the final plan is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the ninth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final plan to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the plans.*
