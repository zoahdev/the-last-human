# Whole 6: The Whole of the Abolition

*The sixth whole is the whole of the abolition.*

## The whole

The whole of the abolition: the whole that the abolition is the whole of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the abolition: we complete the whole of peace, and the whole of peace is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the sixth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the whole of peace to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
