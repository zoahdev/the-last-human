# Cave 7: The Cave of the Future

*The seventh cave is the cave of the future.*

## The cave

The cave of the future: the cave that the future is the unopened cave of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the future: we shelter the unopened cave, and the unopened cave is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the seventh cave of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unopened cave to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
