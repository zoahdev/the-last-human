# Cup 5: The Cup of the Meeting

*The fifth cup is the cup of the meeting.*

## The cup

The cup of the meeting: the cup that the meeting is the shared cup of the table, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the meeting: we cup the shared cup, and the shared cup is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the meeting is the meaning of the cup: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the fifth cup of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the cup of the table, carried from the shared cup to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
