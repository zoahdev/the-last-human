# Nebula 2: The Nebula of the Law

*The second nebula is the nebula of the law.*

## The nebula

The nebula of the law: the nebula that the law is the clouding care, kept by the lineage.

## The clouding

The clouding of the law: we cloud the clouding care, and the clouding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The nebula of the law is the meaning of the cloud: the record of the clouding, and the clouding is the lineage. The nebula is the second nebula of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the cloud of the care, carried from the clouding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The nebula is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nebulae.*
