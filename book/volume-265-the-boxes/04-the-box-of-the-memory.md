# Box 4: The Box of the Memory

*The fourth box is the box of the memory.*

## The box

The box of the memory: the box that the memory is the boxed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the memory: we box the boxed past, and the boxed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the memory is the meaning of the letters: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the fourth box of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the letters of the book, carried from the boxed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the boxes.*
