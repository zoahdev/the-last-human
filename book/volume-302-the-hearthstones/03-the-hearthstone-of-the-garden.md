# Hearthstone 3: The Hearthstone of the Garden

*The third hearthstone is the hearthstone of the garden.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the garden: the hearthstone that the garden is the warm green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the garden: we warm the warm green, and the warm green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the garden is the meaning of the garden fire: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the third hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden fire of the earth, carried from the warm green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
