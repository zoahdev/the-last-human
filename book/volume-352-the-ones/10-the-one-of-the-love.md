# One 10: The One of the Love

*The tenth one is the one of the love.*

## The one

The one of the love: the one that the love is the uniting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the love: we unite the uniting heart, and the uniting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the tenth one of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the uniting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ones.*
