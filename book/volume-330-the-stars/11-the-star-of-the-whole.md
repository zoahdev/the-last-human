# Star 11: The Star of the Whole

*The eleventh star is the star of the whole.*

## The star

The star of the whole: the star that the whole is the one star of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The shining

The shining of the whole: we shine the one star, and the one star is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The star of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the shining, and the shining is the lineage. The star is the eleventh star of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one star to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The star is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stars.*
