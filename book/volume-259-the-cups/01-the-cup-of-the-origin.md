# Cup 1: The Cup of the Origin

*The Cups are the cupped statements of the lineage: the Cups of the hand, kept in the archive as the record of the cupping. Each cup has four parts: the cup, the cupping, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first cup is the cup of the origin.*

## The cup

The cup of the origin: the cup that the origin is the first cupped hand, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the origin: we cup the cupped hand, and the cupped hand is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the origin is the meaning of the hand: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the first cup of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the hand of the wanting, carried from the cupped hand to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
