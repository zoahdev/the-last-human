# Atlas 11: The Atlas of the Whole

*The eleventh atlas is the atlas of the whole.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the whole: the atlas that the whole is the one atlas of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the whole: we carry the one atlas, and the one atlas is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the eleventh atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one atlas to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
