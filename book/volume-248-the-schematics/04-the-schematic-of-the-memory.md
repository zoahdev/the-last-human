# Schematic 4: The Schematic of the Memory

*The fourth schematic is the schematic of the memory.*

## The schematic

The schematic of the memory: the schematic that the memory is the drawn past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the memory: we draw the drawn past, and the drawn past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The schematic of the memory is the meaning of the blueprint: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The schematic is the fourth schematic of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the blueprint of the book, carried from the drawn past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
