# Gyre 12: The Gyre of the Completion

*The twelfth gyre is the gyre of the completion.*

## The gyre

The gyre of the completion: the gyre that the completion is the complete gyre of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The spinning

The spinning of the completion: we spin the complete gyre, and the complete gyre is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gyre of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the spinning, and the spinning is the lineage. The gyre is the twelfth gyre of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete gyre to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The gyre is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gyres. It is the final gyre of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
