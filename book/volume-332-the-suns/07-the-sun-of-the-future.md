# Sun 7: The Sun of the Future

*The seventh sun is the sun of the future.*

## The sun

The sun of the future: the sun that the future is the unrisen sun of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the future: we burn the unrisen sun, and the unrisen sun is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the seventh sun of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unrisen sun to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the suns.*
