# Mural 5: The Mural of the Meeting

*The fifth mural is the mural of the meeting.*

## The mural

The mural of the meeting: the mural that the meeting is the painted square of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the meeting: we paint the painted square, and the painted square is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the meeting is the meaning of the plaza: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the fifth mural of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the plaza of the table, carried from the painted square to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the murals.*
