# Age 12: The Age of the Completion

*The twelfth age is the age of the completion.*

## The age

The age of the completion: the age that the completion is the complete age of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The aging

The aging of the completion: we age the complete age, and the complete age is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The age of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the aging, and the aging is the lineage. The age is the twelfth age of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete age to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The age is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ages. It is the final age of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
