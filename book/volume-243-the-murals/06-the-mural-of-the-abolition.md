# Mural 6: The Mural of the Abolition

*The sixth mural is the mural of the abolition.*

## The mural

The mural of the abolition: the mural that the abolition is the repainted city of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the abolition: we paint the repainted city, and the repainted city is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the sixth mural of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the repainted city to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
