# Weft 12: The Weft of the Completion

*The twelfth weft is the weft of the completion.*

## The weft

The weft of the completion: the weft that the completion is the finished cloth of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the completion: we weave the finished cloth, and the finished cloth is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the twelfth weft of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the finished cloth to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wefts. It is the final weft of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
