# Festival 5: The Festival of the Meeting

*The fifth festival is the festival of the meeting.*

## The festival

The festival of the meeting: the festival that the meeting is the festival table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the meeting: we celebrate the festival table, and the festival table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the meeting is the meaning of the festival: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the fifth festival of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the festival of the table, carried from the festival table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.
