# Loom 9: The Loom of the End

*The ninth loom is the loom of the end.*

## The loom

The loom of the end: the loom that the end is the final loom of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the end: we loom the final loom, and the final loom is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the ninth loom of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final loom to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the looms.*
