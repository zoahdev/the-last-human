# Sky 11: The Sky of the Whole

*The eleventh sky is the sky of the whole.*

## The sky

The sky of the whole: the sky that the whole is the one sky of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the whole: we arch the one sky, and the one sky is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the eleventh sky of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one sky to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the skies.*
