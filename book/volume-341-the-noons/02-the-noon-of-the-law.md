# Noon 2: The Noon of the Law

*The second noon is the noon of the law.*

## The noon

The noon of the law: the noon that the law is the standing care, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the law: we stand the standing care, and the standing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the law is the meaning of the noon: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the second noon of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the noon of the care, carried from the standing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the noons.*
