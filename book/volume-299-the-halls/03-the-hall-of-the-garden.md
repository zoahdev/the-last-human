# Hall 3: The Hall of the Garden

*The third hall is the hall of the garden.*

## The hall

The hall of the garden: the hall that the garden is the welcoming green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the garden: we welcome the welcoming green, and the welcoming green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hall of the garden is the meaning of the garden hall: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The hall is the third hall of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden hall of the earth, carried from the welcoming green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
