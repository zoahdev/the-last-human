# Lake 11: The Lake of the Whole

*The eleventh lake is the lake of the whole.*

## The lake

The lake of the whole: the lake that the whole is the one lake of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The mirroring

The mirroring of the whole: we mirror the one lake, and the one lake is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The lake of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the mirroring, and the mirroring is the lineage. The lake is the eleventh lake of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one lake to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The lake is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the lakes.*
