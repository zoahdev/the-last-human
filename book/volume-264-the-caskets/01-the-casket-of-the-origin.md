# Casket 1: The Casket of the Origin

*The Caskets are the shut statements of the lineage: the Caskets of the jewels, kept in the archive as the record of the shutting. Each casket has four parts: the casket, the shutting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first casket is the casket of the origin.*

## The casket

The casket of the origin: the casket that the origin is the first jewel box, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the origin: we shut the jewel box, and the jewel box is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the origin is the meaning of the jewel: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the first casket of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the jewel of the wanting, carried from the jewel box to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caskets.*
