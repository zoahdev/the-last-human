# Doorway 1: The Doorway of the Origin

*The Doorways are the opened statements of the lineage: the Doorways of the door, kept in the archive as the record of the opening. Each doorway has four parts: the doorway, the opening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first doorway is the doorway of the origin.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the origin: the doorway that the origin is the first arch, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the origin: we open the arch, and the arch is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the origin is the meaning of the door: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the first doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the door of the wanting, carried from the arch to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the doorways.*
