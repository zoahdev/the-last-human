# Whole 12: The Whole of the Completion

*The twelfth whole is the whole of the completion.*

## The whole

The whole of the completion: the whole that the completion is the complete whole of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the completion: we complete the complete whole, and the complete whole is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the twelfth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete whole to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wholes. It is the final whole of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
