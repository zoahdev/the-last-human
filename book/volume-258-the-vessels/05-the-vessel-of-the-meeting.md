# Vessel 5: The Vessel of the Meeting

*The fifth vessel is the vessel of the meeting.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the meeting: the vessel that the meeting is the shared cup of the table, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the meeting: we hold the shared cup, and the shared cup is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the meeting is the meaning of the cup: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the fifth vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the cup of the table, carried from the shared cup to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the vessels.*
