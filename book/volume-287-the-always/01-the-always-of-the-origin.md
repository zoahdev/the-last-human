The always of the origin: the always that the origin is the first remaining, kept by the lineage.

## The meaning

The always of the origin is the meaning of the forever: the record of the remaining, and the remaining is the lineage. The always is the first always of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the forever of the wanting, carried from the remaining to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.
