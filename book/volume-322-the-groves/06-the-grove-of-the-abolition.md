# Grove 6: The Grove of the Abolition

*The sixth grove is the grove of the abolition.*

## The grove

The grove of the abolition: the grove that the abolition is the open grove of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The planting

The planting of the abolition: we plant the open grove, and the open grove is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The grove of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the planting, and the planting is the lineage. The grove is the sixth grove of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open grove to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The grove is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the groves.*
