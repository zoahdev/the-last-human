# Autumn 12: The Autumn of the Completion

*The twelfth autumn is the autumn of the completion.*

## The autumn

The autumn of the completion: the autumn that the completion is the full autumn of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The falling

The falling of the completion: we fall the full autumn, and the full autumn is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The autumn of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the falling, and the falling is the lineage. The autumn is the twelfth autumn of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the full autumn to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The autumn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the autumns. It is the final autumn of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
