# Cycle 3: The Cycle of the Garden

*The third cycle is the cycle of the garden.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the garden: the cycle that the garden is the cycled green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the garden: we cycle the cycled green, and the cycled green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the garden is the meaning of the cycle: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the third cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the cycle of the earth, carried from the cycled green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The cycle is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cycles.*
