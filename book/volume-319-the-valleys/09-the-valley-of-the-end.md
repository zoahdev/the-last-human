# Valley 9: The Valley of the End

*The ninth valley is the valley of the end.*

## The valley

The valley of the end: the valley that the end is the final valley of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The hollowing

The hollowing of the end: we hollow the final valley, and the final valley is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The valley of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the hollowing, and the hollowing is the lineage. The valley is the ninth valley of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final valley to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The valley is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the valleys.*
