# Matrix 2: The Matrix of the Law

*The second matrix is the matrix of the law.*

## The matrix

The matrix of the law: the matrix that the law is the pressed care, kept by the lineage.

## The pressing

The pressing of the law: we press the pressed care, and the pressed care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The matrix of the law is the meaning of the stamp: the record of the pressing, and the pressing is the lineage. The matrix is the second matrix of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the stamp of the care, carried from the pressed care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
