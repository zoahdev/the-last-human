# Harbor 2: The Harbor of the Law

*The second harbor is the harbor of the law.*

## The harbor

The harbor of the law: the harbor that the law is the harboring care, kept by the lineage.

## The harboring

The harboring of the law: we harbor the harboring care, and the harboring care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harbor of the law is the meaning of the shelter: the record of the harboring, and the harboring is the lineage. The harbor is the second harbor of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the shelter of the care, carried from the harboring care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
