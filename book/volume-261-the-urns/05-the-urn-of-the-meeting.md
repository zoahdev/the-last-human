# Urn 5: The Urn of the Meeting

*The fifth urn is the urn of the meeting.*

## The urn

The urn of the meeting: the urn that the meeting is the kept table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the meeting: we keep the kept table, and the kept table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the meeting is the meaning of the keepsake: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the fifth urn of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the keepsake of the table, carried from the kept table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the urns.*
