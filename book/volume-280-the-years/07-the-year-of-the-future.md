# Year 7: The Year of the Future

*The seventh year is the year of the future.*

## The year

The year of the future: the year that the future is the unnumbered year of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the future: we count the unnumbered year, and the unnumbered year is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the seventh year of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unnumbered year to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
