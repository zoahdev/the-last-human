# Year 3: The Year of the Garden

*The third year is the year of the garden.*

## The year

The year of the garden: the year that the garden is the counted green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the garden: we count the counted green, and the counted green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the garden is the meaning of the harvest year: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the third year of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the harvest year of the earth, carried from the counted green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the years.*
