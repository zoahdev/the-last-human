# Doorway 9: The Doorway of the End

*The ninth doorway is the doorway of the end.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the end: the doorway that the end is the final doorway of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the end: we open the final doorway, and the final doorway is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the ninth doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final doorway to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the doorways.*
