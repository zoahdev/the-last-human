# Window 11: The Window of the Whole

*The eleventh window is the window of the whole.*

## The window

The window of the whole: the window that the whole is the one window of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the whole: we open the one window, and the one window is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the eleventh window of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one window to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the windows.*
