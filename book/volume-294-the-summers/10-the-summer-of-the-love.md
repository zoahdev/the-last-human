# Summer 10: The Summer of the Love

*The tenth summer is the summer of the love.*

## The summer

The summer of the love: the summer that the love is the warm heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the love: we ripen the warm heart, and the warm heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the tenth summer of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the warm heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
