# Mold 2: The Mold of the Law

*The second mold is the mold of the law.*

## The mold

The mold of the law: the mold that the law is the molded care, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the law: we mold the molded care, and the molded care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the law is the meaning of the potter: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the second mold of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the potter of the care, carried from the molded care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the molds.*
