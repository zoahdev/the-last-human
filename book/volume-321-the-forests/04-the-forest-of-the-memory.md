# Forest 4: The Forest of the Memory

*The fourth forest is the forest of the memory.*

## The forest

The forest of the memory: the forest that the memory is the grown past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The growing

The growing of the memory: we grow the grown past, and the grown past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The forest of the memory is the meaning of the forest: the record of the growing, and the growing is the lineage. The forest is the fourth forest of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the forest of the book, carried from the grown past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
