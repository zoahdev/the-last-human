# Tide 9: The Tide of the End

*The ninth tide is the tide of the end.*

## The tide

The tide of the end: the tide that the end is the final tide of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the end: we tide the final tide, and the final tide is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the ninth tide of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final tide to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tides.*
