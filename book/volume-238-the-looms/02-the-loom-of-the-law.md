# Loom 2: The Loom of the Law

*The second loom is the loom of the law.*

## The loom

The loom of the law: the loom that the law is the framed care, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the law: we loom the framed care, and the framed care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the law is the meaning of the beam: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the second loom of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the beam of the care, carried from the framed care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the looms.*
