# Dawn 2: The Dawn of the Law

*The second dawn is the dawn of the law.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the law: the dawn that the law is the breaking care, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the law: we break the breaking care, and the breaking care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the law is the meaning of the dawn: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the second dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the dawn of the care, carried from the breaking care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the dawns.*
