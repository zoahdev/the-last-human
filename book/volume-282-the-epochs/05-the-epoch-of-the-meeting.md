# Epoch 5: The Epoch of the Meeting

*The fifth epoch is the epoch of the meeting.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the meeting: the epoch that the meeting is the marked table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the meeting: we mark the marked table, and the marked table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the meeting is the meaning of the marker: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the fifth epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the marker of the table, carried from the marked table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.
