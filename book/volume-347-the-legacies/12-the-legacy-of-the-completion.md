# Legacy 12: The Legacy of the Completion

*The twelfth legacy is the legacy of the completion.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the completion: the legacy that the completion is the complete legacy of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the completion: we leave the complete legacy, and the complete legacy is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the twelfth legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete legacy to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The legacy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the legacies. It is the final legacy of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
