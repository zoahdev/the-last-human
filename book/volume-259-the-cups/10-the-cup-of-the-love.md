# Cup 10: The Cup of the Love

*The tenth cup is the cup of the love.*

## The cup

The cup of the love: the cup that the love is the cupped heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the love: we cup the cupped heart, and the cupped heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the tenth cup of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the cupped heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
