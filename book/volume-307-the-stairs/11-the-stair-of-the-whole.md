# Stair 11: The Stair of the Whole

*The eleventh stair is the stair of the whole.*

## The stair

The stair of the whole: the stair that the whole is the one stair of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the whole: we wind the one stair, and the one stair is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the eleventh stair of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one stair to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stairs.*
