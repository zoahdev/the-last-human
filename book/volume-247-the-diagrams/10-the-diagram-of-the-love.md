# Diagram 10: The Diagram of the Love

*The tenth diagram is the diagram of the love.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the love: the diagram that the love is the drawn heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the love: we diagram the drawn heart, and the drawn heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the tenth diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the drawn heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The diagram is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the diagrams.*
