# Night 2: The Night of the Law

*The second night is the night of the law.*

## The night

The night of the law: the night that the law is the darkening care, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the law: we darken the darkening care, and the darkening care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the law is the meaning of the watch: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the second night of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the watch of the care, carried from the darkening care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights.*
