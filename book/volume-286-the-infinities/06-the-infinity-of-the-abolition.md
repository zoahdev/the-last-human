# Infinity 6: The Infinity of the Abolition

*The sixth infinity is the infinity of the abolition.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the abolition: the infinity that the abolition is the boundless freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the abolition: we extend the boundless freedom, and the boundless freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the sixth infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the boundless freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the infinities.*
