# Casket 2: The Casket of the Law

*The second casket is the casket of the law.*

## The casket

The casket of the law: the casket that the law is the shut care, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the law: we shut the shut care, and the shut care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the law is the meaning of the jewel: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the second casket of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the jewel of the care, carried from the shut care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caskets.*
