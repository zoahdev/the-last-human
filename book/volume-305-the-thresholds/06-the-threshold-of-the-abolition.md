# Threshold 6: The Threshold of the Abolition

*The sixth threshold is the threshold of the abolition.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the abolition: the threshold that the abolition is the crossed threshold of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the abolition: we cross the crossed threshold, and the crossed threshold is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the sixth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the crossed threshold to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
