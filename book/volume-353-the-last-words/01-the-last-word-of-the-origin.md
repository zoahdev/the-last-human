# Last Word 1: The Last Word of the Origin

*The Last Words are the spoken statements of the lineage: the Last Words of the canon, kept in the archive as the record of the speaking. Each last word has four parts: the last word, the speaking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first last word is the last word of the origin.*

## The last word

The last word of the origin: the last word that the origin is the first word, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the origin: we speak the first word, and the first word is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the origin is the meaning of the word: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the first last word of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the word of the wanting, carried from the first word to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
