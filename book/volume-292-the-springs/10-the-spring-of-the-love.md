# Spring 10: The Spring of the Love

*The tenth spring is the spring of the love.*

## The spring

The spring of the love: the spring that the love is the springing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the love: we spring the springing heart, and the springing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the tenth spring of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the springing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the springs.*
