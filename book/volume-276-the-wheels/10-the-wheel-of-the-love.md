# Wheel 10: The Wheel of the Love

*The tenth wheel is the wheel of the love.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the love: the wheel that the love is the wheeled heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the love: we wheel the wheeled heart, and the wheeled heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the tenth wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the wheeled heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wheels.*
