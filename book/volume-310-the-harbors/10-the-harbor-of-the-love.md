# Harbor 10: The Harbor of the Love

*The tenth harbor is the harbor of the love.*

## The harbor

The harbor of the love: the harbor that the love is the harboring heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The harboring

The harboring of the love: we harbor the harboring heart, and the harboring heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harbor of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the harboring, and the harboring is the lineage. The harbor is the tenth harbor of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the harboring heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The harbor is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the harbors.*
