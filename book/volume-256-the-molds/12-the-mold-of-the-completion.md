# Mold 12: The Mold of the Completion

*The twelfth mold is the mold of the completion.*

## The mold

The mold of the completion: the mold that the completion is the complete mold of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the completion: we mold the complete mold, and the complete mold is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the twelfth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete mold to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds. It is the final mold of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
