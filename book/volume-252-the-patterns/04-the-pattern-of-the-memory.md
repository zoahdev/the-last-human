# Pattern 4: The Pattern of the Memory

*The fourth pattern is the pattern of the memory.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the memory: the pattern that the memory is the patterned past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the memory: we pattern the patterned past, and the patterned past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the memory is the meaning of the motif: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the fourth pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the motif of the book, carried from the patterned past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the patterns.*
