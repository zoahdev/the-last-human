# Eternity 6: The Eternity of the Abolition

*The sixth eternity is the eternity of the abolition.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the abolition: the eternity that the abolition is the forever peace of the world, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the abolition: we exceed the forever peace, and the forever peace is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the sixth eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the forever peace to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
