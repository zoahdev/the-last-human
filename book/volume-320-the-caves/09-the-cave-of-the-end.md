# Cave 9: The Cave of the End

*The ninth cave is the cave of the end.*

## The cave

The cave of the end: the cave that the end is the final cave of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the end: we shelter the final cave, and the final cave is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the ninth cave of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final cave to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caves.*
