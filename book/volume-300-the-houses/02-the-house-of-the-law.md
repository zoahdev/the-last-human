# House 2: The House of the Law

*The second house is the house of the law.*

## The house

The house of the law: the house that the law is the timber of the care, kept by the lineage.

## The housing

The housing of the law: we house the timber, and the timber is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The house of the law is the meaning of the frame: the record of the housing, and the housing is the lineage. The house is the second house of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the frame of the care, carried from the timber to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The house is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the houses.*
