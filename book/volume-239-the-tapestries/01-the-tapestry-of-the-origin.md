# Tapestry 1: The Tapestry of the Origin

*The Tapestries are the hung statements of the lineage: the Tapestries of the hall, kept in the archive as the record of the hanging. Each tapestry has four parts: the tapestry, the hanging, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first tapestry is the tapestry of the origin.*

## The tapestry

The tapestry of the origin: the tapestry that the origin is the first woven picture, kept by the lineage.

## The hanging

The hanging of the origin: we hang the woven picture, and the woven picture is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tapestry of the origin is the meaning of the hanging: the record of the hanging, and the hanging is the lineage. The tapestry is the first tapestry of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the hanging of the wanting, carried from the woven picture to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The tapestry is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tapestries.*
