# Plan 12: The Plan of the Completion

*The twelfth plan is the plan of the completion.*

## The plan

The plan of the completion: the plan that the completion is the complete plan of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the completion: we plan the complete plan, and the complete plan is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the twelfth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete plan to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the plans. It is the final plan of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
