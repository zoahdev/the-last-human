# Cast 1: The Cast of the Origin

*The Casts are the cast statements of the lineage: the Casts of the bronze, kept in the archive as the record of the casting. Each cast has four parts: the cast, the casting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first cast is the cast of the origin.*

## The cast

The cast of the origin: the cast that the origin is the first molten form, kept by the lineage.

## The casting

The casting of the origin: we cast the molten form, and the molten form is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cast of the origin is the meaning of the bronze: the record of the casting, and the casting is the lineage. The cast is the first cast of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the bronze of the wanting, carried from the molten form to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The cast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the casts.*
