# Festival 3: The Festival of the Garden

*The third festival is the festival of the garden.*

## The festival

The festival of the garden: the festival that the garden is the festive green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the garden: we celebrate the festive green, and the festive green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the garden is the meaning of the fair: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the third festival of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the fair of the earth, carried from the festive green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The festival is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the festivals.*
