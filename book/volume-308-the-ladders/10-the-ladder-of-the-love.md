# Ladder 10: The Ladder of the Love

*The tenth ladder is the ladder of the love.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the love: the ladder that the love is the scaling heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the love: we scale the scaling heart, and the scaling heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the tenth ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the scaling heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The ladder is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ladders.*
