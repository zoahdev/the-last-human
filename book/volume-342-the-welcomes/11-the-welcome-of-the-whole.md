# Welcome 11: The Welcome of the Whole

*The eleventh welcome is the welcome of the whole.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the whole: the welcome that the whole is the one welcome of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the whole: we welcome the one welcome, and the one welcome is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the eleventh welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one welcome to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the welcomes.*
