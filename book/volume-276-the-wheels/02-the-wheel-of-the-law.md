# Wheel 2: The Wheel of the Law

*The second wheel is the wheel of the law.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the law: the wheel that the law is the wheeled care, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the law: we wheel the wheeled care, and the wheeled care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the law is the meaning of the axle: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the second wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the axle of the care, carried from the wheeled care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wheels.*
