# Window 5: The Window of the Meeting

*The fifth window is the window of the meeting.*

## The window

The window of the meeting: the window that the meeting is the bay table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the meeting: we open the bay table, and the bay table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the meeting is the meaning of the bay: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the fifth window of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the bay of the table, carried from the bay table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the windows.*
