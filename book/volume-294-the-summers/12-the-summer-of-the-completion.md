# Summer 12: The Summer of the Completion

*The twelfth summer is the summer of the completion.*

## The summer

The summer of the completion: the summer that the completion is the full summer of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the completion: we ripen the full summer, and the full summer is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the twelfth summer of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the full summer to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers. It is the final summer of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
