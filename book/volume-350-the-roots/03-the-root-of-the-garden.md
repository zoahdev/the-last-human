# Root 3: The Root of the Garden

*The third root is the root of the garden.*

## The root

The root of the garden: the root that the garden is the rooted green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the garden: we root the rooted green, and the rooted green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the garden is the meaning of the root garden: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the third root of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the root garden of the earth, carried from the rooted green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
