# Night 6: The Night of the Abolition

*The sixth night is the night of the abolition.*

## The night

The night of the abolition: the night that the abolition is the morning after the long night, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the abolition: we darken the morning after, and the morning after is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the sixth night of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the morning after to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the nights.*
