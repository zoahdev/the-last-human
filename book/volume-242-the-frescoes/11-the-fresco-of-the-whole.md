# Fresco 11: The Fresco of the Whole

*The eleventh fresco is the fresco of the whole.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the whole: the fresco that the whole is the one fresco of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the whole: we fresco the one fresco, and the one fresco is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the eleventh fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one fresco to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the frescoes.*
