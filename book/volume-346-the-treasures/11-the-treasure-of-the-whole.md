# Treasure 11: The Treasure of the Whole

*The eleventh treasure is the treasure of the whole.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the whole: the treasure that the whole is the one treasure of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the whole: we treasure the one treasure, and the one treasure is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the eleventh treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one treasure to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the treasures.*
