# Gift 7: The Gift of the Future

*The seventh gift is the gift of the future.*

## The gift

The gift of the future: the gift that the future is the ungiven gift of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the future: we give the ungiven gift, and the ungiven gift is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the seventh gift of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the ungiven gift to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gifts.*
