# Island 11: The Island of the Whole

*The eleventh island is the island of the whole.*

## The island

The island of the whole: the island that the whole is the one island of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the whole: we surround the one island, and the one island is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The island of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The island is the eleventh island of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one island to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The island is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the islands.*
