# Helix 7: The Helix of the Future

*The seventh helix is the helix of the future.*

## The helix

The helix of the future: the helix that the future is the unwound spring of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the future: we wind the unwound spring, and the unwound spring is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the seventh helix of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unwound spring to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
