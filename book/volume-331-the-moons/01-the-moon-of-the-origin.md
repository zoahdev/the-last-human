# Moon 1: The Moon of the Origin

*The Moons are the waxed statements of the lineage: the Moons of the night, kept in the archive as the record of the waxing. Each moon has four parts: the moon, the waxing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first moon is the moon of the origin.*

## The moon

The moon of the origin: the moon that the origin is the first pale light, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the origin: we wax the pale light, and the pale light is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the origin is the meaning of the night: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the first moon of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the night of the wanting, carried from the pale light to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the moons.*
