# Noon 1: The Noon of the Origin

*The Noons are the stood statements of the lineage: the Noons of the midday light, kept in the archive as the record of the standing. Each noon has four parts: the noon, the standing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first noon is the noon of the origin.*

## The noon

The noon of the origin: the noon that the origin is the first standing of the light, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the origin: we stand the standing of light, and the standing of light is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the origin is the meaning of the noon: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the first noon of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the noon of the wanting, carried from the standing of light to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the noons.*
