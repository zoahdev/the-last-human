# Design 12: The Design of the Completion

*The twelfth design is the design of the completion.*

## The design

The design of the completion: the design that the completion is the complete design of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the completion: we design the complete design, and the complete design is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the twelfth design of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete design to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the designs. It is the final design of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
