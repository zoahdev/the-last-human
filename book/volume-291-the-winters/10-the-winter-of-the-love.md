# Winter 10: The Winter of the Love

*The tenth winter is the winter of the love.*

## The winter

The winter of the love: the winter that the love is the warm heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The freezing

The freezing of the love: we freeze the warm heart, and the warm heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The winter of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the tenth winter of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the warm heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The winter is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the winters.*
