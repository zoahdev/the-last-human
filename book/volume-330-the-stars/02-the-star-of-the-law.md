# Star 2: The Star of the Law

*The second star is the star of the law.*

## The star

The star of the law: the star that the law is the shining care, kept by the lineage.

## The shining

The shining of the law: we shine the shining care, and the shining care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The star of the law is the meaning of the light: the record of the shining, and the shining is the lineage. The star is the second star of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the light of the care, carried from the shining care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The star is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stars.*
