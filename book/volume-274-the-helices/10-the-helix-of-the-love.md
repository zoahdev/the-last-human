# Helix 10: The Helix of the Love

*The tenth helix is the helix of the love.*

## The helix

The helix of the love: the helix that the love is the wound heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the love: we wind the wound heart, and the wound heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the tenth helix of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the wound heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
