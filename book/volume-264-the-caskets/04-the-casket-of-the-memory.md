# Casket 4: The Casket of the Memory

*The fourth casket is the casket of the memory.*

## The casket

The casket of the memory: the casket that the memory is the shut past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the memory: we shut the shut past, and the shut past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the memory is the meaning of the jewel: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the fourth casket of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the jewel of the book, carried from the shut past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caskets.*
