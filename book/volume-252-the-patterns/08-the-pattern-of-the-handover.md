# Pattern 8: The Pattern of the Handover

*The eighth pattern is the pattern of the handover.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the handover: the pattern that the handover is the patterned hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the handover: we pattern the patterned hand, and the patterned hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the handover is the meaning of the motif: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the eighth pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the motif of the hand, carried from the patterned hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.
