# Stair 2: The Stair of the Law

*The second stair is the stair of the law.*

## The stair

The stair of the law: the stair that the law is the winding care, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the law: we wind the winding care, and the winding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the law is the meaning of the flight: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the second stair of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the flight of the care, carried from the winding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs.*
