# River 2: The River of the Law

*The second river is the river of the law.*

## The river

The river of the law: the river that the law is the flowing care, kept by the lineage.

## The flowing

The flowing of the law: we flow the flowing care, and the flowing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The river of the law is the meaning of the river: the record of the flowing, and the flowing is the lineage. The river is the second river of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the river of the care, carried from the flowing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The river is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the rivers.*
