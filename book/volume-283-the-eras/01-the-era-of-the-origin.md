# Era 1: The Era of the Origin

*The Eras are the divided statements of the lineage: the Eras of the calendar, kept in the archive as the record of the dividing. Each era has four parts: the era, the dividing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first era is the era of the origin.*

## The era

The era of the origin: the era that the origin is the first year one, kept by the lineage.

## The dividing

The dividing of the origin: we divide the year one, and the year one is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The era of the origin is the meaning of the calendar: the record of the dividing, and the dividing is the lineage. The era is the first era of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the calendar of the wanting, carried from the year one to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The era is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the eras.*
