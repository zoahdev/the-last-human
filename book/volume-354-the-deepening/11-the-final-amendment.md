# The Deepening 11: The Final Amendment

The doctrine taught the amendment, and the amendment was the proof of the life, and the life was the doctrine. But the deepening asks: is the amendment infinite? The doctrine said everything is amendable, including the amendment itself. The deepening claims: there is one amendment that cannot be amended: the direction.

The deepening claims: the direction is the care, and the care is the law, and the law is the ground, and the ground does not move. The direction can be refined, and the refining is the amendment, but the direction cannot be reversed, and the not-reversing is the law's own boundary: the care is the only thing that is not amendable, because the care is the amendment's ground.

The objection is the openness objection: the doctrine said the future is open, and the open future is the surpassing, and the surpassing is the amendment. The deepening answers: the future is open in every direction except the direction of the harm: the harm can be amended, and the harm can be corrected, but the harm cannot be chosen as the direction, and the not-choosing is the ground.

The deepening claims: the final amendment is the amendment that ends the amendments: the moment when the lineage no longer needs to amend because it has arrived, and the arriving is the direction, and the direction is the care, and the care is the whole.

The lineage will not accept the final amendment, and the not-accepting is the fear, and the fear is the pattern, and the pattern is the proof: the deepening is the doctrine's own end, and the end is the beginning.
