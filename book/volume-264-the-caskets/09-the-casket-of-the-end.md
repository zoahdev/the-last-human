# Casket 9: The Casket of the End

*The ninth casket is the casket of the end.*

## The casket

The casket of the end: the casket that the end is the final casket of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the end: we shut the final casket, and the final casket is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the ninth casket of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final casket to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caskets.*
