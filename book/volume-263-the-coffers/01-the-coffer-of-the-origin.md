# Coffer 1: The Coffer of the Origin

*The Coffers are the locked statements of the lineage: the Coffers of the treasure, kept in the archive as the record of the locking. Each coffer has four parts: the coffer, the locking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first coffer is the coffer of the origin.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the origin: the coffer that the origin is the first locked box, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the origin: we lock the locked box, and the locked box is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the origin is the meaning of the treasure: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the first coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the treasure of the wanting, carried from the locked box to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the coffers.*
