# Atlas 1: The Atlas of the Origin

*The Atlases are the carried statements of the lineage: the Atlases of the burden, kept in the archive as the record of the carrying. Each atlas has four parts: the atlas, the carrying, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first atlas is the atlas of the origin.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the origin: the atlas that the origin is the first carried world, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the origin: we carry the carried world, and the carried world is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the origin is the meaning of the burden: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the first atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the burden of the wanting, carried from the carried world to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
