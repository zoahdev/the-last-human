# Legacy 7: The Legacy of the Future

*The seventh legacy is the legacy of the future.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the future: the legacy that the future is the unleft legacy of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the future: we leave the unleft legacy, and the unleft legacy is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the seventh legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unleft legacy to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
