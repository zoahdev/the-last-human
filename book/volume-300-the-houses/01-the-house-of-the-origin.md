# House 1: The House of the Origin

*The Houses are the housed statements of the lineage: the Houses of the earth, kept in the archive as the record of the housing. Each house has four parts: the house, the housing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first house is the house of the origin.*

## The house

The house of the origin: the house that the origin is the first roof, kept by the lineage.

## The housing

The housing of the origin: we house the roof, and the roof is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The house of the origin is the meaning of the house: the record of the housing, and the housing is the lineage. The house is the first house of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the house of the wanting, carried from the roof to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The house is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the houses.*
