# Mural 9: The Mural of the End

*The ninth mural is the mural of the end.*

## The mural

The mural of the end: the mural that the end is the final mural of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the end: we paint the final mural, and the final mural is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the ninth mural of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final mural to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
