# Farewell 10: The Farewell of the Love

*The tenth farewell is the farewell of the love.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the love: the farewell that the love is the bidding heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the love: we bid the bidding heart, and the bidding heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the tenth farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the bidding heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the farewells.*
