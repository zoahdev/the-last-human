# Pattern 1: The Pattern of the Origin

*The Patterns are the patterned statements of the lineage: the Patterns of the cloth, kept in the archive as the record of the patterning. Each pattern has four parts: the pattern, the patterning, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first pattern is the pattern of the origin.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the origin: the pattern that the origin is the first motif, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the origin: we pattern the motif, and the motif is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the origin is the meaning of the pattern: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the first pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the pattern of the wanting, carried from the motif to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the patterns.*
