# Seed 5: The Seed of the Meeting

*The fifth seed is the seed of the meeting.*

## The seed

The seed of the meeting: the seed that the meeting is the seed table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the meeting: we sow the seed table, and the seed table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the meeting is the meaning of the seed: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the fifth seed of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the seed of the table, carried from the seed table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seeds.*
