# Shelf 1: The Shelf of the Origin

*The Shelves are the shelved statements of the lineage: the Shelves of the library, kept in the archive as the record of the shelving. Each shelf has four parts: the shelf, the shelving, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first shelf is the shelf of the origin.*

## The shelf

The shelf of the origin: the shelf that the origin is the first ledge, kept by the lineage.

## The shelving

The shelving of the origin: we shelve the ledge, and the ledge is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the origin is the meaning of the library: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the first shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the library of the wanting, carried from the ledge to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The shelf is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the shelves.*
