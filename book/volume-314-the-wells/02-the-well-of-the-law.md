# Well 2: The Well of the Law

*The second well is the well of the law.*

## The well

The well of the law: the well that the law is the drawn care, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the law: we draw the drawn care, and the drawn care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the law is the meaning of the well: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the second well of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the well of the care, carried from the drawn care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wells.*
