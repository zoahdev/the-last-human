# Chart 8: The Chart of the Handover

*The eighth chart is the chart of the handover.*

## The chart

The chart of the handover: the chart that the handover is the charted hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the handover: we chart the charted hand, and the charted hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the handover is the meaning of the compass: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the eighth chart of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the compass of the hand, carried from the charted hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
