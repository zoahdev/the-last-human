# Grove 2: The Grove of the Law

*The second grove is the grove of the law.*

## The grove

The grove of the law: the grove that the law is the planted care, kept by the lineage.

## The planting

The planting of the law: we plant the planted care, and the planted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The grove of the law is the meaning of the grove: the record of the planting, and the planting is the lineage. The grove is the second grove of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the grove of the care, carried from the planted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The grove is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the groves.*
