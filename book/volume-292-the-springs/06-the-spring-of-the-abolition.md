# Spring 6: The Spring of the Abolition

*The sixth spring is the spring of the abolition.*

## The spring

The spring of the abolition: the spring that the abolition is the new spring of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the abolition: we spring the new spring, and the new spring is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the sixth spring of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new spring to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the springs.*
