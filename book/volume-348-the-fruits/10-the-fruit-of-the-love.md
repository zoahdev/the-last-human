# Fruit 10: The Fruit of the Love

*The tenth fruit is the fruit of the love.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the love: the fruit that the love is the ripening heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the love: we ripen the ripening heart, and the ripening heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the tenth fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the ripening heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits.*
