# Ocean 11: The Ocean of the Whole

*The eleventh ocean is the ocean of the whole.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the whole: the ocean that the whole is the one ocean of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the whole: we encompass the one ocean, and the one ocean is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the eleventh ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one ocean to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the oceans.*
