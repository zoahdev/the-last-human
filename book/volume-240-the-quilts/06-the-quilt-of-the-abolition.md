# Quilt 6: The Quilt of the Abolition

*The sixth quilt is the quilt of the abolition.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the abolition: the quilt that the abolition is the new patch of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the abolition: we quilt the new patch, and the new patch is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the sixth quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new patch to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the quilts.*
