# Spiral 3: The Spiral of the Garden

*The third spiral is the spiral of the garden.*

## The spiral

The spiral of the garden: the spiral that the garden is the spiraled green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The spiraling

The spiraling of the garden: we spiral the spiraled green, and the spiraled green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spiral of the garden is the meaning of the snail: the record of the spiraling, and the spiraling is the lineage. The spiral is the third spiral of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the snail of the earth, carried from the spiraled green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
