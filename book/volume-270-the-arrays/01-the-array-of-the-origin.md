# Array 1: The Array of the Origin

*The Arrays are the arrayed statements of the lineage: the Arrays of the stars, kept in the archive as the record of the arraying. Each array has four parts: the array, the arraying, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first array is the array of the origin.*

## The array

The array of the origin: the array that the origin is the first arranged stars, kept by the lineage.

## The arraying

The arraying of the origin: we array the arranged stars, and the arranged stars is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The array of the origin is the meaning of the constellation: the record of the arraying, and the arraying is the lineage. The array is the first array of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the constellation of the wanting, carried from the arranged stars to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The array is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the arrays.*
