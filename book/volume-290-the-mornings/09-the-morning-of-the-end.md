# Morning 9: The Morning of the End

*The ninth morning is the morning of the end.*

## The morning

The morning of the end: the morning that the end is the final morning of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the end: we wake the final morning, and the final morning is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the ninth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final morning to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
