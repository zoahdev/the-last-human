# Year 12: The Year of the Completion

*The twelfth year is the year of the completion.*

## The year

The year of the completion: the year that the completion is the complete year of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the completion: we count the complete year, and the complete year is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the twelfth year of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete year to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years. It is the final year of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
