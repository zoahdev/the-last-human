# Root 10: The Root of the Love

*The tenth root is the root of the love.*

## The root

The root of the love: the root that the love is the rooting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the love: we root the rooting heart, and the rooting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the tenth root of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the rooting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The root is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the roots.*
