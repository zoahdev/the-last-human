# Island 10: The Island of the Love

*The tenth island is the island of the love.*

## The island

The island of the love: the island that the love is the surrounding heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the love: we surround the surrounding heart, and the surrounding heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The island of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The island is the tenth island of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the surrounding heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
