# Bridge 2: The Bridge of the Law

*The second bridge is the bridge of the law.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the law: the bridge that the law is the bridging care, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the law: we bridge the bridging care, and the bridging care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the law is the meaning of the crossing: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the second bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the crossing of the care, carried from the bridging care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bridges.*
