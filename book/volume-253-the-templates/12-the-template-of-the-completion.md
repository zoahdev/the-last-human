## The meaning

The template of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the cutting, and the cutting is the lineage. The template is the twelfth template of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete template to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.
