# Feast 9: The Feast of the End

*The ninth feast is the feast of the end.*

## The feast

The feast of the end: the feast that the end is the final feast of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the end: we feast the final feast, and the final feast is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the ninth feast of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final feast to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts.*
