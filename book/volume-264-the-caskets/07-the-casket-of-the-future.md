# Casket 7: The Casket of the Future

*The seventh casket is the casket of the future.*

## The casket

The casket of the future: the casket that the future is the unopened casket of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the future: we shut the unopened casket, and the unopened casket is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the seventh casket of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unopened casket to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caskets.*
