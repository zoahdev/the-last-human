# Welcome 1: The Welcome of the Origin

*The Welcomes are the welcomed statements of the lineage: the Welcomes of the guest, kept in the archive as the record of the welcoming. Each welcome has four parts: the welcome, the welcoming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first welcome is the welcome of the origin.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the origin: the welcome that the origin is the first open door, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the origin: we welcome the open door, and the open door is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the origin is the meaning of the guest: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the first welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the guest of the wanting, carried from the open door to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the welcomes.*
