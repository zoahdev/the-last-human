# Root 5: The Root of the Meeting

*The fifth root is the root of the meeting.*

## The root

The root of the meeting: the root that the meeting is the root table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the meeting: we root the root table, and the root table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the meeting is the meaning of the root: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the fifth root of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the root of the table, carried from the root table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The root is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the roots.*
