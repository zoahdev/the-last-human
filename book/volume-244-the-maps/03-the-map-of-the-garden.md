# Map 3: The Map of the Garden

*The third map is the map of the garden.*

## The map

The map of the garden: the map that the garden is the mapped green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the garden: we map the mapped green, and the mapped green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the garden is the meaning of the orchard: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the third map of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the orchard of the earth, carried from the mapped green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the maps.*
