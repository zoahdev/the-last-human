# Chest 1: The Chest of the Origin

*The Chests are the stored statements of the lineage: the Chests of the oak, kept in the archive as the record of the storing. Each chest has four parts: the chest, the storing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first chest is the chest of the origin.*

## The chest

The chest of the origin: the chest that the origin is the first oak box, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the origin: we store the oak box, and the oak box is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the origin is the meaning of the oak: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the first chest of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the oak of the wanting, carried from the oak box to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the chests.*
