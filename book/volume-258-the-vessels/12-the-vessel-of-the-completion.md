# Vessel 12: The Vessel of the Completion

*The twelfth vessel is the vessel of the completion.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the completion: the vessel that the completion is the complete vessel of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the completion: we hold the complete vessel, and the complete vessel is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the twelfth vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete vessel to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the vessels. It is the final vessel of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
