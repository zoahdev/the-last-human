# Epoch 10: The Epoch of the Love

*The tenth epoch is the epoch of the love.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the love: the epoch that the love is the marked heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the love: we mark the marked heart, and the marked heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the tenth epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the marked heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the epochs.*
