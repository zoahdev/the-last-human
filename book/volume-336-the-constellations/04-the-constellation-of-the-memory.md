# Constellation 4: The Constellation of the Memory

*The fourth constellation is the constellation of the memory.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the memory: the constellation that the memory is the arranged past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the memory: we arrange the arranged past, and the arranged past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the memory is the meaning of the constellation: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the fourth constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the constellation of the book, carried from the arranged past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The constellation is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the constellations.*
