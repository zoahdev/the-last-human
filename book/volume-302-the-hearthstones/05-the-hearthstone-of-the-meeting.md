# Hearthstone 5: The Hearthstone of the Meeting

*The fifth hearthstone is the hearthstone of the meeting.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the meeting: the hearthstone that the meeting is the hearth table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the meeting: we warm the hearth table, and the hearth table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the meeting is the meaning of the stone: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the fifth hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the stone of the table, carried from the hearth table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
