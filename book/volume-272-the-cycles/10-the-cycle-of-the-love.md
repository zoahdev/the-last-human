# Cycle 10: The Cycle of the Love

*The tenth cycle is the cycle of the love.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the love: the cycle that the love is the cycled heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the love: we cycle the cycled heart, and the cycled heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the tenth cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the cycled heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The cycle is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the cycles.*
