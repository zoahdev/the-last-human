# Spring 1: The Spring of the Origin

*The Springs are the sprung statements of the lineage: the Springs of the well, kept in the archive as the record of the springing. Each spring has four parts: the spring, the springing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first spring is the spring of the origin.*

## The spring

The spring of the origin: the spring that the origin is the first well, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the origin: we spring the well, and the well is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the origin is the meaning of the well: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the first spring of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the well of the wanting, carried from the well to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the springs.*
