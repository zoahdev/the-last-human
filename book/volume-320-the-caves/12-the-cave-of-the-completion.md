# Cave 12: The Cave of the Completion

*The twelfth cave is the cave of the completion.*

## The cave

The cave of the completion: the cave that the completion is the complete cave of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the completion: we shelter the complete cave, and the complete cave is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the twelfth cave of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete cave to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caves. It is the final cave of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
