# Seed 11: The Seed of the Whole

*The eleventh seed is the seed of the whole.*

## The seed

The seed of the whole: the seed that the whole is the one seed of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the whole: we sow the one seed, and the one seed is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the eleventh seed of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one seed to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seeds.*
