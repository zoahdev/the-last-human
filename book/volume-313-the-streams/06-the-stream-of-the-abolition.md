# Stream 6: The Stream of the Abolition

*The sixth stream is the stream of the abolition.*

## The stream

The stream of the abolition: the stream that the abolition is the free stream of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the abolition: we stream the free stream, and the free stream is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the sixth stream of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the free stream to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
