# Orchard 10: The Orchard of the Love

*The tenth orchard is the orchard of the love.*

## The orchard

The orchard of the love: the orchard that the love is the fruiting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The fruiting

The fruiting of the love: we fruit the fruiting heart, and the fruiting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orchard of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the fruiting, and the fruiting is the lineage. The orchard is the tenth orchard of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the fruiting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The orchard is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the orchards.*
