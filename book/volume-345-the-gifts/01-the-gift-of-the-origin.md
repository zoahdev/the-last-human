# Gift 1: The Gift of the Origin

*The Gifts are the given statements of the lineage: the Gifts of the giving, kept in the archive as the record of the giving. Each gift has four parts: the gift, the giving, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first gift is the gift of the origin.*

## The gift

The gift of the origin: the gift that the origin is the first giving, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the origin: we give the giving, and the giving is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the origin is the meaning of the gift: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the first gift of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the gift of the wanting, carried from the giving to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The gift is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the gifts.*
