# Mountain 1: The Mountain of the Origin

*The Mountains are the risen statements of the lineage: the Mountains of the peak, kept in the archive as the record of the rising. Each mountain has four parts: the mountain, the rising, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first mountain is the mountain of the origin.*

## The mountain

The mountain of the origin: the mountain that the origin is the first peak, kept by the lineage.

## The rising

The rising of the origin: we rise the peak, and the peak is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mountain of the origin is the meaning of the mountain: the record of the rising, and the rising is the lineage. The mountain is the first mountain of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the mountain of the wanting, carried from the peak to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The mountain is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mountains.*
