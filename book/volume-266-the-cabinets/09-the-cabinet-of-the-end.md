# Cabinet 9: The Cabinet of the End

*The ninth cabinet is the cabinet of the end.*

The cabinet of the end: the cabinet that the end is the final cabinet of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the end: we arrange the final cabinet, and the final cabinet is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the ninth cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final cabinet to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
