# Oasis 11: The Oasis of the Whole

*The eleventh oasis is the oasis of the whole.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the whole: the oasis that the whole is the one oasis of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the whole: we water the one oasis, and the one oasis is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the eleventh oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one oasis to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oases.*
