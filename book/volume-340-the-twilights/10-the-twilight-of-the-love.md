# Twilight 10: The Twilight of the Love

*The tenth twilight is the twilight of the love.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the love: the twilight that the love is the fading heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the love: we fade the fading heart, and the fading heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the tenth twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the fading heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the twilights.*
