# Box 1: The Box of the Origin

*The Boxes are the boxed statements of the lineage: the Boxes of the letters, kept in the archive as the record of the boxing. Each box has four parts: the box, the boxing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first box is the box of the origin.*

## The box

The box of the origin: the box that the origin is the first container, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the origin: we box the container, and the container is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the origin is the meaning of the container: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the first box of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the container of the wanting, carried from the container to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the boxes.*
