# Constellation 3: The Constellation of the Garden

*The third constellation is the constellation of the garden.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the garden: the constellation that the garden is the starry green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the garden: we arrange the starry green, and the starry green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the garden is the meaning of the star garden: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the third constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the star garden of the earth, carried from the starry green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The constellation is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the constellations.*
