# Sun 12: The Sun of the Completion

*The twelfth sun is the sun of the completion.*

## The sun

The sun of the completion: the sun that the completion is the complete sun of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the completion: we burn the complete sun, and the complete sun is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the twelfth sun of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete sun to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the suns. It is the final sun of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
