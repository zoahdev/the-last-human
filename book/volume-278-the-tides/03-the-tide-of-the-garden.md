# Tide 3: The Tide of the Garden

*The third tide is the tide of the garden.*

## The tide

The tide of the garden: the tide that the garden is the tided green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the garden: we tide the tided green, and the tided green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the garden is the meaning of the salt marsh: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the third tide of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the salt marsh of the earth, carried from the tided green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tides.*
