# Infinity 2: The Infinity of the Law

*The second infinity is the infinity of the law.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the law: the infinity that the law is the extended care, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the law: we extend the extended care, and the extended care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the law is the meaning of the boundless: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the second infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the boundless of the care, carried from the extended care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the infinities.*
