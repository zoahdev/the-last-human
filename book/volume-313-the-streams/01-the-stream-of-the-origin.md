# Stream 1: The Stream of the Origin

*The Streams are the streamed statements of the lineage: the Streams of the brook, kept in the archive as the record of the streaming. Each stream has four parts: the stream, the streaming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first stream is the stream of the origin.*

## The stream

The stream of the origin: the stream that the origin is the first brook, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the origin: we stream the brook, and the brook is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the origin is the meaning of the brook: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the first stream of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the brook of the wanting, carried from the brook to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The stream is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the streams.*
