# Stream 10: The Stream of the Love

*The tenth stream is the stream of the love.*

## The stream

The stream of the love: the stream that the love is the streaming heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the love: we stream the streaming heart, and the streaming heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the tenth stream of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the streaming heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The stream is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the streams.*
