# Infinity 11: The Infinity of the Whole

*The eleventh infinity is the infinity of the whole.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the whole: the infinity that the whole is the one infinity of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the whole: we extend the one infinity, and the one infinity is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the eleventh infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one infinity to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the infinities.*
