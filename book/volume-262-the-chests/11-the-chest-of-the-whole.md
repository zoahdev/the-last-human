# Chest 11: The Chest of the Whole

*The eleventh chest is the chest of the whole.*

## The chest

The chest of the whole: the chest that the whole is the one chest of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the whole: we store the one chest, and the one chest is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the eleventh chest of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one chest to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the chests.*
