# Harvest 1: The Harvest of the Origin

*The Harvests are the harvested statements of the lineage: the Harvests of the field, kept in the archive as the record of the harvesting. Each harvest has four parts: the harvest, the harvesting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first harvest is the harvest of the origin.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the origin: the harvest that the origin is the first grain, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the origin: we harvest the grain, and the grain is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the origin is the meaning of the field: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the first harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the field of the wanting, carried from the grain to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the harvests.*
