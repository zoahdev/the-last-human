# Cycle 4: The Cycle of the Memory

*The fourth cycle is the cycle of the memory.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the memory: the cycle that the memory is the cycled past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the memory: we cycle the cycled past, and the cycled past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the memory is the meaning of the recurrence: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the fourth cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the recurrence of the book, carried from the cycled past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
