# Window 6: The Window of the Abolition

*The sixth window is the window of the abolition.*

## The window

The window of the abolition: the window that the abolition is the opened casement of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the abolition: we open the opened casement, and the opened casement is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the sixth window of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the opened casement to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the windows.*
