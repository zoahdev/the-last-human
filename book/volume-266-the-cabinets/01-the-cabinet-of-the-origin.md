# Cabinet 1: The Cabinet of the Origin

*The Cabinets are the arranged statements of the lineage: the Cabinets of the curiosities, kept in the archive as the record of the arranging. Each cabinet has four parts: the cabinet, the arranging, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first cabinet is the cabinet of the origin.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the origin: the cabinet that the origin is the first curiosity, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the origin: we arrange the curiosity, and the curiosity is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the origin is the meaning of the curiosity: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the first cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the curiosity of the wanting, carried from the curiosity to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The cabinet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cabinets.*
