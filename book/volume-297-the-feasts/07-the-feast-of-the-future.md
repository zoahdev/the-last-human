# Feast 7: The Feast of the Future

*The seventh feast is the feast of the future.*

## The feast

The feast of the future: the feast that the future is the coming feast of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the future: we feast the coming feast, and the coming feast is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the seventh feast of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the coming feast to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the feasts.*
