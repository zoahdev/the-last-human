# Schematic 2: The Schematic of the Law

*The second schematic is the schematic of the law.*

## The schematic

The schematic of the law: the schematic that the law is the drawn care, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the law: we draw the drawn care, and the drawn care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The schematic of the law is the meaning of the specification: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The schematic is the second schematic of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the specification of the care, carried from the drawn care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The schematic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the schematics.*
