# Cycle 12: The Cycle of the Completion

*The twelfth cycle is the cycle of the completion.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the completion: the cycle that the completion is the complete cycle of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the completion: we cycle the complete cycle, and the complete cycle is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the twelfth cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete cycle to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The cycle is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cycles. It is the final cycle of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
