# Lake 3: The Lake of the Garden

*The third lake is the lake of the garden.*

## The lake

The lake of the garden: the lake that the garden is the lakeside green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The mirroring

The mirroring of the garden: we mirror the lakeside green, and the lakeside green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The lake of the garden is the meaning of the lakeside: the record of the mirroring, and the mirroring is the lineage. The lake is the third lake of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the lakeside of the earth, carried from the lakeside green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The lake is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the lakes.*
