# Last Word 5: The Last Word of the Meeting

*The fifth last word is the last word of the meeting.*

## The last word

The last word of the meeting: the last word that the meeting is the final table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the meeting: we speak the final table, and the final table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the meeting is the meaning of the table: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the fifth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the table of the table, carried from the final table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
