# Farewell 6: The Farewell of the Abolition

*The sixth farewell is the farewell of the abolition.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the abolition: the farewell that the abolition is the farewell to the old order, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the abolition: we bid the old order, and the old order is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the sixth farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the old order to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the farewells.*
