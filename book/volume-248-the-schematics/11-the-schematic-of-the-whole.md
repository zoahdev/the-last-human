# Schematic 11: The Schematic of the Whole

*The eleventh schematic is the schematic of the whole.*

## The schematic

The schematic of the whole: the schematic that the whole is the one schematic of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the whole: we draw the one schematic, and the one schematic is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The schematic of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The schematic is the eleventh schematic of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one schematic to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The schematic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the schematics.*
