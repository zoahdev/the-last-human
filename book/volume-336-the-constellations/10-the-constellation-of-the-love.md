# Constellation 10: The Constellation of the Love

*The tenth constellation is the constellation of the love.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the love: the constellation that the love is the arranging heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the love: we arrange the arranging heart, and the arranging heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the tenth constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the arranging heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The constellation is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the constellations.*
