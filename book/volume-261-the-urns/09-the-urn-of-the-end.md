# Urn 9: The Urn of the End

*The ninth urn is the urn of the end.*

## The urn

The urn of the end: the urn that the end is the final urn of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the end: we keep the final urn, and the final urn is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the ninth urn of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final urn to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the urns.*
