# Well 5: The Well of the Meeting

*The fifth well is the well of the meeting.*

## The well

The well of the meeting: the well that the meeting is the well table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the meeting: we draw the well table, and the well table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the meeting is the meaning of the bucket: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the fifth well of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the bucket of the table, carried from the well table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
