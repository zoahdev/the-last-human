# Forest 11: The Forest of the Whole

*The eleventh forest is the forest of the whole.*

## The forest

The forest of the whole: the forest that the whole is the one forest of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The growing

The growing of the whole: we grow the one forest, and the one forest is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The forest of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the growing, and the growing is the lineage. The forest is the eleventh forest of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one forest to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The forest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the forests.*
