# Last Word 6: The Last Word of the Abolition

*The sixth last word is the last word of the abolition.*

## The last word

The last word of the abolition: the last word that the abolition is the open freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the abolition: we speak the open freedom, and the open freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the sixth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
