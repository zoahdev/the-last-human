# Wheel 11: The Wheel of the Whole

*The eleventh wheel is the wheel of the whole.*

## The wheel

The wheel of the whole: the wheel that the whole is the one wheel of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The wheeling

The wheeling of the whole: we wheel the one wheel, and the one wheel is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The wheel of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the wheeling, and the wheeling is the lineage. The wheel is the eleventh wheel of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one wheel to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The wheel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wheels.*
