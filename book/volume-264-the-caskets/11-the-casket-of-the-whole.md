# Casket 11: The Casket of the Whole

*The eleventh casket is the casket of the whole.*

## The casket

The casket of the whole: the casket that the whole is the one casket of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the whole: we shut the one casket, and the one casket is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the eleventh casket of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one casket to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caskets.*
