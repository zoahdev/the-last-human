# Field 11: The Field of the Whole

*The eleventh field is the field of the whole.*

## The field

The field of the whole: the field that the whole is the one field of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The tilling

The tilling of the whole: we till the one field, and the one field is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The field of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the tilling, and the tilling is the lineage. The field is the eleventh field of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one field to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The field is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the fields.*
