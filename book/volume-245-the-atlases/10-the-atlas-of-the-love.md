# Atlas 10: The Atlas of the Love

*The tenth atlas is the atlas of the love.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the love: the atlas that the love is the carried heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the love: we carry the carried heart, and the carried heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the tenth atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the carried heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the atlases.*
