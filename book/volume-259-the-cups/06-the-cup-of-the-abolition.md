# Cup 6: The Cup of the Abolition

*The sixth cup is the cup of the abolition.*

## The cup

The cup of the abolition: the cup that the abolition is the overflowing cup of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the abolition: we cup the overflowing cup, and the overflowing cup is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the sixth cup of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the overflowing cup to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the cups.*
