# Loom 4: The Loom of the Memory

*The fourth loom is the loom of the memory.*

## The loom

The loom of the memory: the loom that the memory is the loomed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the memory: we loom the loomed past, and the loomed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the memory is the meaning of the frame: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the fourth loom of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the frame of the book, carried from the loomed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the looms.*
