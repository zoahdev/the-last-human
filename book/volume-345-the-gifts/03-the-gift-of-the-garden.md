# Gift 3: The Gift of the Garden

*The third gift is the gift of the garden.*

## The gift

The gift of the garden: the gift that the garden is the giving green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The giving

The giving of the garden: we give the giving green, and the giving green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The gift of the garden is the meaning of the gift garden: the record of the giving, and the giving is the lineage. The gift is the third gift of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the gift garden of the earth, carried from the giving green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
