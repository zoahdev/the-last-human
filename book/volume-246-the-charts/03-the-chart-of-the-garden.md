# Chart 3: The Chart of the Garden

*The third chart is the chart of the garden.*

## The chart

The chart of the garden: the chart that the garden is the charted green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the garden: we chart the charted green, and the charted green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the garden is the meaning of the latitude: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the third chart of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the latitude of the earth, carried from the charted green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the charts.*
