# Comet 10: The Comet of the Love

*The tenth comet is the comet of the love.*

## The comet

The comet of the love: the comet that the love is the streaking heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The streaking

The streaking of the love: we streak the streaking heart, and the streaking heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The comet of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the streaking, and the streaking is the lineage. The comet is the tenth comet of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the streaking heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The comet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the comets.*
