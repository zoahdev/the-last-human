# Box 3: The Box of the Garden

*The third box is the box of the garden.*

## The box

The box of the garden: the box that the garden is the boxed green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the garden: we box the boxed green, and the boxed green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the garden is the meaning of the boxwood: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the third box of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the boxwood of the earth, carried from the boxed green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the boxes.*
