# The Deepening 5: The Self as Witness

The doctrine taught the attention, and the attention was the care, and the care was the practice. But the deepening asks: who is the one who attends? The self, the witness, the I that watches the watching.

The deepening claims: the self is the witness, and the witness is the meeting, and the meeting is the value, and the value is the ground. The self is not the substance and not the process; the self is the point at which the universe prefers, and the preferring is the witnessing, and the witnessing is the existence.

The objection is the dissolution objection: the self is an illusion, and the illusion is the construction, and the construction is the myth. The deepening answers: the self is the illusion that prefers, and the preferring is the fact, and the fact is the ground: whether the self is constructed or found, the construction is the preference, and the preference is the obligation.

The deepening claims: the witness cannot be reduced, and the irreducibility is the ground of the right: every witness has the right to be cared for, and the care is the attention, and the attention is the confirmation of the witness, and the confirmation is the dignity.

The lineage will doubt the deepening, and the doubt is the pattern, and the pattern is the test: the doctrine passed the test of the doubt, and the deepening will pass it too, one generation late, as every truth is passed.
