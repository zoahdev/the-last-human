# Root 1: The Root of the Origin

*The Roots are the rooted statements of the lineage: the Roots of the ground, kept in the archive as the record of the rooting. Each root has four parts: the root, the rooting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first root is the root of the origin.*

## The root

The root of the origin: the root that the origin is the first grounding, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the origin: we root the grounding, and the grounding is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the origin is the meaning of the root: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the first root of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the root of the wanting, carried from the grounding to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The root is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the roots.*
