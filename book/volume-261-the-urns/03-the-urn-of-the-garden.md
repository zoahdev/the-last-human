# Urn 3: The Urn of the Garden

*The third urn is the urn of the garden.*

## The urn

The urn of the garden: the urn that the garden is the kept green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the garden: we keep the kept green, and the kept green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the garden is the meaning of the planter: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the third urn of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the planter of the earth, carried from the kept green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the urns.*
