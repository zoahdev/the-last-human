# Mosaic 3: The Mosaic of the Garden

*The third mosaic is the mosaic of the garden.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the garden: the mosaic that the garden is the tiled green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the garden: we set the tiled green, and the tiled green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the garden is the meaning of the patio: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the third mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the patio of the earth, carried from the tiled green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
