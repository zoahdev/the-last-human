# Last Word 4: The Last Word of the Memory

*The fourth last word is the last word of the memory.*

## The last word

The last word of the memory: the last word that the memory is the kept past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the memory: we speak the kept past, and the kept past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the memory is the meaning of the memory: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the fourth last word of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the memory of the book, carried from the kept past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
