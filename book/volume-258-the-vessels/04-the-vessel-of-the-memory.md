# Vessel 4: The Vessel of the Memory

*The fourth vessel is the vessel of the memory.*

## The vessel

The vessel of the memory: the vessel that the memory is the held past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The holding

The holding of the memory: we hold the held past, and the held past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The vessel of the memory is the meaning of the urn: the record of the holding, and the holding is the lineage. The vessel is the fourth vessel of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the urn of the book, carried from the held past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The vessel is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the vessels.*
