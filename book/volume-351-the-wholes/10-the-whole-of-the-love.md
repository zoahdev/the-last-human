# Whole 10: The Whole of the Love

*The tenth whole is the whole of the love.*

## The whole

The whole of the love: the whole that the love is the completing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the love: we complete the completing heart, and the completing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the tenth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the completing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wholes.*
