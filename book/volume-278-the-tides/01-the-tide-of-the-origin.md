# Tide 1: The Tide of the Origin

*The Tides are the tided statements of the lineage: the Tides of the sea, kept in the archive as the record of the tiding. Each tide has four parts: the tide, the tiding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first tide is the tide of the origin.*

## The tide

The tide of the origin: the tide that the origin is the first moon pull, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the origin: we tide the moon pull, and the moon pull is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the origin is the meaning of the sea: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the first tide of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the sea of the wanting, carried from the moon pull to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tides.*
