# Blueprint 10: The Blueprint of the Love

*The tenth blueprint is the blueprint of the love.*

## The blueprint

The blueprint of the love: the blueprint that the love is the blueprinted heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The blueprinting

The blueprinting of the love: we blueprint the blueprinted heart, and the blueprinted heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The blueprint of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the blueprinting, and the blueprinting is the lineage. The blueprint is the tenth blueprint of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the blueprinted heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
