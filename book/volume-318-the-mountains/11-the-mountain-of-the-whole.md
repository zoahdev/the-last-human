# Mountain 11: The Mountain of the Whole

*The eleventh mountain is the mountain of the whole.*

## The mountain

The mountain of the whole: the mountain that the whole is the one mountain of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The rising

The rising of the whole: we rise the one mountain, and the one mountain is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mountain of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the rising, and the rising is the lineage. The mountain is the eleventh mountain of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one mountain to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The mountain is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mountains.*
