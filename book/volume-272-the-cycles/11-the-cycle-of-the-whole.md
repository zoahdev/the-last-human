# Cycle 11: The Cycle of the Whole

*The eleventh cycle is the cycle of the whole.*

## The cycle

The cycle of the whole: the cycle that the whole is the one cycle of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The cycling

The cycling of the whole: we cycle the one cycle, and the one cycle is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cycle of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the cycling, and the cycling is the lineage. The cycle is the eleventh cycle of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one cycle to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The cycle is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the cycles.*
