# Cave 5: The Cave of the Meeting

*The fifth cave is the cave of the meeting.*

## The cave

The cave of the meeting: the cave that the meeting is the cave table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the meeting: we shelter the cave table, and the cave table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the meeting is the meaning of the cave: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the fifth cave of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the cave of the table, carried from the cave table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caves.*
