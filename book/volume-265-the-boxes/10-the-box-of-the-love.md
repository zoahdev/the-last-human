# Box 10: The Box of the Love

*The tenth box is the box of the love.*

## The box

The box of the love: the box that the love is the boxed heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the love: we box the boxed heart, and the boxed heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the tenth box of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the boxed heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the boxes.*
