# Feast 2: The Feast of the Law

*The second feast is the feast of the law.*

## The feast

The feast of the law: the feast that the law is the feasted care, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the law: we feast the feasted care, and the feasted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the law is the meaning of the meal: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the second feast of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the meal of the care, carried from the feasted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts.*
