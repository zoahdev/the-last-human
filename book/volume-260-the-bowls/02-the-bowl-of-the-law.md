# Bowl 2: The Bowl of the Law

*The second bowl is the bowl of the law.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the law: the bowl that the law is the rounded care, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the law: we round the rounded care, and the rounded care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the law is the meaning of the bowl: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the second bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the bowl of the care, carried from the rounded care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bowls.*
