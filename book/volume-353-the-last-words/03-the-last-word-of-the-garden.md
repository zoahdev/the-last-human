# Last Word 3: The Last Word of the Garden

*The third last word is the last word of the garden.*

## The last word

The last word of the garden: the last word that the garden is the lasting green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The speaking

The speaking of the garden: we speak the lasting green, and the lasting green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The last word of the garden is the meaning of the garden: the record of the speaking, and the speaking is the lineage. The last word is the third last word of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden of the earth, carried from the lasting green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The last word is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the last words.*
