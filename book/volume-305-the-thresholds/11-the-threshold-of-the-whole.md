# Threshold 11: The Threshold of the Whole

*The eleventh threshold is the threshold of the whole.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the whole: the threshold that the whole is the one threshold of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the whole: we cross the one threshold, and the one threshold is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the eleventh threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one threshold to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the thresholds.*
