# Epoch 3: The Epoch of the Garden

*The third epoch is the epoch of the garden.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the garden: the epoch that the garden is the marked green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the garden: we mark the marked green, and the marked green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the garden is the meaning of the strata: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the third epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the strata of the earth, carried from the marked green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the epochs.*
