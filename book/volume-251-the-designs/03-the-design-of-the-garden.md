# Design 3: The Design of the Garden

*The third design is the design of the garden.*

## The design

The design of the garden: the design that the garden is the designed green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the garden: we design the designed green, and the designed green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the garden is the meaning of the landscape: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the third design of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the landscape of the earth, carried from the designed green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the designs.*
