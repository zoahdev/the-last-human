# Morning 5: The Morning of the Meeting

*The fifth morning is the morning of the meeting.*

## The morning

The morning of the meeting: the morning that the meeting is the morning table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the meeting: we wake the morning table, and the morning table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the meeting is the meaning of the breakfast: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the fifth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the breakfast of the table, carried from the morning table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
