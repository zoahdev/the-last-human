# Doorway 3: The Doorway of the Garden

*The third doorway is the doorway of the garden.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the garden: the doorway that the garden is the garden gate of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the garden: we open the garden gate, and the garden gate is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the garden is the meaning of the gate: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the third doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the gate of the earth, carried from the garden gate to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the doorways.*
