# Infinity 4: The Infinity of the Memory

*The fourth infinity is the infinity of the memory.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the memory: the infinity that the memory is the extended past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the memory: we extend the extended past, and the extended past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the memory is the meaning of the series: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the fourth infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the series of the book, carried from the extended past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
