# Window 1: The Window of the Origin

*The Windows are the opened statements of the lineage: the Windows of the light, kept in the archive as the record of the opening. Each window has four parts: the window, the opening, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first window is the window of the origin.*

## The window

The window of the origin: the window that the origin is the first glass, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the origin: we open the glass, and the glass is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the origin is the meaning of the light: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the first window of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the light of the wanting, carried from the glass to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the windows.*
