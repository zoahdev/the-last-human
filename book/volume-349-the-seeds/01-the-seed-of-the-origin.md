# Seed 1: The Seed of the Origin

*The Seeds are the sown statements of the lineage: the Seeds of the soil, kept in the archive as the record of the sowing. Each seed has four parts: the seed, the sowing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first seed is the seed of the origin.*

## The seed

The seed of the origin: the seed that the origin is the first sowing, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the origin: we sow the sowing, and the sowing is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the origin is the meaning of the seed: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the first seed of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the seed of the wanting, carried from the sowing to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the seeds.*
