# Shelf 12: The Shelf of the Completion

*The twelfth shelf is the shelf of the completion.*

## The shelf

The shelf of the completion: the shelf that the completion is the complete shelf of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The shelving

The shelving of the completion: we shelve the complete shelf, and the complete shelf is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the twelfth shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete shelf to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The shelf is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the shelves. It is the final shelf of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
