# Mural 4: The Mural of the Memory

*The fourth mural is the mural of the memory.*

## The mural

The mural of the memory: the mural that the memory is the public painting of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the memory: we paint the public painting, and the public painting is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the memory is the meaning of the mural: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the fourth mural of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the mural of the book, carried from the public painting to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
