# Diagram 12: The Diagram of the Completion

*The twelfth diagram is the diagram of the completion.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the completion: the diagram that the completion is the complete diagram of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the completion: we diagram the complete diagram, and the complete diagram is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the twelfth diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete diagram to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The diagram is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the diagrams. It is the final diagram of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
