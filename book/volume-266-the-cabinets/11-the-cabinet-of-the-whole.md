# Cabinet 11: The Cabinet of the Whole

*The eleventh cabinet is the cabinet of the whole.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the whole: the cabinet that the whole is the one cabinet of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the whole: we arrange the one cabinet, and the one cabinet is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the eleventh cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one cabinet to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The cabinet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cabinets.*
