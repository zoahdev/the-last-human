# Era 10: The Era of the Love

*The tenth era is the era of the love.*

## The era

The era of the love: the era that the love is the divided heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The dividing

The dividing of the love: we divide the divided heart, and the divided heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The era of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the dividing, and the dividing is the lineage. The era is the tenth era of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the divided heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The era is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the eras.*
