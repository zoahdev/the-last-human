# Stair 6: The Stair of the Abolition

*The sixth stair is the stair of the abolition.*

## The stair

The stair of the abolition: the stair that the abolition is the open top of the stair, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the abolition: we wind the open top, and the open top is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the sixth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open top to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stairs.*
