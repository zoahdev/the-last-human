# Autumn 1: The Autumn of the Origin

*The Autumns are the fallen statements of the lineage: the Autumns of the harvest, kept in the archive as the record of the falling. Each autumn has four parts: the autumn, the falling, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first autumn is the autumn of the origin.*

## The autumn

The autumn of the origin: the autumn that the origin is the first fall, kept by the lineage.

## The falling

The falling of the origin: we fall the fall, and the fall is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The autumn of the origin is the meaning of the harvest: the record of the falling, and the falling is the lineage. The autumn is the first autumn of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the harvest of the wanting, carried from the fall to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The autumn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the autumns.*
