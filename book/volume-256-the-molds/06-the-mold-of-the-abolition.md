# Mold 6: The Mold of the Abolition

*The sixth mold is the mold of the abolition.*

## The mold

The mold of the abolition: the mold that the abolition is the remolded freedom of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the abolition: we mold the remolded freedom, and the remolded freedom is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the sixth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the remolded freedom to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the molds.*
