# Season 1: The Season of the Origin

*The Seasons are the seasoned statements of the lineage: the Seasons of the year, kept in the archive as the record of the seasoning. Each season has four parts: the season, the seasoning, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first season is the season of the origin.*

## The season

The season of the origin: the season that the origin is the first year turn, kept by the lineage.

## The seasoning

The seasoning of the origin: we season the year turn, and the year turn is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The season of the origin is the meaning of the year: the record of the seasoning, and the seasoning is the lineage. The season is the first season of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the year of the wanting, carried from the year turn to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The season is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the seasons.*
