# Mold 4: The Mold of the Memory

*The fourth mold is the mold of the memory.*

## The mold

The mold of the memory: the mold that the memory is the molded past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The molding

The molding of the memory: we mold the molded past, and the molded past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mold of the memory is the meaning of the clay: the record of the molding, and the molding is the lineage. The mold is the fourth mold of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the clay of the book, carried from the molded past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The mold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the molds.*
