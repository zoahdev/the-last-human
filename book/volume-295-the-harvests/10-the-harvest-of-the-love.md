# Harvest 10: The Harvest of the Love

*The tenth harvest is the harvest of the love.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the love: the harvest that the love is the harvested heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the love: we harvest the harvested heart, and the harvested heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the tenth harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the harvested heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the harvests.*
