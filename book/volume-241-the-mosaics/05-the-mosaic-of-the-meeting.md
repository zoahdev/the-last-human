# Mosaic 5: The Mosaic of the Meeting

*The fifth mosaic is the mosaic of the meeting.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the meeting: the mosaic that the meeting is the mosaic table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the meeting: we set the mosaic table, and the mosaic table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the meeting is the meaning of the fragment: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the fifth mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the fragment of the table, carried from the mosaic table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
