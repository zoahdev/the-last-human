# Cup 4: The Cup of the Memory

*The fourth cup is the cup of the memory.*

## The cup

The cup of the memory: the cup that the memory is the cupped past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the memory: we cup the cupped past, and the cupped past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the memory is the meaning of the handful: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the fourth cup of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the handful of the book, carried from the cupped past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the cups.*
