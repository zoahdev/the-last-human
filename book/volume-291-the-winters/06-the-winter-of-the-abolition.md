# Winter 6: The Winter of the Abolition

## The freezing

## The meaning

The winter of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the freezing, and the freezing is the lineage. The winter is the sixth winter of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the thaw to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
