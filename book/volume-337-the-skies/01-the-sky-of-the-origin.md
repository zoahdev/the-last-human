# Sky 1: The Sky of the Origin

*The Skies are the arched statements of the lineage: the Skies of the blue, kept in the archive as the record of the arching. Each sky has four parts: the sky, the arching, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first sky is the sky of the origin.*

## The sky

The sky of the origin: the sky that the origin is the first arching blue, kept by the lineage.

## The arching

The arching of the origin: we arch the arching blue, and the arching blue is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sky of the origin is the meaning of the blue: the record of the arching, and the arching is the lineage. The sky is the first sky of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the blue of the wanting, carried from the arching blue to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The sky is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the skies.*
