# Quilt 3: The Quilt of the Garden

*The third quilt is the quilt of the garden.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the garden: the quilt that the garden is the quilted green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the garden: we quilt the quilted green, and the quilted green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the garden is the meaning of the garden patch: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the third quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden patch of the earth, carried from the quilted green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the quilts.*
