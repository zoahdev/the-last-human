# Quilt 12: The Quilt of the Completion

*The twelfth quilt is the quilt of the completion.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the completion: the quilt that the completion is the complete quilt of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the completion: we quilt the complete quilt, and the complete quilt is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the twelfth quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete quilt to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the quilts. It is the final quilt of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
