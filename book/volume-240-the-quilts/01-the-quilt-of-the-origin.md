# Quilt 1: The Quilt of the Origin

*The Quilts are the quilted statements of the lineage: the Quilts of the bed, kept in the archive as the record of the quilting. Each quilt has four parts: the quilt, the quilting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first quilt is the quilt of the origin.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the origin: the quilt that the origin is the first patch, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the origin: we quilt the patch, and the patch is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the origin is the meaning of the blanket: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the first quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the blanket of the wanting, carried from the patch to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the quilts.*
