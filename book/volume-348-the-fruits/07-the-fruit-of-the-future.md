# Fruit 7: The Fruit of the Future

*The seventh fruit is the fruit of the future.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the future: the fruit that the future is the unripe fruit of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the future: we ripen the unripe fruit, and the unripe fruit is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the seventh fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unripe fruit to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits.*
