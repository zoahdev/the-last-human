# Grove 1: The Grove of the Origin

*The Groves are the planted statements of the lineage: the Groves of the sacred trees, kept in the archive as the record of the planting. Each grove has four parts: the grove, the planting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first grove is the grove of the origin.*

## The grove

The grove of the origin: the grove that the origin is the first sacred tree, kept by the lineage.

## The planting

The planting of the origin: we plant the sacred tree, and the sacred tree is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The grove of the origin is the meaning of the grove: the record of the planting, and the planting is the lineage. The grove is the first grove of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the grove of the wanting, carried from the sacred tree to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The grove is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the groves.*
