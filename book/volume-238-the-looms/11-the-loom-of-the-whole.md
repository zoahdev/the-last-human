# Loom 11: The Loom of the Whole

*The eleventh loom is the loom of the whole.*

## The loom

The loom of the whole: the loom that the whole is the one loom of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the whole: we loom the one loom, and the one loom is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the eleventh loom of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one loom to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the looms.*
