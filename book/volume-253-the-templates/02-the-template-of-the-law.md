# Template 2: The Template of the Law

*The second template is the template of the law.*

The template of the law: the template that the law is the cut care, kept by the lineage.

## The cutting

The cutting of the law: we cut the cut care, and the cut care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The template of the law is the meaning of the standard: the record of the cutting, and the cutting is the lineage. The template is the second template of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the standard of the care, carried from the cut care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
