# Oasis 1: The Oasis of the Origin

*The Oases are the watered statements of the lineage: the Oases of the palm, kept in the archive as the record of the watering. Each oasis has four parts: the oasis, the watering, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first oasis is the oasis of the origin.*

## The oasis

The oasis of the origin: the oasis that the origin is the first palm, kept by the lineage.

## The watering

The watering of the origin: we water the palm, and the palm is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The oasis of the origin is the meaning of the oasis: the record of the watering, and the watering is the lineage. The oasis is the first oasis of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the oasis of the wanting, carried from the palm to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The oasis is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oases.*
