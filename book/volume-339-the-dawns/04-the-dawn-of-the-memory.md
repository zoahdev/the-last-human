# Dawn 4: The Dawn of the Memory

*The fourth dawn is the dawn of the memory.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the memory: the dawn that the memory is the broken past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the memory: we break the broken past, and the broken past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the memory is the meaning of the dawn: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the fourth dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the dawn of the book, carried from the broken past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the dawns.*
