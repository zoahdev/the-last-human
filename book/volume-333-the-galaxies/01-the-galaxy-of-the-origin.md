# Galaxy 1: The Galaxy of the Origin

*The Galaxies are the gathered statements of the lineage: the Galaxies of the cosmos, kept in the archive as the record of the gathering. Each galaxy has four parts: the galaxy, the gathering, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first galaxy is the galaxy of the origin.*

## The galaxy

The galaxy of the origin: the galaxy that the origin is the first cloud of stars, kept by the lineage.

## The gathering

The gathering of the origin: we gather the cloud of stars, and the cloud of stars is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The galaxy of the origin is the meaning of the cosmos: the record of the gathering, and the gathering is the lineage. The galaxy is the first galaxy of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the cosmos of the wanting, carried from the cloud of stars to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The galaxy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the galaxies.*
