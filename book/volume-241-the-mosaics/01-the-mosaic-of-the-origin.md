# Mosaic 1: The Mosaic of the Origin

*The Mosaics are the set statements of the lineage: the Mosaics of the floor, kept in the archive as the record of the setting. Each mosaic has four parts: the mosaic, the setting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first mosaic is the mosaic of the origin.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the origin: the mosaic that the origin is the first tile, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the origin: we set the tile, and the tile is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the origin is the meaning of the floor: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the first mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the floor of the wanting, carried from the tile to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the mosaics.*
