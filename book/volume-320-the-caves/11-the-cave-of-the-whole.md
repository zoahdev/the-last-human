# Cave 11: The Cave of the Whole

*The eleventh cave is the cave of the whole.*

## The cave

The cave of the whole: the cave that the whole is the one cave of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the whole: we shelter the one cave, and the one cave is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the eleventh cave of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one cave to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the caves.*
