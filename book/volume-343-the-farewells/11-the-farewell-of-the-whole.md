# Farewell 11: The Farewell of the Whole

*The eleventh farewell is the farewell of the whole.*

## The farewell

The farewell of the whole: the farewell that the whole is the one farewell of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The bidding

The bidding of the whole: we bid the one farewell, and the one farewell is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The farewell of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the bidding, and the bidding is the lineage. The farewell is the eleventh farewell of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one farewell to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The farewell is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the farewells.*
