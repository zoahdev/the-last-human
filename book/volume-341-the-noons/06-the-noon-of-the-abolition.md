# Noon 6: The Noon of the Abolition

*The sixth noon is the noon of the abolition.*

## The noon

The noon of the abolition: the noon that the abolition is the high noon of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the abolition: we stand the high noon, and the high noon is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the sixth noon of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the high noon to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the noons.*
