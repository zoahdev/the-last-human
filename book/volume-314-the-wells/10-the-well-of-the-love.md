# Well 10: The Well of the Love

*The tenth well is the well of the love.*

## The well

The well of the love: the well that the love is the drawn heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the love: we draw the drawn heart, and the drawn heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The well of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The well is the tenth well of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the drawn heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The well is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wells.*
