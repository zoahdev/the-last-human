# Heaven 4: The Heaven of the Memory

*The fourth heaven is the heaven of the memory.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the memory: the heaven that the memory is the raised past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the memory: we raise the raised past, and the raised past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the memory is the meaning of the beyond: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the fourth heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the beyond of the book, carried from the raised past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the heavens.*
