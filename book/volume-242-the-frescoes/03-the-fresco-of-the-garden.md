# Fresco 3: The Fresco of the Garden

*The third fresco is the fresco of the garden.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the garden: the fresco that the garden is the painted green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the garden: we fresco the painted green, and the painted green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the garden is the meaning of the garden wall: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the third fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden wall of the earth, carried from the painted green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the frescoes.*
