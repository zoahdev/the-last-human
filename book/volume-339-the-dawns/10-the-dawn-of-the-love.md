# Dawn 10: The Dawn of the Love

*The tenth dawn is the dawn of the love.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the love: the dawn that the love is the breaking heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the love: we break the breaking heart, and the breaking heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the tenth dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the breaking heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the dawns.*
