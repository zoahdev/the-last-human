# Twilight 9: The Twilight of the End

*The ninth twilight is the twilight of the end.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the end: the twilight that the end is the final twilight of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the end: we fade the final twilight, and the final twilight is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the ninth twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final twilight to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the twilights.*
