# Meadow 2: The Meadow of the Law

*The second meadow is the meadow of the law.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the law: the meadow that the law is the blooming care, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the law: we bloom the blooming care, and the blooming care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the law is the meaning of the meadow: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the second meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the meadow of the care, carried from the blooming care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the meadows.*
