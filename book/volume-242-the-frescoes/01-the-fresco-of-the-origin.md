# Fresco 1: The Fresco of the Origin

*The Frescoes are the frescoed statements of the lineage: the Frescoes of the wall, kept in the archive as the record of the frescoing. Each fresco has four parts: the fresco, the frescoing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first fresco is the fresco of the origin.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the origin: the fresco that the origin is the first painted wall, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the origin: we fresco the painted wall, and the painted wall is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the origin is the meaning of the plaster: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the first fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the plaster of the wanting, carried from the painted wall to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the frescoes.*
