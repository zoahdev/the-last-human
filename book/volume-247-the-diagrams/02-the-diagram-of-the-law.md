# Diagram 2: The Diagram of the Law

*The second diagram is the diagram of the law.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the law: the diagram that the law is the outlined care, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the law: we diagram the outlined care, and the outlined care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the law is the meaning of the sketch: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the second diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the sketch of the care, carried from the outlined care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
