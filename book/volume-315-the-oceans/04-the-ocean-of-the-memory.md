# Ocean 4: The Ocean of the Memory

*The fourth ocean is the ocean of the memory.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the memory: the ocean that the memory is the encompassed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the memory: we encompass the encompassed past, and the encompassed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the memory is the meaning of the world: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the fourth ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the world of the book, carried from the encompassed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the oceans.*
