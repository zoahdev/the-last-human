# Fruit 4: The Fruit of the Memory

*The fourth fruit is the fruit of the memory.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the memory: the fruit that the memory is the ripened past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the memory: we ripen the ripened past, and the ripened past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the memory is the meaning of the fruit: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the fourth fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the fruit of the book, carried from the ripened past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the fruits.*
