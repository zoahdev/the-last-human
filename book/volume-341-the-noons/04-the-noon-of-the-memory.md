# Noon 4: The Noon of the Memory

*The fourth noon is the noon of the memory.*

## The noon

The noon of the memory: the noon that the memory is the stood past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the memory: we stand the stood past, and the stood past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the memory is the meaning of the noon: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the fourth noon of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the noon of the book, carried from the stood past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the noons.*
