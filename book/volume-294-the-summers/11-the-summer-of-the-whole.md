# Summer 11: The Summer of the Whole

*The eleventh summer is the summer of the whole.*

## The summer

The summer of the whole: the summer that the whole is the one summer of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the whole: we ripen the one summer, and the one summer is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the eleventh summer of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one summer to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
