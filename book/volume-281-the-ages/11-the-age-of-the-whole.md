# Age 11: The Age of the Whole

*The eleventh age is the age of the whole.*

## The age

The age of the whole: the age that the whole is the one age of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The aging

The aging of the whole: we age the one age, and the one age is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The age of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the aging, and the aging is the lineage. The age is the eleventh age of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one age to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The age is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ages.*
