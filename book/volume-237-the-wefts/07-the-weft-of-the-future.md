# Weft 7: The Weft of the Future

*The seventh weft is the weft of the future.*

## The weft

The weft of the future: the weft that the future is the unwoven cloth of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the future: we weave the unwoven cloth, and the unwoven cloth is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the seventh weft of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unwoven cloth to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wefts.*
