# Coffer 11: The Coffer of the Whole

*The eleventh coffer is the coffer of the whole.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the whole: the coffer that the whole is the one coffer of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the whole: we lock the one coffer, and the one coffer is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the eleventh coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one coffer to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the coffers.*
