# Quilt 7: The Quilt of the Future

*The seventh quilt is the quilt of the future.*

## The quilt

The quilt of the future: the quilt that the future is the unquilted dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The quilting

The quilting of the future: we quilt the unquilted dawn, and the unquilted dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The quilt of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the quilting, and the quilting is the lineage. The quilt is the seventh quilt of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unquilted dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The quilt is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the quilts.*
