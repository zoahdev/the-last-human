# Lake 4: The Lake of the Memory

*The fourth lake is the lake of the memory.*

## The lake

The lake of the memory: the lake that the memory is the mirrored past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The mirroring

The mirroring of the memory: we mirror the mirrored past, and the mirrored past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The lake of the memory is the meaning of the valley: the record of the mirroring, and the mirroring is the lineage. The lake is the fourth lake of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the valley of the book, carried from the mirrored past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The lake is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the lakes.*
