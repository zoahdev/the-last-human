# Return 10: The Return of the Love

*The tenth return is the return of the love.*

## The return

The return of the love: the return that the love is the returning heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The returning

The returning of the love: we return the returning heart, and the returning heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The return of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the returning, and the returning is the lineage. The return is the tenth return of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the returning heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The return is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the returns.*
