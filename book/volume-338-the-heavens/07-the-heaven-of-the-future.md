# Heaven 7: The Heaven of the Future

*The seventh heaven is the heaven of the future.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the future: the heaven that the future is the unraised heaven of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the future: we raise the unraised heaven, and the unraised heaven is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the seventh heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unraised heaven to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the heavens.*
