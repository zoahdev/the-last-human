# Harvest 12: The Harvest of the Completion

*The twelfth harvest is the harvest of the completion.*

## The harvest

The harvest of the completion: the harvest that the completion is the complete harvest of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The harvesting

The harvesting of the completion: we harvest the complete harvest, and the complete harvest is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harvest of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the harvesting, and the harvesting is the lineage. The harvest is the twelfth harvest of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete harvest to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The harvest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the harvests. It is the final harvest of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
