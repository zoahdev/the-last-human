# Season 10: The Season of the Love

*The tenth season is the season of the love.*

## The season

The season of the love: the season that the love is the seasoned heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The seasoning

The seasoning of the love: we season the seasoned heart, and the seasoned heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The season of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the seasoning, and the seasoning is the lineage. The season is the tenth season of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the seasoned heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
