# Morning 12: The Morning of the Completion

*The twelfth morning is the morning of the completion.*

## The morning

The morning of the completion: the morning that the completion is the full morning of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the completion: we wake the full morning, and the full morning is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the twelfth morning of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the full morning to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings. It is the final morning of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
