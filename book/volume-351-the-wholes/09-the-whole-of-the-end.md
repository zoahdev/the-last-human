# Whole 9: The Whole of the End

*The ninth whole is the whole of the end.*

## The whole

The whole of the end: the whole that the end is the final whole of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the end: we complete the final whole, and the final whole is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the ninth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final whole to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wholes.*
