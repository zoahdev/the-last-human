# Night 3: The Night of the Garden

*The third night is the night of the garden.*

## The night

The night of the garden: the night that the garden is the dark green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The darkening

The darkening of the garden: we darken the dark green, and the dark green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The night of the garden is the meaning of the night garden: the record of the darkening, and the darkening is the lineage. The night is the third night of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the night garden of the earth, carried from the dark green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The night is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the nights.*
