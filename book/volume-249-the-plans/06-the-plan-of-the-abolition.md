# Plan 6: The Plan of the Abolition

*The sixth plan is the plan of the abolition.*

## The plan

The plan of the abolition: the plan that the abolition is the new plan of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the abolition: we plan the new plan, and the new plan is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the sixth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the new plan to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
