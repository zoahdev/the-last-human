# One 9: The One of the End

*The ninth one is the one of the end.*

## The one

The one of the end: the one that the end is the final one of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the end: we unite the final one, and the final one is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the ninth one of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final one to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ones.*
