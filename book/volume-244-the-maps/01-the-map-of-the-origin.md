# Map 1: The Map of the Origin

*The Maps are the mapped statements of the lineage: the Maps of the world, kept in the archive as the record of the mapping. Each map has four parts: the map, the mapping, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first map is the map of the origin.*

## The map

The map of the origin: the map that the origin is the first chart, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the origin: we map the chart, and the chart is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the origin is the meaning of the world: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the first map of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the world of the wanting, carried from the chart to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the maps.*
