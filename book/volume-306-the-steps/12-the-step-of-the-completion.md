# Step 12: The Step of the Completion

*The twelfth step is the step of the completion.*

## The step

The step of the completion: the step that the completion is the complete step of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the completion: we step the complete step, and the complete step is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the twelfth step of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete step to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the steps. It is the final step of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
