# Weft 3: The Weft of the Garden

*The third weft is the weft of the garden.*

## The weft

The weft of the garden: the weft that the garden is the flowering cloth of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the garden: we weave the flowering cloth, and the flowering cloth is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the garden is the meaning of the pattern: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the third weft of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the pattern of the earth, carried from the flowering cloth to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wefts.*
