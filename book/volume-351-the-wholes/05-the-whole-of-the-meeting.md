# Whole 5: The Whole of the Meeting

*The fifth whole is the whole of the meeting.*

## The whole

The whole of the meeting: the whole that the meeting is the whole table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the meeting: we complete the whole table, and the whole table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the meeting is the meaning of the whole: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the fifth whole of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the whole of the table, carried from the whole table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wholes.*
