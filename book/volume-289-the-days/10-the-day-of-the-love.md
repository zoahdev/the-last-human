# Day 10: The Day of the Love

*The tenth day is the day of the love.*

## The day

The day of the love: the day that the love is the bright heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the love: we open the bright heart, and the bright heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The day of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The day is the tenth day of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the bright heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The day is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the days.*
