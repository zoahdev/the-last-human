# Weft 5: The Weft of the Meeting

*The fifth weft is the weft of the meeting.*

## The weft

The weft of the meeting: the weft that the meeting is the shared loom of the table, kept by the lineage.

## The weaving

The weaving of the meeting: we weave the shared loom, and the shared loom is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The weft of the meeting is the meaning of the table: the record of the weaving, and the weaving is the lineage. The weft is the fifth weft of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the table of the table, carried from the shared loom to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The weft is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wefts.*
