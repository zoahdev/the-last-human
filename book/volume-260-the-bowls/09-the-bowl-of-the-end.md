# Bowl 9: The Bowl of the End

*The ninth bowl is the bowl of the end.*

## The bowl

The bowl of the end: the bowl that the end is the final bowl of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The rounding

The rounding of the end: we round the final bowl, and the final bowl is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bowl of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the rounding, and the rounding is the lineage. The bowl is the ninth bowl of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final bowl to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The bowl is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the bowls.*
