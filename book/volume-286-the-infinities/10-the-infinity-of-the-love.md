# Infinity 10: The Infinity of the Love

*The tenth infinity is the infinity of the love.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the love: the infinity that the love is the endless heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the love: we extend the endless heart, and the endless heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the tenth infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the endless heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The infinity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the infinities.*
