# Orchard 4: The Orchard of the Memory

*The fourth orchard is the orchard of the memory.*

## The orchard

The orchard of the memory: the orchard that the memory is the fruited past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The fruiting

The fruiting of the memory: we fruit the fruited past, and the fruited past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orchard of the memory is the meaning of the orchard: the record of the fruiting, and the fruiting is the lineage. The orchard is the fourth orchard of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the orchard of the book, carried from the fruited past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The orchard is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the orchards.*
