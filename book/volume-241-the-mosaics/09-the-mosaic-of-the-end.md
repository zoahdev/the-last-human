# Mosaic 9: The Mosaic of the End

*The ninth mosaic is the mosaic of the end.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the end: the mosaic that the end is the final mosaic of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the end: we set the final mosaic, and the final mosaic is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the ninth mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final mosaic to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
