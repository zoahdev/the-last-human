# Island 2: The Island of the Law

*The second island is the island of the law.*

The island of the law: the island that the law is the surrounding care, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the law: we surround the surrounding care, and the surrounding care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The island of the law is the meaning of the island: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The island is the second island of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the island of the care, carried from the surrounding care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
