# House 10: The House of the Love

*The tenth house is the house of the love.*

## The house

The house of the love: the house that the love is the hearth of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The housing

The housing of the love: we house the hearth, and the hearth is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The house of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the housing, and the housing is the lineage. The house is the tenth house of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the hearth to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The house is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the houses.*
