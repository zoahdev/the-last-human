# Heaven 10: The Heaven of the Love

*The tenth heaven is the heaven of the love.*

## The heaven

The heaven of the love: the heaven that the love is the raising heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The raising

The raising of the love: we raise the raising heart, and the raising heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The heaven of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the raising, and the raising is the lineage. The heaven is the tenth heaven of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the raising heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The heaven is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the heavens.*
