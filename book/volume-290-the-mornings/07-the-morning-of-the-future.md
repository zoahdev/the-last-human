# Morning 7: The Morning of the Future

*The seventh morning is the morning of the future.*

## The morning

The morning of the future: the morning that the future is the coming morning of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the future: we wake the coming morning, and the coming morning is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the seventh morning of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the coming morning to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mornings.*
