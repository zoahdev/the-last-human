# Matrix 1: The Matrix of the Origin

*The Matrices are the pressed statements of the lineage: the Matrices of the coin, kept in the archive as the record of the pressing. Each matrix has four parts: the matrix, the pressing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first matrix is the matrix of the origin.*

## The matrix

The matrix of the origin: the matrix that the origin is the first die, kept by the lineage.

## The pressing

The pressing of the origin: we press the die, and the die is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The matrix of the origin is the meaning of the minting: the record of the pressing, and the pressing is the lineage. The matrix is the first matrix of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the minting of the wanting, carried from the die to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The matrix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the matrices.*
