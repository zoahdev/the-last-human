# Design 7: The Design of the Future

*The seventh design is the design of the future.*

## The design

The design of the future: the design that the future is the undesigned dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the future: we design the undesigned dawn, and the undesigned dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the seventh design of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the undesigned dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the designs.*
