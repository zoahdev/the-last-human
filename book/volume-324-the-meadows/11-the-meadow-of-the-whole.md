# Meadow 11: The Meadow of the Whole

*The eleventh meadow is the meadow of the whole.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the whole: the meadow that the whole is the one meadow of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the whole: we bloom the one meadow, and the one meadow is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the eleventh meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one meadow to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the meadows.*
