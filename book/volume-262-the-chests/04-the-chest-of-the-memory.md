# Chest 4: The Chest of the Memory

*The fourth chest is the chest of the memory.*

## The chest

The chest of the memory: the chest that the memory is the stored past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the memory: we store the stored past, and the stored past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the memory is the meaning of the box: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the fourth chest of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the box of the book, carried from the stored past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the chests.*
