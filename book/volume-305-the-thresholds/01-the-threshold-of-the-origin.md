# Threshold 1: The Threshold of the Origin

*The Thresholds are the crossed statements of the lineage: the Thresholds of the lintel, kept in the archive as the record of the crossing. Each threshold has four parts: the threshold, the crossing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first threshold is the threshold of the origin.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the origin: the threshold that the origin is the first lintel, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the origin: we cross the lintel, and the lintel is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the origin is the meaning of the lintel: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the first threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the lintel of the wanting, carried from the lintel to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The threshold is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the thresholds.*
