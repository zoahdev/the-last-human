# Seed 2: The Seed of the Law

*The second seed is the seed of the law.*

## The seed

The seed of the law: the seed that the law is the sowing care, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the law: we sow the sowing care, and the sowing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the law is the meaning of the seed: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the second seed of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the seed of the care, carried from the sowing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the seeds.*
