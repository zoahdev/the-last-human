# Fresco 10: The Fresco of the Love

*The tenth fresco is the fresco of the love.*

## The fresco

The fresco of the love: the fresco that the love is the painted heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The frescoing

The frescoing of the love: we fresco the painted heart, and the painted heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fresco of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the frescoing, and the frescoing is the lineage. The fresco is the tenth fresco of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the painted heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The fresco is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the frescoes.*
