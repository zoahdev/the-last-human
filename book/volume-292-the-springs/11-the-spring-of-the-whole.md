# Spring 11: The Spring of the Whole

*The eleventh spring is the spring of the whole.*

## The spring

The spring of the whole: the spring that the whole is the one spring of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the whole: we spring the one spring, and the one spring is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the eleventh spring of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one spring to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the springs.*
