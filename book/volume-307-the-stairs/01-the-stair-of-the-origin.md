# Stair 1: The Stair of the Origin

*The Stairs are the wound statements of the lineage: the Stairs of the tower, kept in the archive as the record of the winding. Each stair has four parts: the stair, the winding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first stair is the stair of the origin.*

## The stair

The stair of the origin: the stair that the origin is the first tower, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the origin: we wind the tower, and the tower is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the origin is the meaning of the stair: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the first stair of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the stair of the wanting, carried from the tower to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs.*
