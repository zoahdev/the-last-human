# Orchard 6: The Orchard of the Abolition

*The sixth orchard is the orchard of the abolition.*

## The orchard

The orchard of the abolition: the orchard that the abolition is the open orchard of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The fruiting

The fruiting of the abolition: we fruit the open orchard, and the open orchard is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The orchard of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the fruiting, and the fruiting is the lineage. The orchard is the sixth orchard of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the open orchard to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The orchard is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the orchards.*
