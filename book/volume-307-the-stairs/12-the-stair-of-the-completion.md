# Stair 12: The Stair of the Completion

*The twelfth stair is the stair of the completion.*

## The stair

The stair of the completion: the stair that the completion is the complete stair of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the completion: we wind the complete stair, and the complete stair is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the twelfth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete stair to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the stairs. It is the final stair of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
