# Tide 11: The Tide of the Whole

*The eleventh tide is the tide of the whole.*

## The tide

The tide of the whole: the tide that the whole is the one tide of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the whole: we tide the one tide, and the one tide is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the eleventh tide of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one tide to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tides.*
