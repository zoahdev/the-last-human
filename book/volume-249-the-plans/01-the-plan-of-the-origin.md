# Plan 1: The Plan of the Origin

*The Plans are the planned statements of the lineage: the Plans of the house, kept in the archive as the record of the planning. Each plan has four parts: the plan, the planning, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first plan is the plan of the origin.*

## The plan

The plan of the origin: the plan that the origin is the first purpose, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the origin: we plan the purpose, and the purpose is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the origin is the meaning of the design: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the first plan of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the design of the wanting, carried from the purpose to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the plans.*
