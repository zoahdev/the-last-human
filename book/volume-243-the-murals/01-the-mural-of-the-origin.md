# Mural 1: The Mural of the Origin

*The Murals are the painted statements of the lineage: the Murals of the city, kept in the archive as the record of the painting. Each mural has four parts: the mural, the painting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first mural is the mural of the origin.*

## The mural

The mural of the origin: the mural that the origin is the first wall painting, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the origin: we paint the wall painting, and the wall painting is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the origin is the meaning of the city: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the first mural of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the city of the wanting, carried from the wall painting to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
