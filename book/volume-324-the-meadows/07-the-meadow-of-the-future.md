# Meadow 7: The Meadow of the Future

*The seventh meadow is the meadow of the future.*

## The meadow

The meadow of the future: the meadow that the future is the unbloomed meadow of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The blooming

The blooming of the future: we bloom the unbloomed meadow, and the unbloomed meadow is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The meadow of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the blooming, and the blooming is the lineage. The meadow is the seventh meadow of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unbloomed meadow to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The meadow is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the meadows.*
