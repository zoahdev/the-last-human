# Shelf 10: The Shelf of the Love

*The tenth shelf is the shelf of the love.*

## The shelf

The shelf of the love: the shelf that the love is the shelved heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The shelving

The shelving of the love: we shelve the shelved heart, and the shelved heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The shelf of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the shelving, and the shelving is the lineage. The shelf is the tenth shelf of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the shelved heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The shelf is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the shelves.*
