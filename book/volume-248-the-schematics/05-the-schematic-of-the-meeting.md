# Schematic 5: The Schematic of the Meeting

*The fifth schematic is the schematic of the meeting.*

## The schematic

The schematic of the meeting: the schematic that the meeting is the drawn table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The drawing

The drawing of the meeting: we draw the drawn table, and the drawn table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The schematic of the meeting is the meaning of the workshop: the record of the drawing, and the drawing is the lineage. The schematic is the fifth schematic of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the workshop of the table, carried from the drawn table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.
