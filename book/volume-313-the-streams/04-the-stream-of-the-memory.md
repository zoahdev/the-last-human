# Stream 4: The Stream of the Memory

*The fourth stream is the stream of the memory.*

## The stream

The stream of the memory: the stream that the memory is the streamed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The streaming

The streaming of the memory: we stream the streamed past, and the streamed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stream of the memory is the meaning of the brook: the record of the streaming, and the streaming is the lineage. The stream is the fourth stream of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the brook of the book, carried from the streamed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The stream is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the streams.*
