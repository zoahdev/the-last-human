# Threshold 9: The Threshold of the End

*The ninth threshold is the threshold of the end.*

## The threshold

The threshold of the end: the threshold that the end is the final threshold of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The crossing

The crossing of the end: we cross the final threshold, and the final threshold is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The threshold of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the crossing, and the crossing is the lineage. The threshold is the ninth threshold of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final threshold to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
