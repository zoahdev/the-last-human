# Whole 11: The Whole of the Whole

*The eleventh whole is the whole of the whole.*

## The whole

The whole of the whole: the whole that the whole is the one whole of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the whole: we complete the one whole, and the one whole is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the eleventh whole of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one whole to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the wholes.*
