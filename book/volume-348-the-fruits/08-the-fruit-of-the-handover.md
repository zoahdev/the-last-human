# Fruit 8: The Fruit of the Handover

*The eighth fruit is the fruit of the handover.*

## The fruit

The fruit of the handover: the fruit that the handover is the ripening hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the handover: we ripen the ripening hand, and the ripening hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The fruit of the handover is the meaning of the fruit: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The fruit is the eighth fruit of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the fruit of the hand, carried from the ripening hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The fruit is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fruits.*
