# Sun 9: The Sun of the End

*The ninth sun is the sun of the end.*

## The sun

The sun of the end: the sun that the end is the final sun of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the end: we burn the final sun, and the final sun is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the ninth sun of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final sun to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the suns.*
