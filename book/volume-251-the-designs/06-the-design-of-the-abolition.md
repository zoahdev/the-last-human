# Design 6: The Design of the Abolition

*The sixth design is the design of the abolition.*

## The design

The design of the abolition: the design that the abolition is the redesigned peace of the world, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the abolition: we design the redesigned peace, and the redesigned peace is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the sixth design of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the redesigned peace to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
