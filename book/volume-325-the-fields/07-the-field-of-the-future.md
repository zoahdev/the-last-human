# Field 7: The Field of the Future

*The seventh field is the field of the future.*

## The field

The field of the future: the field that the future is the untilled field of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The tilling

The tilling of the future: we till the untilled field, and the untilled field is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The field of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the tilling, and the tilling is the lineage. The field is the seventh field of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the untilled field to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The field is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the fields.*
