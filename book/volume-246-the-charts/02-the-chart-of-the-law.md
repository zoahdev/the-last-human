# Chart 2: The Chart of the Law

*The second chart is the chart of the law.*

## The chart

The chart of the law: the chart that the law is the charted care, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the law: we chart the charted care, and the charted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the law is the meaning of the navigation: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the second chart of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the navigation of the care, carried from the charted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
