# Doorway 2: The Doorway of the Law

*The second doorway is the doorway of the law.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the law: the doorway that the law is the gate of the care, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the law: we open the gate, and the gate is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the law is the meaning of the portal: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the second doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the portal of the care, carried from the gate to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the doorways.*
