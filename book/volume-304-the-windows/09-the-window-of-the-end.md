# Window 9: The Window of the End

*The ninth window is the window of the end.*

## The window

The window of the end: the window that the end is the final window of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the end: we open the final window, and the final window is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The window of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The window is the ninth window of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final window to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The window is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the windows.*
