# Doorway 4: The Doorway of the Memory

*The fourth doorway is the doorway of the memory.*

## The doorway

The doorway of the memory: the doorway that the memory is the remembered doorway of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The opening

The opening of the memory: we open the remembered doorway, and the remembered doorway is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The doorway of the memory is the meaning of the doorway: the record of the opening, and the opening is the lineage. The doorway is the fourth doorway of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the doorway of the book, carried from the remembered doorway to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The doorway is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the doorways.*
