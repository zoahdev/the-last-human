# Sun 2: The Sun of the Law

*The second sun is the sun of the law.*

## The sun

The sun of the law: the sun that the law is the burning care, kept by the lineage.

## The burning

The burning of the law: we burn the burning care, and the burning care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The sun of the law is the meaning of the light: the record of the burning, and the burning is the lineage. The sun is the second sun of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the light of the care, carried from the burning care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The sun is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the suns.*
