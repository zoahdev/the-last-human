# Infinity 5: The Infinity of the Meeting

*The fifth infinity is the infinity of the meeting.*

## The infinity

The infinity of the meeting: the infinity that the meeting is the endless table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The extending

The extending of the meeting: we extend the endless table, and the endless table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The infinity of the meeting is the meaning of the gathering: the record of the extending, and the extending is the lineage. The infinity is the fifth infinity of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the gathering of the table, carried from the endless table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.
