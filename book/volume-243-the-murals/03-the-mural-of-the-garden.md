# Mural 3: The Mural of the Garden

*The third mural is the mural of the garden.*

## The mural

The mural of the garden: the mural that the garden is the painted park of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The painting

The painting of the garden: we paint the painted park, and the painted park is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mural of the garden is the meaning of the garden: the record of the painting, and the painting is the lineage. The mural is the third mural of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the garden of the earth, carried from the painted park to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The mural is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the murals.*
