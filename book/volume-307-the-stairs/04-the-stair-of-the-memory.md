# Stair 4: The Stair of the Memory

*The fourth stair is the stair of the memory.*

## The stair

The stair of the memory: the stair that the memory is the winding past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the memory: we wind the winding past, and the winding past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The stair of the memory is the meaning of the staircase: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The stair is the fourth stair of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the staircase of the book, carried from the winding past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The stair is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the stairs.*
