# Feast 12: The Feast of the Completion

*The twelfth feast is the feast of the completion.*

## The feast

The feast of the completion: the feast that the completion is the complete feast of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the completion: we feast the complete feast, and the complete feast is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the twelfth feast of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete feast to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts. It is the final feast of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
