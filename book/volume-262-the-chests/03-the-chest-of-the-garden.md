# Chest 3: The Chest of the Garden

*The third chest is the chest of the garden.*

## The chest

The chest of the garden: the chest that the garden is the stored green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The storing

The storing of the garden: we store the stored green, and the stored green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chest of the garden is the meaning of the seed chest: the record of the storing, and the storing is the lineage. The chest is the third chest of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the seed chest of the earth, carried from the stored green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The chest is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the chests.*
