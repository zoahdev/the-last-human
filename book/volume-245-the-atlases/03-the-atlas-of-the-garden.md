# Atlas 3: The Atlas of the Garden

*The third atlas is the atlas of the garden.*

## The atlas

The atlas of the garden: the atlas that the garden is the carried green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The carrying

The carrying of the garden: we carry the carried green, and the carried green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The atlas of the garden is the meaning of the globe: the record of the carrying, and the carrying is the lineage. The atlas is the third atlas of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the globe of the earth, carried from the carried green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The atlas is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the atlases.*
