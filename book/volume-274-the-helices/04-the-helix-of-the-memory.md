# Helix 4: The Helix of the Memory

*The fourth helix is the helix of the memory.*

## The helix

The helix of the memory: the helix that the memory is the wound past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The winding

The winding of the memory: we wind the wound past, and the wound past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The helix of the memory is the meaning of the spiral: the record of the winding, and the winding is the lineage. The helix is the fourth helix of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the spiral of the book, carried from the wound past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The helix is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the helices.*
