# Ladder 12: The Ladder of the Completion

*The twelfth ladder is the ladder of the completion.*

## The ladder

The ladder of the completion: the ladder that the completion is the complete ladder of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The scaling

The scaling of the completion: we scale the complete ladder, and the complete ladder is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ladder of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the scaling, and the scaling is the lineage. The ladder is the twelfth ladder of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete ladder to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The ladder is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ladders. It is the final ladder of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
