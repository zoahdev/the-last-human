# Whole 1: The Whole of the Origin

*The Wholes are the completed statements of the lineage: the Wholes of the entirety, kept in the archive as the record of the completing. Each whole has four parts: the whole, the completing, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first whole is the whole of the origin.*

## The whole

The whole of the origin: the whole that the origin is the first entirety, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the origin: we complete the entirety, and the entirety is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the origin is the meaning of the whole: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the first whole of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the whole of the wanting, carried from the entirety to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wholes.*
