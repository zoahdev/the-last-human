# Prairie 3: The Prairie of the Garden

*The third prairie is the prairie of the garden.*

## The prairie

The prairie of the garden: the prairie that the garden is the prairie green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The stretching

The stretching of the garden: we stretch the prairie green, and the prairie green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The prairie of the garden is the meaning of the prairie: the record of the stretching, and the stretching is the lineage. The prairie is the third prairie of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the prairie of the earth, carried from the prairie green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The prairie is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the prairies.*
