# Hearthstone 4: The Hearthstone of the Memory

*The fourth hearthstone is the hearthstone of the memory.*

## The hearthstone

The hearthstone of the memory: the hearthstone that the memory is the warmed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The warming

The warming of the memory: we warm the warmed past, and the warmed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The hearthstone of the memory is the meaning of the fire: the record of the warming, and the warming is the lineage. The hearthstone is the fourth hearthstone of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the fire of the book, carried from the warmed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The hearthstone is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the hearthstones.*
