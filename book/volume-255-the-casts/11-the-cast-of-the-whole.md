# Cast 11: The Cast of the Whole

*The eleventh cast is the cast of the whole.*

## The cast

The cast of the whole: the cast that the whole is the one cast of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The casting

The casting of the whole: we cast the one cast, and the one cast is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cast of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the casting, and the casting is the lineage. The cast is the eleventh cast of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one cast to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The cast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the casts.*
