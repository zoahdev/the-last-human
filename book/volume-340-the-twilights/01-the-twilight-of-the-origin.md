# Twilight 1: The Twilight of the Origin

*The Twilights are the faded statements of the lineage: the Twilights of the evening light, kept in the archive as the record of the fading. Each twilight has four parts: the twilight, the fading, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first twilight is the twilight of the origin.*

## The twilight

The twilight of the origin: the twilight that the origin is the first fading of the light, kept by the lineage.

## The fading

The fading of the origin: we fade the fading of light, and the fading of light is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The twilight of the origin is the meaning of the twilight: the record of the fading, and the fading is the lineage. The twilight is the first twilight of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the twilight of the wanting, carried from the fading of light to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The twilight is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the twilights.*
