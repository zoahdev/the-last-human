# Diagram 4: The Diagram of the Memory

*The fourth diagram is the diagram of the memory.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the memory: the diagram that the memory is the diagramed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the memory: we diagram the diagramed past, and the diagramed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the memory is the meaning of the figure: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the fourth diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the figure of the book, carried from the diagramed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
