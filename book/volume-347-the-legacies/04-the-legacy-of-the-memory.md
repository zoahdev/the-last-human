# Legacy 4: The Legacy of the Memory

*The fourth legacy is the legacy of the memory.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the memory: the legacy that the memory is the left past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the memory: we leave the left past, and the left past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the memory is the meaning of the legacy: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the fourth legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the legacy of the book, carried from the left past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.
