# Welcome 12: The Welcome of the Completion

*The twelfth welcome is the welcome of the completion.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the completion: the welcome that the completion is the complete welcome of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the completion: we welcome the complete welcome, and the complete welcome is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the twelfth welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete welcome to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the welcomes. It is the final welcome of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
