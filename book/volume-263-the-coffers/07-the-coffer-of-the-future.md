# Coffer 7: The Coffer of the Future

*The seventh coffer is the coffer of the future.*

## The coffer

The coffer of the future: the coffer that the future is the unopened coffer of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The locking

The locking of the future: we lock the unopened coffer, and the unopened coffer is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The coffer of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the locking, and the locking is the lineage. The coffer is the seventh coffer of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unopened coffer to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The coffer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the coffers.*
