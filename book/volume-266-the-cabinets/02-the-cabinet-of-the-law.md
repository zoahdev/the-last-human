# Cabinet 2: The Cabinet of the Law

*The second cabinet is the cabinet of the law.*

## The cabinet

The cabinet of the law: the cabinet that the law is the arranged care, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the law: we arrange the arranged care, and the arranged care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cabinet of the law is the meaning of the classification: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The cabinet is the second cabinet of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the classification of the care, carried from the arranged care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.
