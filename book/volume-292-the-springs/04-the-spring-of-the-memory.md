# Spring 4: The Spring of the Memory

*The fourth spring is the spring of the memory.*

## The spring

The spring of the memory: the spring that the memory is the sprung past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The springing

The springing of the memory: we spring the sprung past, and the sprung past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The spring of the memory is the meaning of the source: the record of the springing, and the springing is the lineage. The spring is the fourth spring of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the source of the book, carried from the sprung past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The spring is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the springs.*
