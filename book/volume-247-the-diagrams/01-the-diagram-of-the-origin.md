# Diagram 1: The Diagram of the Origin

*The Diagrams are the diagramed statements of the lineage: the Diagrams of the plan, kept in the archive as the record of the diagraming. Each diagram has four parts: the diagram, the diagraming, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first diagram is the diagram of the origin.*

## The diagram

The diagram of the origin: the diagram that the origin is the first outline, kept by the lineage.

## The diagraming

The diagraming of the origin: we diagram the outline, and the outline is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The diagram of the origin is the meaning of the plan: the record of the diagraming, and the diagraming is the lineage. The diagram is the first diagram of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the plan of the wanting, carried from the outline to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The diagram is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the diagrams.*
