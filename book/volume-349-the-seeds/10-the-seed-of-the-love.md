# Seed 10: The Seed of the Love

*The tenth seed is the seed of the love.*

## The seed

The seed of the love: the seed that the love is the sowing heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sowing

The sowing of the love: we sow the sowing heart, and the sowing heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The seed of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the sowing, and the sowing is the lineage. The seed is the tenth seed of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the sowing heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The seed is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the seeds.*
