# One 4: The One of the Memory

*The fourth one is the one of the memory.*

## The one

The one of the memory: the one that the memory is the united past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the memory: we unite the united past, and the united past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the memory is the meaning of the one: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the fourth one of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the one of the book, carried from the united past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ones.*
