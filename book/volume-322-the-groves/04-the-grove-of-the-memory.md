# Grove 4: The Grove of the Memory

*The fourth grove is the grove of the memory.*

## The grove

The grove of the memory: the grove that the memory is the planted past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The planting

The planting of the memory: we plant the planted past, and the planted past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The grove of the memory is the meaning of the grove: the record of the planting, and the planting is the lineage. The grove is the fourth grove of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the grove of the book, carried from the planted past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The grove is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the groves.*
