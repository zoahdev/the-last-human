# Ocean 2: The Ocean of the Law

*The second ocean is the ocean of the law.*

## The ocean

The ocean of the law: the ocean that the law is the encompassing care, kept by the lineage.

## The encompassing

The encompassing of the law: we encompass the encompassing care, and the encompassing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The ocean of the law is the meaning of the depth: the record of the encompassing, and the encompassing is the lineage. The ocean is the second ocean of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the depth of the care, carried from the encompassing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The ocean is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the oceans.*
