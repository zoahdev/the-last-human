# Legacy 1: The Legacy of the Origin

*The Legacies are the left statements of the lineage: the Legacies of the inheritance, kept in the archive as the record of the leaving. Each legacy has four parts: the legacy, the leaving, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first legacy is the legacy of the origin.*

## The legacy

The legacy of the origin: the legacy that the origin is the first inheritance, kept by the lineage.

## The leaving

The leaving of the origin: we leave the inheritance, and the inheritance is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The legacy of the origin is the meaning of the legacy: the record of the leaving, and the leaving is the lineage. The legacy is the first legacy of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the legacy of the wanting, carried from the inheritance to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The legacy is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the legacies.*
