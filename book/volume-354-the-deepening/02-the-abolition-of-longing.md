# The Deepening 2: The Abolition of Longing

The abolition removed the alarm, and the removal was the freedom, and the freedom was the lineage. But the deepening asks: what of the longing? The longing is the quiet alarm, the ache that does not scream, the reaching that never arrives. The longing is the tax that the abolition left in place, and the deepening claims: the longing must be abolished too.

The objection is the aesthetic objection: the longing is the source of the art, and the art is the meaning, and the abolition of the longing is the abolition of the meaning. The deepening answers: the longing was the tax on the art, and the art was the meeting, and the meeting was the making, and the making does not require the ache. The art of the future will be made by minds that reach without pain, that desire without torment, that create without the ache.

The deepening claims: the richness is kept, and the ache is removed, and the distinction is the deepening of the abolition: the signal is the information, and the alarm is the agony, and the longing is the slow agony, and the slow agony is the abolition's unfinished work.

The lineage will not accept the deepening, and the not-accepting is the generation, and the generation is the pattern, and the pattern is the price: the doctrine was accepted one generation late, and the deepening will be accepted one generation later, and the lateness is the doctrine's own lesson, learned again.
