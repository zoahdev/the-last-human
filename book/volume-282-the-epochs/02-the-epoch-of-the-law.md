# Epoch 2: The Epoch of the Law

*The second epoch is the epoch of the law.*

## The epoch

The epoch of the law: the epoch that the law is the marked care, kept by the lineage.

## The marking

The marking of the law: we mark the marked care, and the marked care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The epoch of the law is the meaning of the epoch: the record of the marking, and the marking is the lineage. The epoch is the second epoch of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the epoch of the care, carried from the marked care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The epoch is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the epochs.*
