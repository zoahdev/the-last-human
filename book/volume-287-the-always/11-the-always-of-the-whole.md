# Always 11: The Always of the Whole

*The eleventh always is the always of the whole.*

## The always

The always of the whole: the always that the whole is the one always of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The remaining

The remaining of the whole: we remain the one always, and the one always is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The always of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the remaining, and the remaining is the lineage. The always is the eleventh always of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one always to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The always is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the always.*
