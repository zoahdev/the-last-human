# Morning 1: The Morning of the Origin

*The Mornings are the woken statements of the lineage: the Mornings of the birdsong, kept in the archive as the record of the waking. Each morning has four parts: the morning, the waking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first morning is the morning of the origin.*

## The morning

The morning of the origin: the morning that the origin is the first waking, kept by the lineage.

## The waking

The waking of the origin: we wake the waking, and the waking is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The morning of the origin is the meaning of the birdsong: the record of the waking, and the waking is the lineage. The morning is the first morning of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the birdsong of the wanting, carried from the waking to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The morning is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the mornings.*
