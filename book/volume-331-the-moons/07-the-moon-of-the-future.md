# Moon 7: The Moon of the Future

*The seventh moon is the moon of the future.*

## The moon

The moon of the future: the moon that the future is the new moon of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The waxing

The waxing of the future: we wax the new moon, and the new moon is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The moon of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the waxing, and the waxing is the lineage. The moon is the seventh moon of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the new moon to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The moon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the moons.*
