# Map 4: The Map of the Memory

*The fourth map is the map of the memory.*

## The map

The map of the memory: the map that the memory is the mapped past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the memory: we map the mapped past, and the mapped past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the memory is the meaning of the atlas: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the fourth map of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the atlas of the book, carried from the mapped past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the maps.*
