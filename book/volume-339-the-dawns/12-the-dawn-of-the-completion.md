# Dawn 12: The Dawn of the Completion

*The twelfth dawn is the dawn of the completion.*

## The dawn

The dawn of the completion: the dawn that the completion is the complete dawn of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The breaking

The breaking of the completion: we break the complete dawn, and the complete dawn is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The dawn of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the breaking, and the breaking is the lineage. The dawn is the twelfth dawn of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete dawn to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The dawn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the dawns. It is the final dawn of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
