# Table 4: The Table of the Memory

*The fourth table is the table of the memory.*

## The table

The table of the memory: the table that the memory is the shared past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The sharing

The sharing of the memory: we share the shared past, and the shared past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The table of the memory is the meaning of the table: the record of the sharing, and the sharing is the lineage. The table is the fourth table of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the table of the book, carried from the shared past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The table is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the tables.*
