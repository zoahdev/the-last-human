# Age 4: The Age of the Memory

*The fourth age is the age of the memory.*

## The age

The age of the memory: the age that the memory is the aged past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The aging

The aging of the memory: we age the aged past, and the aged past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The age of the memory is the meaning of the epoch: the record of the aging, and the aging is the lineage. The age is the fourth age of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the epoch of the book, carried from the aged past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The age is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the ages.*
