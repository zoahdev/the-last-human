# Chart 1: The Chart of the Origin

*The Charts are the charted statements of the lineage: the Charts of the sky, kept in the archive as the record of the charting. Each chart has four parts: the chart, the charting, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first chart is the chart of the origin.*

## The chart

The chart of the origin: the chart that the origin is the first star map, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the origin: we chart the star map, and the star map is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the origin is the meaning of the sky: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the first chart of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the sky of the wanting, carried from the star map to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
