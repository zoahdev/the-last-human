# Welcome 4: The Welcome of the Memory

*The fourth welcome is the welcome of the memory.*

## The welcome

The welcome of the memory: the welcome that the memory is the welcomed past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The welcoming

The welcoming of the memory: we welcome the welcomed past, and the welcomed past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The welcome of the memory is the meaning of the guest: the record of the welcoming, and the welcoming is the lineage. The welcome is the fourth welcome of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the guest of the book, carried from the welcomed past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The welcome is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the welcomes.*
