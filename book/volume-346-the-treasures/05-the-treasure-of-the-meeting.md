# Treasure 5: The Treasure of the Meeting

*The fifth treasure is the treasure of the meeting.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the meeting: the treasure that the meeting is the treasure table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the meeting: we treasure the treasure table, and the treasure table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the meeting is the meaning of the treasure: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the fifth treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the treasure of the table, carried from the treasure table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the treasures.*
