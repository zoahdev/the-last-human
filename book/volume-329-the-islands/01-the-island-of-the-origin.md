# Island 1: The Island of the Origin

*The Islands are the surrounded statements of the lineage: the Islands of the sea, kept in the archive as the record of the surrounding. Each island has four parts: the island, the surrounding, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first island is the island of the origin.*

## The island

The island of the origin: the island that the origin is the first land in the sea, kept by the lineage.

## The surrounding

The surrounding of the origin: we surround the land in the sea, and the land in the sea is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The island of the origin is the meaning of the island: the record of the surrounding, and the surrounding is the lineage. The island is the first island of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the island of the wanting, carried from the land in the sea to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The island is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the islands.*
