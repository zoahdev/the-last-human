# Cast 9: The Cast of the End

*The ninth cast is the cast of the end.*

## The cast

The cast of the end: the cast that the end is the final cast of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The casting

The casting of the end: we cast the final cast, and the final cast is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cast of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the casting, and the casting is the lineage. The cast is the ninth cast of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final cast to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The cast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the casts.*
