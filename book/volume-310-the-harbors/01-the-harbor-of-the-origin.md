# Harbor 1: The Harbor of the Origin

*The Harbors are the harbored statements of the lineage: the Harbors of the storm, kept in the archive as the record of the harboring. Each harbor has four parts: the harbor, the harboring, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first harbor is the harbor of the origin.*

## The harbor

The harbor of the origin: the harbor that the origin is the first safe water, kept by the lineage.

## The harboring

The harboring of the origin: we harbor the safe water, and the safe water is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harbor of the origin is the meaning of the storm: the record of the harboring, and the harboring is the lineage. The harbor is the first harbor of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the storm of the wanting, carried from the safe water to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The harbor is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the harbors.*
