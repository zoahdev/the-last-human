# Plan 10: The Plan of the Love

*The tenth plan is the plan of the love.*

## The plan

The plan of the love: the plan that the love is the planned heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the love: we plan the planned heart, and the planned heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the tenth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the planned heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the plans.*
