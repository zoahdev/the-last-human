# Bridge 12: The Bridge of the Completion

*The twelfth bridge is the bridge of the completion.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the completion: the bridge that the completion is the complete bridge of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the completion: we bridge the complete bridge, and the complete bridge is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the twelfth bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete bridge to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the bridges. It is the final bridge of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
