# Pattern 11: The Pattern of the Whole

*The eleventh pattern is the pattern of the whole.*

## The pattern

The pattern of the whole: the pattern that the whole is the one pattern of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The patterning

The patterning of the whole: we pattern the one pattern, and the one pattern is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The pattern of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the patterning, and the patterning is the lineage. The pattern is the eleventh pattern of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one pattern to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The pattern is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the patterns.*
