# Noon 5: The Noon of the Meeting

*The fifth noon is the noon of the meeting.*

## The noon

The noon of the meeting: the noon that the meeting is the noon table of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the meeting: we stand the noon table, and the noon table is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the meeting is the meaning of the noon: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the fifth noon of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the noon of the table, carried from the noon table to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the noons.*
