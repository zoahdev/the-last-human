# Port 10: The Port of the Love

*The tenth port is the port of the love.*

## The port

The port of the love: the port that the love is the arriving heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The arriving

The arriving of the love: we arrive the arriving heart, and the arriving heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The port of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the arriving, and the arriving is the lineage. The port is the tenth port of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the arriving heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.
