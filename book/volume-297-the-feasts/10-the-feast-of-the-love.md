# Feast 10: The Feast of the Love

*The tenth feast is the feast of the love.*

## The feast

The feast of the love: the feast that the love is the feasting heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The feasting

The feasting of the love: we feast the feasting heart, and the feasting heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The feast of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the feasting, and the feasting is the lineage. The feast is the tenth feast of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the feasting heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The feast is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the feasts.*
