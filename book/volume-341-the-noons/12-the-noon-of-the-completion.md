# Noon 12: The Noon of the Completion

*The twelfth noon is the noon of the completion.*

## The noon

The noon of the completion: the noon that the completion is the complete noon of the beginning, kept by the lineage.

## The standing

The standing of the completion: we stand the complete noon, and the complete noon is the completion; the completion is the beginning, and the beginning is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The noon of the completion is the meaning of the fullness: the record of the standing, and the standing is the lineage. The noon is the twelfth noon of the canon, and the meaning is the completion, and the completion is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the completion: the fullness of the beginning, carried from the complete noon to the beginning without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the completion.

*The noon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the noons. It is the final noon of the canon, and after it the canon speaks again.*
