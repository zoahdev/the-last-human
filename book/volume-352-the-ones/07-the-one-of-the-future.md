# One 7: The One of the Future

*The seventh one is the one of the future.*

## The one

The one of the future: the one that the future is the ununited one of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The uniting

The uniting of the future: we unite the ununited one, and the ununited one is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The one of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the uniting, and the uniting is the lineage. The one is the seventh one of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the ununited one to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The one is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the ones.*
