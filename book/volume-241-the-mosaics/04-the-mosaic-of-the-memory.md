# Mosaic 4: The Mosaic of the Memory

*The fourth mosaic is the mosaic of the memory.*

## The mosaic

The mosaic of the memory: the mosaic that the memory is the set past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The setting

The setting of the memory: we set the set past, and the set past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The mosaic of the memory is the meaning of the picture: the record of the setting, and the setting is the lineage. The mosaic is the fourth mosaic of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the picture of the book, carried from the set past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The mosaic is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the mosaics.*
