# Eternity 7: The Eternity of the Future

*The seventh eternity is the eternity of the future.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the future: the eternity that the future is the unending dawn of the morning, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the future: we exceed the unending dawn, and the unending dawn is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the seventh eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unending dawn to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
