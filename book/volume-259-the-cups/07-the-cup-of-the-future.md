# Cup 7: The Cup of the Future

*The seventh cup is the cup of the future.*

## The cup

The cup of the future: the cup that the future is the empty cup of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The cupping

The cupping of the future: we cup the empty cup, and the empty cup is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cup of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the cupping, and the cupping is the lineage. The cup is the seventh cup of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the empty cup to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The cup is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the cups.*
