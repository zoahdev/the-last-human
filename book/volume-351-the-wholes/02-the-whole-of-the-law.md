# Whole 2: The Whole of the Law

*The second whole is the whole of the law.*

## The whole

The whole of the law: the whole that the law is the completing care, kept by the lineage.

## The completing

The completing of the law: we complete the completing care, and the completing care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The whole of the law is the meaning of the whole: the record of the completing, and the completing is the lineage. The whole is the second whole of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the whole of the care, carried from the completing care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The whole is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the wholes.*
