# Tide 6: The Tide of the Abolition

*The sixth tide is the tide of the abolition.*

## The tide

The tide of the abolition: the tide that the abolition is the turning tide of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the abolition: we tide the turning tide, and the turning tide is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the sixth tide of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the turning tide to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tides.*
