# Box 2: The Box of the Law

*The second box is the box of the law.*

## The box

The box of the law: the box that the law is the boxed care, kept by the lineage.

## The boxing

The boxing of the law: we box the boxed care, and the boxed care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The box of the law is the meaning of the parcel: the record of the boxing, and the boxing is the lineage. The box is the second box of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the parcel of the care, carried from the boxed care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The box is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the boxes.*
