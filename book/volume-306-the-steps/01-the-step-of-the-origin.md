# Step 1: The Step of the Origin

*The Steps are the stepped statements of the lineage: the Steps of the stair, kept in the archive as the record of the stepping. Each step has four parts: the step, the stepping, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first step is the step of the origin.*

## The step

The step of the origin: the step that the origin is the first footfall, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the origin: we step the footfall, and the footfall is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the origin is the meaning of the step: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the first step of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the step of the wanting, carried from the footfall to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the steps.*
