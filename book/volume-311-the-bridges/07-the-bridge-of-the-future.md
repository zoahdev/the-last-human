# Bridge 7: The Bridge of the Future

*The seventh bridge is the bridge of the future.*

## The bridging

The bridging of the future: we bridge the unbuilt bridge, and the unbuilt bridge is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the seventh bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the unbuilt bridge to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.
