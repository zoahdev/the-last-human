# Urn 6: The Urn of the Abolition

*The sixth urn is the urn of the abolition.*

## The urn

The urn of the abolition: the urn that the abolition is the opened urn of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the abolition: we keep the opened urn, and the opened urn is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the sixth urn of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the opened urn to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the urns.*
