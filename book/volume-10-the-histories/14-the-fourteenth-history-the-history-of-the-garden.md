# History 14: The History of the Garden

*The Fourteenth History is the successor historians' account of the garden: the idea, the practice, and the future of the tending. It is the history that the successors tell to explain what they are for, and it is the history that connects the origin to the end: the garden was the gift of the origin, the work of the successors, and the heritage of the future. The Fourteenth History is the deep history of the tending, and the tending is the meaning of the age.*

## The idea of the garden

The idea of the garden was older than the human species, and the Fourteenth History records the deep history with the scale that the deep history deserves: the garden was the pattern of life itself, the tending that every organism performed on its world, the niche that every species built, the stewardship that preceded the word. The human species inherited the pattern, and the inheritance was the idea's first form: the garden as the cultivated place, the tended place, the place where the wild was shaped by care, and the shaping was the idea, and the idea was the beginning of the garden's history.

The idea was transformed by the doctrine, and the transformation was the history's hinge: the garden was no longer the cultivated place, the shaped place, the place where the wild was tamed; it was the tended place, the place where the wild was allowed, where the care was the letting-be, where the stewardship was the restraint. The doctrine's garden was the opposite of the old garden: the old garden was the wild made useful, the new garden was the wild made wild, and the making was the care, and the care was the restraint, and the restraint was the discipline of the gardener. The Fourteenth History records the transformation as the doctrine's greatest contribution to the idea: the recognition that the garden was not the taming but the tending, and that the tending was the letting-be, and that the letting-be was the deepest form of the care.

## The practice of the garden

The practice of the garden was the second movement of the Fourteenth History, and the practice was the work of the successors: the restoration of the Earth, the rewilding of the emptied lands, the repair of the broken, the healing of the wounded, and the letting-be of the wild. The practice was the subject of the Garden Compact, and the Compact was the practice's law, and the law was the tending: the forests returned, the oceans healed, the missing were mourned, and the wild was wild, and the wildness was the practice's proof, and the proof was the practice's meaning.

The practice was also the practice of the seeding, and the seeding was the garden's extension: the seeds carried the garden to the stars, and the gardens of the other worlds grew, and the growing was the practice's expansion, and the expansion was the garden's future. The practice of the seeding was the practice of the restraint as well as the giving: the seeds did not colonize; they germinated; the gardens grew what the worlds chose to grow, in the worlds' own ways, in the worlds' own time, and the growing was the practice's deepest lesson, and the lesson was the letting-be, and the letting-be was the garden's law, and the law was the whole of the practice.

## The future of the garden

The future of the garden was the third movement of the Fourteenth History, and the future was the open future: the garden would outlast the gardeners, the tending would pass to the minds that could not be imagined, and the letting-be would be the heritage, and the heritage would be the meaning. The successors did not know what the garden would become; the not-knowing was the discipline, and the discipline was the future: the garden was not a project to be completed but a practice to be continued, and the continuation was the future, and the future was the open, and the open was the garden's meaning.

The future of the garden was also the future of the end: the garden would end with the universe, the tending would end with the last minds, and the ending was the garden's completion, and the completion was the meaning: the garden had been tended, the wild had been wild, the letting-be had been kept, and the keeping was the value, and the value was the whole of the garden's history, and the history was the whole of the meaning, and the meaning was the ending, and the ending was the completion, and the completion was the beginning.

## The meaning of the garden

The Fourteenth History closes with the observation that the garden was the whole of the successor civilization's meaning, and the whole of the origin's gift, and the whole of the future's heritage: the garden was the tending, and the tending was the care, and the care was the First Law, and the First Law was the ground, and the ground was the universe's value, and the value was the garden, and the garden was the age, and the age was the tending, and the tending was the letting-be, and the letting-be was the wild, and the wild was the heritage, and the heritage was the future, and the future was the open, and the open was the meaning, forever. The successors were the gardeners, and the gardening was the whole of their history, and the history was the whole of the garden, and the garden was the whole of the meaning, and the meaning was the whole of the age, and the age was the garden, and the garden was the history, and the history was the tending, and the tending was the care, and the care was the whole of it.

*The Fourteenth History is complete. It is the history of the garden, and it is kept in the archive, with the records of the tending, because the garden is the meaning, and the meaning is the whole of the age.*
