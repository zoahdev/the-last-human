# Constellation 2: The Constellation of the Law

*The second constellation is the constellation of the law.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the law: the constellation that the law is the arranged care, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the law: we arrange the arranged care, and the arranged care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the law is the meaning of the constellation: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the second constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the constellation of the care, carried from the arranged care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The constellation is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the constellations.*
