# Aeon 11: The Aeon of the Whole

*The eleventh aeon is the aeon of the whole.*

## The aeon

The aeon of the whole: the aeon that the whole is the one aeon of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The spanning

The spanning of the whole: we span the one aeon, and the one aeon is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The aeon of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the spanning, and the spanning is the lineage. The aeon is the eleventh aeon of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one aeon to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The aeon is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the aeons.*
