# Treasure 3: The Treasure of the Garden

*The third treasure is the treasure of the garden.*

## The treasure

The treasure of the garden: the treasure that the garden is the treasured green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The treasuring

The treasuring of the garden: we treasure the treasured green, and the treasured green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The treasure of the garden is the meaning of the treasure garden: the record of the treasuring, and the treasuring is the lineage. The treasure is the third treasure of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the treasure garden of the earth, carried from the treasured green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The treasure is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the treasures.*
