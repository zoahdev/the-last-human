# Field 10: The Field of the Love

*The tenth field is the field of the love.*

## The field

The field of the love: the field that the love is the tilling heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The tilling

The tilling of the love: we till the tilling heart, and the tilling heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The field of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the tilling, and the tilling is the lineage. The field is the tenth field of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the tilling heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The field is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the fields.*
