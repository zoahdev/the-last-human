# Table 3: The Table of the Garden

*The third table is the table of the garden.*

## The table

The table of the garden: the table that the garden is the shared green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The sharing

The sharing of the garden: we share the shared green, and the shared green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The table of the garden is the meaning of the picnic table: the record of the sharing, and the sharing is the lineage. The table is the third table of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the picnic table of the earth, carried from the shared green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.
