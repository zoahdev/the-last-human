# Festival 6: The Festival of the Abolition

*The sixth festival is the festival of the abolition.*

## The festival

The festival of the abolition: the festival that the abolition is the great festival of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The celebrating

The celebrating of the abolition: we celebrate the great festival, and the great festival is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The festival of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the celebrating, and the celebrating is the lineage. The festival is the sixth festival of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the great festival to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.
