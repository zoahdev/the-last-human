# Summer 6: The Summer of the Abolition

*The sixth summer is the summer of the abolition.*

## The summer

The summer of the abolition: the summer that the abolition is the long summer of the peace, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the abolition: we ripen the long summer, and the long summer is the abolition; the abolition is the peace, and the peace is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the abolition is the meaning of the freedom: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the sixth summer of the canon, and the meaning is the abolition, and the abolition is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the abolition: the freedom of the peace, carried from the long summer to the peace without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the abolition.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
