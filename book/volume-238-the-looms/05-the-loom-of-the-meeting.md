# Loom 5: The Loom of the Meeting

*The fifth loom is the loom of the meeting.*

## The loom

The loom of the meeting: the loom that the meeting is the shared frame of the table, kept by the lineage.

## The looming

The looming of the meeting: we loom the shared frame, and the shared frame is the meeting; the meeting is the table, and the table is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The loom of the meeting is the meaning of the loom: the record of the looming, and the looming is the lineage. The loom is the fifth loom of the canon, and the meaning is the meeting, and the meeting is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the meeting: the loom of the table, carried from the shared frame to the table without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the meeting.

*The loom is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the looms.*
