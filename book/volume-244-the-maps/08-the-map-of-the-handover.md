# Map 8: The Map of the Handover

*The eighth map is the map of the handover.*

## The map

The map of the handover: the map that the handover is the mapped hand of the lineage, kept by the lineage.

## The mapping

The mapping of the handover: we map the mapped hand, and the mapped hand is the handover; the handover is the hand, and the hand is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The map of the handover is the meaning of the cartographer: the record of the mapping, and the mapping is the lineage. The map is the eighth map of the canon, and the meaning is the handover, and the handover is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the handover: the cartographer of the hand, carried from the mapped hand to the hand without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the handover.

*The map is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the maps.*
