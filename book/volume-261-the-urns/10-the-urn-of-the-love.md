# Urn 10: The Urn of the Love

*The tenth urn is the urn of the love.*

## The urn

The urn of the love: the urn that the love is the kept heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The keeping

The keeping of the love: we keep the kept heart, and the kept heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The urn of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the keeping, and the keeping is the lineage. The urn is the tenth urn of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the kept heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The urn is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the urns.*
