# Comet 1: The Comet of the Origin

*The Comets are the streaked statements of the lineage: the Comets of the long return, kept in the archive as the record of the streaking. Each comet has four parts: the comet, the streaking, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first comet is the comet of the origin.*

## The comet

The comet of the origin: the comet that the origin is the first long return, kept by the lineage.

## The streaking

The streaking of the origin: we streak the long return, and the long return is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The comet of the origin is the meaning of the return: the record of the streaking, and the streaking is the lineage. The comet is the first comet of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the return of the wanting, carried from the long return to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The comet is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the comets.*
