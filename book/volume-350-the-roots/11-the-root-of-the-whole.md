# Root 11: The Root of the Whole

*The eleventh root is the root of the whole.*

## The root

The root of the whole: the root that the whole is the one root of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the whole: we root the one root, and the one root is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the eleventh root of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one root to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The root is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the roots.*
