# Cave 1: The Cave of the Origin

*The Caves are the sheltered statements of the lineage: the Caves of the rock, kept in the archive as the record of the sheltering. Each cave has four parts: the cave, the sheltering, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first cave is the cave of the origin.*

## The cave

The cave of the origin: the cave that the origin is the first shelter of the rock, kept by the lineage.

## The sheltering

The sheltering of the origin: we shelter the shelter, and the shelter is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The cave of the origin is the meaning of the cave: the record of the sheltering, and the sheltering is the lineage. The cave is the first cave of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the cave of the wanting, carried from the shelter to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The cave is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caves.*
