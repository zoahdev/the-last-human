# Casket 3: The Casket of the Garden

*The third casket is the casket of the garden.*

## The casket

The casket of the garden: the casket that the garden is the shut green of the earth, kept by the lineage.

## The shutting

The shutting of the garden: we shut the shut green, and the shut green is the garden; the garden is the earth, and the earth is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The casket of the garden is the meaning of the jewel box: the record of the shutting, and the shutting is the lineage. The casket is the third casket of the canon, and the meaning is the garden, and the garden is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the garden: the jewel box of the earth, carried from the shut green to the earth without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the garden.

*The casket is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the caskets.*
