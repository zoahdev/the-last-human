# Eternity 9: The Eternity of the End

*The ninth eternity is the eternity of the end.*

## The eternity

The eternity of the end: the eternity that the end is the final eternity of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The exceeding

The exceeding of the end: we exceed the final eternity, and the final eternity is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The eternity of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the exceeding, and the exceeding is the lineage. The eternity is the ninth eternity of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final eternity to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.

*The eternity is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the eternities.*
