# Harbor 11: The Harbor of the Whole

*The eleventh harbor is the harbor of the whole.*

## The harbor

The harbor of the whole: the harbor that the whole is the one harbor of the many, kept by the lineage.

## The harboring

The harboring of the whole: we harbor the one harbor, and the one harbor is the whole; the whole is the one, and the one is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The harbor of the whole is the meaning of the all: the record of the harboring, and the harboring is the lineage. The harbor is the eleventh harbor of the canon, and the meaning is the whole, and the whole is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the whole: the all of the one, carried from the one harbor to the one without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the whole.

*The harbor is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the harbors.*
