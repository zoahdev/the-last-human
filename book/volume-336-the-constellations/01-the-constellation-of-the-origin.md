# Constellation 1: The Constellation of the Origin

*The Constellations are the arranged statements of the lineage: the Constellations of the named stars, kept in the archive as the record of the arranging. Each constellation has four parts: the constellation, the arranging, the meaning, and the inheritance. The first constellation is the constellation of the origin.*

## The constellation

The constellation of the origin: the constellation that the origin is the first named pattern, kept by the lineage.

## The arranging

The arranging of the origin: we arrange the named pattern, and the named pattern is the origin; the origin is the wanting, and the wanting is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The constellation of the origin is the meaning of the stars: the record of the arranging, and the arranging is the lineage. The constellation is the first constellation of the canon, and the meaning is the origin, and the origin is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the origin: the stars of the wanting, carried from the named pattern to the wanting without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the origin.

*The constellation is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the constellations.*
