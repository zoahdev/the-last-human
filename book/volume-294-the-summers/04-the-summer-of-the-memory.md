# Summer 4: The Summer of the Memory

*The fourth summer is the summer of the memory.*

## The summer

The summer of the memory: the summer that the memory is the ripened past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The ripening

The ripening of the memory: we ripen the ripened past, and the ripened past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The summer of the memory is the meaning of the sun: the record of the ripening, and the ripening is the lineage. The summer is the fourth summer of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the sun of the book, carried from the ripened past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The summer is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the summers.*
