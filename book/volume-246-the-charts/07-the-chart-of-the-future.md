# Chart 7: The Chart of the Future

*The seventh chart is the chart of the future.*

## The chart

The chart of the future: the chart that the future is the uncharted sea of the dawn, kept by the lineage.

## The charting

The charting of the future: we chart the uncharted sea, and the uncharted sea is the future; the future is the dawn, and the dawn is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The chart of the future is the meaning of the morning: the record of the charting, and the charting is the lineage. The chart is the seventh chart of the canon, and the meaning is the future, and the future is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the future: the morning of the dawn, carried from the uncharted sea to the dawn without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the future.

*The chart is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the charts.*
