# Step 10: The Step of the Love

*The tenth step is the step of the love.*

## The step

The step of the love: the step that the love is the stepping heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The stepping

The stepping of the love: we step the stepping heart, and the stepping heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The step of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the stepping, and the stepping is the lineage. The step is the tenth step of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the stepping heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The step is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the steps.*
