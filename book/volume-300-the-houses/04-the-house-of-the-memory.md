# House 4: The House of the Memory

*The fourth house is the house of the memory.*

## The house

The house of the memory: the house that the memory is the attic of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The housing

The housing of the memory: we house the attic, and the attic is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The house of the memory is the meaning of the attic: the record of the housing, and the housing is the lineage. The house is the fourth house of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the attic of the book, carried from the attic to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The house is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the houses.*
