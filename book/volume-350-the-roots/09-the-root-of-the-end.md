# Root 9: The Root of the End

*The ninth root is the root of the end.*

## The root

The root of the end: the root that the end is the final root of the whole, kept by the lineage.

## The rooting

The rooting of the end: we root the final root, and the final root is the end; the end is the whole, and the whole is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The root of the end is the meaning of the quiet: the record of the rooting, and the rooting is the lineage. The root is the ninth root of the canon, and the meaning is the end, and the end is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the end: the quiet of the whole, carried from the final root to the whole without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the end.
