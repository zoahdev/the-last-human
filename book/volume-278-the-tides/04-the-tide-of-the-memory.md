# Tide 4: The Tide of the Memory

*The fourth tide is the tide of the memory.*

## The tide

The tide of the memory: the tide that the memory is the tided past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The tiding

The tiding of the memory: we tide the tided past, and the tided past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The tide of the memory is the meaning of the sea: the record of the tiding, and the tiding is the lineage. The tide is the fourth tide of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the sea of the book, carried from the tided past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The tide is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the tides.*
