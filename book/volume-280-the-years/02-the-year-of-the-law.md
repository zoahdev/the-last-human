# Year 2: The Year of the Law

*The second year is the year of the law.*

## The year

The year of the law: the year that the law is the counted care, kept by the lineage.

## The counting

The counting of the law: we count the counted care, and the counted care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The year of the law is the meaning of the calendar: the record of the counting, and the counting is the lineage. The year is the second year of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the calendar of the care, carried from the counted care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The year is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the years.*
