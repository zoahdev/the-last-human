# Design 2: The Design of the Law

*The second design is the design of the law.*

## The design

The design of the law: the design that the law is the designed care, kept by the lineage.

## The designing

The designing of the law: we design the designed care, and the designed care is the law; the law is the care, and the care is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The design of the law is the meaning of the specification: the record of the designing, and the designing is the lineage. The design is the second design of the canon, and the meaning is the law, and the law is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the law: the specification of the care, carried from the designed care to the care without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the law.

*The design is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the designs.*
