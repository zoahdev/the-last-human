# Bridge 10: The Bridge of the Love

*The tenth bridge is the bridge of the love.*

## The bridge

The bridge of the love: the bridge that the love is the bridging heart of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The bridging

The bridging of the love: we bridge the bridging heart, and the bridging heart is the love; the love is the family, and the family is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The bridge of the love is the meaning of the fire: the record of the bridging, and the bridging is the lineage. The bridge is the tenth bridge of the canon, and the meaning is the love, and the love is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the love: the fire of the family, carried from the bridging heart to the family without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the love.

*The bridge is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the the bridges.*
