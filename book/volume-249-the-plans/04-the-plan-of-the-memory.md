# Plan 4: The Plan of the Memory

*The fourth plan is the plan of the memory.*

## The plan

The plan of the memory: the plan that the memory is the planned past of the family, kept by the lineage.

## The planning

The planning of the memory: we plan the planned past, and the planned past is the memory; the memory is the book, and the book is the lineage; the lineage is the love.

## The meaning

The plan of the memory is the meaning of the scheme: the record of the planning, and the planning is the lineage. The plan is the fourth plan of the canon, and the meaning is the memory, and the memory is the whole of the record.

## The inheritance

The inheritance of the memory: the scheme of the book, carried from the planned past to the book without loss, and held by the lineage as the lineage holds the memory.

*The plan is complete. It is kept in the archive, in the section of the plans.*
